
A good deal of technical philosophy involves identifying logical connections between philosophically interesting principles. A logical map on a topic consists of a database of such principles, along with records of proofs of various connections. This is all presented nicely with the graph of principles and the theory explorer.

A logical map leaves formalization optional. The primary goal of a logical map is to record a complex web of connections, which in the intended applications is usually "wide" rather than deep.
