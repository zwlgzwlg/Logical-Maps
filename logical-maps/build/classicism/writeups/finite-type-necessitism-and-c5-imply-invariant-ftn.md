# Finite-Type Necessitism ∧ □ND ⇒ Invariant Finite-Type Necessitism

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Kodsi, Finite-Type Necessitism (2026); produced by Daniel Kodsi, with assistance from GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI); recorded by ChatGPT, at Daniel Kodsi’s request.</p>

## Premises

- **Finite-Type Necessitism.** Every possibly realizable finite arithmetic hierarchy has a rigid realization at the same carrier type, with a full rigid represented power of its last stage.
- **□ND.** Necessarily, distinct things of any type are necessarily distinct.

## Conclusion

- **Invariant Finite-Type Necessitism.** The variant of Finite-Type Necessitism obtained by requiring invariance and inextensibility, rather than persistence and inextensibility, throughout the witnesses and represented powers.

## Proof

The map’s C5 preset is necessary all-type necessary distinctness, equivalent over C to necessary 5. Under it persistence implies invariance, so the rigidity predicates, their canonical representative carriers and their represented-power clauses coincide. Apply FTN. [Paper (PDF)](https://github.com/dkodsi/Logical-Maps/blob/classicism-next-batch-2026-10-30/logical-maps/topics/classicism/papers/finite-type-necessitism.pdf); [source](https://github.com/dkodsi/Logical-Maps/blob/classicism-next-batch-2026-10-30/logical-maps/topics/classicism/papers/finite-type-necessitism.tex).
