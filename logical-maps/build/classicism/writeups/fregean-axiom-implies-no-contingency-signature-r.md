# Fregean Axiom ⇒ No Contingency (signature Σ)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.fregean_axiom_implies_no_contingency_signature_r`; produced by Cian Dorr, suggestion of 22 September 2026; proof written out by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 22 September 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 22 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Fregean Axiom.** Materially equivalent propositions are identical.

## Conclusion

- **No Contingency (signature Σ).** Each closed sentence of the language of the fixed signature Sigma, if true, is necessary. A sentence schema, standing to No Pure Contingency as Possibility Maximalism (signature Σ) stands to Possibility Maximalism (pure).

## Proof

For each closed sentence $P$ of $\mathcal L(\Sigma)$, if $P$ is true it is materially equivalent to $\top$, hence identical to $\top$ by the Fregean Axiom. Thus $P\to\Box P$. Purity of $P$ plays no role.

## Notes

The recorded proof that the Fregean Axiom implies No Pure Contingency never uses purity; this record states the conclusion at full strength.
