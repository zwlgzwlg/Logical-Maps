# Fregean Axiom ⇒ Extensionality

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Classicism (2024), Lean `Classicism.Map.fregean_axiom_implies_extensionality_r`; produced by Andrew Bacon and Cian Dorr; recorded by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 17 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Fregean Axiom.** Materially equivalent propositions are identical.

## Conclusion

- **Extensionality.** At each relational type, coextensive relations are identical; include the nullary propositional case.

## Proof

The Fregean Axiom makes every true proposition identical to $\top$, hence necessary. Apply this to the sentence $\forall\bar z\, .\,X[\bar z]\leftrightarrow Y[\bar z]$ and then use the background Intensionality theorem at that relational type. Note 18 explicitly excludes the inference to Functionality at types ending in e.
