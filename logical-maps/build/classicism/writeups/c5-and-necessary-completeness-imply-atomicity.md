# □ND ∧ □Boolean Completeness ⇒ Atomicity

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Classicism (2024), Lean `Classicism.Map.c5_and_necessary_completeness_imply_atomicity`; produced by Andrew Bacon and Cian Dorr; recorded by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 17 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□ND.** Necessarily, distinct things of any type are necessarily distinct.
- **□Boolean Completeness.** Necessarily, every property of entities of a relational type has a greatest lower bound in that type.

## Conclusion

- **Atomicity.** Every non-bottom entity of each relational type has an atom below it.

## Proof

C5 remains available necessarily by 4. Necessitated instance proofs of Proposition 2.5 turn boxed Boolean Completeness into boxed Actuality. The other direction of Proposition 2.6 then gives Atomicity.
