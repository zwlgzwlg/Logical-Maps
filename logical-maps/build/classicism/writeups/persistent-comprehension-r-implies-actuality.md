# Persistent Comprehension ⇒ Actuality

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Classicism (2024), Lean `Classicism.Map.persistent_comprehension_r_implies_actuality`; produced by Andrew Bacon and Cian Dorr; recorded by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 17 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Persistent Comprehension.** Every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a persistent one.

## Conclusion

- **Actuality.** There is a true proposition that entails every true proposition.

## Proof

Choose a persistent predicate T coextensive with truth and use $\forall p\, .\,Tp\to p$. The proof of Proposition 2.9 uses persistence, not inextensibility, to show that this true proposition entails every truth.
