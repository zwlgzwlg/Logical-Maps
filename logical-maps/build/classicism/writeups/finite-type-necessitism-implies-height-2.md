# Finite-Type Necessitism ⇒ Finite-Type Necessitism: height 2

## Premises

- **Finite-Type Necessitism.** Every possibly realizable finite arithmetic hierarchy has a rigid realization at the same carrier type, with a full rigid represented power of its last stage.

## Conclusion

- **Finite-Type Necessitism: height 2.** The exact height-2 fragment of Finite-Type Necessitism, at every carrier type. A possible presentation of numbers, subset codes and codes for subsets of those codes has such a rigid realization, with a full rigid power of its last carrier.

## Proof

Take the height-2 instances of the full schema, retaining every carrier type.
