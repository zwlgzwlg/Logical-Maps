# No Contingency (signature Σ) ∧ Witnessed Possibility ⇒ ⊥

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Cian Dorr, suggestion of 22 September 2026; proof written out by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 22 September 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 22 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **No Contingency (signature Σ).** Each closed sentence of the language of the fixed signature Sigma, if true, is necessary. A sentence schema, standing to No Pure Contingency as Possibility Maximalism (signature Σ) stands to Possibility Maximalism (pure).
- **Witnessed Possibility.** For pure-formulas P with free variables among a finite tuple x, and distinct matching nonlogical constants c, a witness to P entails that P at those constants is possible.

## Conclusion

- **⊥.** These premises cannot all hold together.

## Proof

Let $c$ be a constant of $\Sigma$ of some relational type $\tau$. The pure formulas $x=\top_\tau$ and $x\ne\top_\tau$ have witnesses, so Witnessed Possibility gives $\Diamond(c=\top_\tau)$ and $\Diamond(c\ne\top_\tau)$. By excluded middle one of the closed $\Sigma$-sentences $c=\top_\tau$, $c\ne\top_\tau$ is true, and No Contingency (signature Σ) makes it necessary, contradicting the possibility of the other.

## Notes

Hence No Contingency (signature Σ) excludes Logical Necessity, Separated Structure, Possibly Witnessed Possibility and Maximalist Classicism for Σ, while it is consistent with No Pure Contingency and holds in every one-world model.
