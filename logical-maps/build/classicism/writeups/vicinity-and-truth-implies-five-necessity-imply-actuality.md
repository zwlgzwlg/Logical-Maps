# Vicinity ∧ Truths are 5-necessary ⇒ Actuality

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Christopher Sun (the 5-necessity form of Vicinity, 19 October 2026); consequence by GPT-6 Astra, prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 30 October 2026; recorded by OpenAI Codex, prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 30 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Vicinity.** There is a true proposition that entails the possibility of each true proposition.
- **Truths are 5-necessary.** Every truth is 5-necessary: necessary under every S5 operator.

## Conclusion

- **Actuality.** There is a true proposition that entails every true proposition.

## Proof

By Sun's recorded 5-necessity form of Vicinity, take a true $v$ with $\forall q\, .\,\Box_5q\to v\le q$. The other current premise gives $q\to\Box_5q$ for every proposition $q$. Hence $\forall q\, .\,q\to v\le q$, and the same true $v$ witnesses Actuality. No optional premise is necessitated.
