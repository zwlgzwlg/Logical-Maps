# Full present: continuum-closed elementary Henkin leaf

See [Subset-closed elementary Henkin leaves and full presents](elementary-henkin-presents.md), especially Sections 1–4 and 8, for the construction and proof. The retained verdicts are necessary Countable Boolean Completeness, necessary Strong Atomicity and SOAN, with failure of full Boolean Completeness. The same real-field witness supports each specified finite relation arity; no full finite-height necessity verdict is asserted for this construction.
