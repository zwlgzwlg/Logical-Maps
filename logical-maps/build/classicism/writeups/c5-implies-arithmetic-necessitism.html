C5 implies Arithmetic Necessitism

Proof by GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 23 October 2026; written out for the map by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 24 October 2026.

Theorem. In C, □ND (at every type) implies Arithmetic Necessitism at every type \sigma, including e:

\operatorname{Rigid}(\mathbb N_\sigma)\land\forall m\,n\in\mathbb N_\sigma\, .\,m\ne n\to\Box\,m\ne n .

The antecedent \Box I^*_\sigma of the principle is not needed. Write \mathbb N for \mathbb N_\sigma, \mathbf 0 for \mathbf 0_\sigma and \operatorname{Suc} for \operatorname{Suc}_\sigma (Background).

Facts used. ND at t implies 5 and hence B, \forall p\, .\,p\to\Box\Diamond p (Classicism, Proposition 2.2), and □ND implies BF at every type (Classicism, Proposition 2.3). Both conclusions are necessary, since □ND is.

Step 1: \mathbb N is persistent

\mathbb N\mathbf 0 is a theorem of C, so \Box\mathbb N\mathbf 0. And \forall n\, .\,\mathbb Nn\to\mathbb N(\operatorname{Suc}n) is a theorem of C, so \forall n\, .\,\Box\mathbb Nn\to\Box\mathbb N(\operatorname{Suc}n). So \lambda n\, .\,\Box\mathbb Nn holds of \mathbf 0 and is closed under successor, and since \mathbb N is the least such property (the definition of \operatorname{FiniteCardinality}),

\forall n\, .\,\mathbb Nn\to\Box\mathbb Nn .

This is a theorem of C, so it is necessary: \mathbb N is persistent.

Step 2: \mathbb N is inextensible

By B and persistence, a non-number is necessarily a non-number: \neg\mathbb Nn\to\Box\neg\mathbb Nn. For if \Diamond\mathbb Nn then \Diamond\Box\mathbb Nn, and B gives \mathbb Nn.

Now suppose \forall n\, .\,\mathbb Nn\to\Box Xn. Then

\forall n\, .\,\Box(\mathbb Nn\to Xn),

from \Box Xn if \mathbb Nn, and from \Box\neg\mathbb Nn otherwise. BF gives \Box\forall n\, .\,\mathbb Nn\to Xn, that is, \mathbb N\le X. So \mathbb N is weakly inextensible. The argument uses only B, BF and Step 1, which hold necessarily, so it can be carried out at every world: \mathbb N is inextensible, and with Step 1, rigid.

Step 3: necessary distinctness

ND at the type (\sigma t)t of cardinalities gives m\ne n\to\Box\,m\ne n for all m,n, in particular for numbers. \square

Rigid Comprehension is not used. The recorded proof from □Rigid Comprehension (□Rigid Comprehension implies Arithmetic Necessitism, Barton and Goodsell’s Theorem 3) needs no ND.
