# □ND ∧ □Rigid Comprehension ⇒ □Gallin Extensional Comprehension

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 20 September 2026, from Cian Dorr's suggestion of the same day; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 20 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□ND.** Necessarily, distinct things of any type are necessarily distinct.
- **□Rigid Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a rigid one.

## Conclusion

- **□Gallin Extensional Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation is coextensive with one that is persistent and has a persistent pointwise negation.

## Proof

By c5-and-persistent-comprehension-imply-gallin and rigid-comprehension-r-implies-persistent-comprehension-r, each closed instance of Gallin Extensional Comprehension follows in C from the corresponding closed instances of □ND and of Rigid Comprehension, so the conditional is a theorem of C and necessitates. K then needs the boxes of the antecedents: the boxed Rigid Comprehension premise supplies one, and □ND supplies the other by 4. Hence every closed instance of Gallin Extensional Comprehension is necessary.

## Notes

The necessitation of the recorded unboxed arrow from C5 and Persistent Comprehension, taken with the boxed form of Rigid Comprehension since the map has no boxed Persistent Comprehension.
