# □Plenitude ⇒ Atomicity by necessarily-world propositions

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by OpenAI Codex, prompted by Daniel Kodsi; recorded by OpenAI Codex, prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 30 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Plenitude.** Necessarily, every total single-valued binary relation is represented by an operation. Its output type is relational, as required by the type system.

## Conclusion

- **Atomicity by necessarily-world propositions.** Every possible proposition is entailed by a proposition that is necessarily a world.

## Proof

See [the common proof](../writeups/necessary-world-atomicity.md), §3. Boxed Plenitude gives C5 and Atomicity. Under C5, propositional ND preserves an atom’s nonzeroness; NI preserves each Boolean decision and BF collects the decision quantifier. Thus each current atom is necessarily an atom. The atom below any given nonzero proposition supplied by Atomicity is therefore the required witness.

## Notes

No uniform choice of atoms is made. The assertion is at propositional type t; its boxed-Plenitude and boxed-Gallin equivalents remain their full native schemata.
