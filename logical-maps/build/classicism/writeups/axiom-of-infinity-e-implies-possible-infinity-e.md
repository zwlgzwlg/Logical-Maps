# Axiom of Infinity (type e) ⇒ Possible Infinity (type e)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.axiom_of_infinity_e_implies_possible_infinity_e`; produced by Cian Dorr, suggestion of 20 September 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 20 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Axiom of Infinity (type e).** There are not finitely many individuals. No finite cardinality holds of the universal property, so the domain of the type cannot be exhausted by finitely many steps from the empty property.

## Conclusion

- **Possible Infinity (type e).** Possibly there are not finitely many individuals: the Axiom of Infinity at this type, under a diamond.

## Proof

By T for the defined necessity, $p\to\Diamond p$, applied to the Axiom of Infinity at type $e$.

## Notes

The map does not yet carry a model separating the two, which would need an evaluation point with finitely many individuals that can see one with infinitely many.
