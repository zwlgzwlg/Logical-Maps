# □ND ∧ Boolean Completeness ⇒ Actuality

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Classicism (2024), Lean `Classicism.Map.c5_and_completeness_imply_actuality`; produced by Andrew Bacon and Cian Dorr; recorded by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 17 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□ND.** Necessarily, distinct things of any type are necessarily distinct.
- **Boolean Completeness.** Every property of entities of a relational type has a greatest lower bound in that type.

## Conclusion

- **Actuality.** There is a true proposition that entails every true proposition.

## Proof

Under C5, Proposition 2.14 obtains Plenitude from Boolean Completeness. Proposition 2.15 then supplies a true atom. The underlying proofs are recorded separately.
