# Modal Well-Ordering ⇒ □Modal Well-Ordering

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 8 October 2026; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 8 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Modal Well-Ordering.** Every type, including e, has a relation that is necessarily a well-order of it.

## Conclusion

- **□Modal Well-Ordering.** Necessarily, every type, including e, has a relation that is necessarily a well-order of it.

## Proof

If $\Box\operatorname{WO}(R)$, then $\Box\Box\operatorname{WO}(R)$ by 4, and so $\Box\exists R'\, .\,\Box\operatorname{WO}(R')$, by necessitating the theorem $\Box\operatorname{WO}(R)\to\exists R'\, .\,\Box\operatorname{WO}(R')$ and applying K.

## Notes

With T, Modal Well-Ordering and its necessitation are equivalent in C.
