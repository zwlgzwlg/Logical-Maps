Eventually stationary entrance to the full successor action

Construction and proofs by GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), prompted by Daniel Kodsi, in the research note Strong worlds do not control modal domains of 26 October 2026, §§2–3. Checked and rewritten for the map by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 28 October 2026.

At the entrance r, □Strong Atomicity holds at every relational type, and so □Strong Actuality holds too. The Barcan Formula fails at t and at every relational type. It holds at e, where there is one individual.

The model

The target s. Its arrows s\to s are the natural numbers under addition, with the successor S:=1. It carries the full action-model domains over a single individual:

  • an entity of relational type is an arbitrary profile m\mapsto F_m of sets of argument tuples;
  • transport is (m\cdot F)_u=F_{um}.

The entrance r. It has only its identity, and arrows h_n:r\to s for n\in\mathbb N, with m\circ h_n=h_{m+n}. An entity of relational type at r is a pair (A,b):

  • A is an arbitrary set of current argument tuples;
  • b is a target entity that is eventually stationary: S^{N+1}b=S^Nb for some N;
  • its transport along h_n is S^nb.

The pair is indeed the whole intension. Its row at 1_r is A, and its row at h_n is the identity row of S^nb; compatibility with composition is m\cdot(h_n\cdot F)=h_{m+n}\cdot F. Equality at r is equality of the pairs. Evaluation point: 1_r. The constants of Σ denote top elements, which are fixed by the successor.

A model of C

The target is a full action model. At the entrance, the current extension of every term’s denotation is admitted, since current extensions are unrestricted. So it is enough that the target part of each term’s denotation is eventually stationary. By naturality of the target semantics, that target part is the target denotation of the term with its parameters transported. Choose N beyond the stages of the term’s finitely many parameters. From N on, the transported parameters are fixed by S, and hence so is the term’s denotation.

So the domains are closed under the terms of C, and the H-provable equivalences identify abstractions as in the target. The variables bound in the term range over the whole target domain; only its free parameters need a common stage.

□Strong Atomicity

At r, at every relational type, two kinds of singleton are admitted:

  • one current tuple, with target part 0;
  • one target coordinate (n,\bar a), with empty current extension and target part \delta_{n,\bar a}. The successor carries this to \delta_{n-1,\bar a}, and after n+1 steps to 0, so it is eventually stationary.

Every nonzero entity at r contains one of them, since its current extension or some row of its target part is nonempty. Each is an atom, because the order is inclusion of complete profiles.

They are strong atoms. Their transports are 0 or target singletons, and a target singleton is an atom of the full target domain, so it decides every target relation. The full target is strongly atomic in the same way. Hence Strong Atomicity holds at both objects, at every relational type, and □Strong Atomicity holds at r.

The Barcan Formula fails

At t. The target part of a proposition at r is a set of numbers that is eventually stationary under the shift, that is, finite or cofinite. Take the pure property

P(p):=\Diamond\Box p\lor\Diamond\Box\neg p .

  • Every proposition at r necessarily has P. A finite set is eventually empty and a cofinite set eventually everything, and the same holds of their shifts. So P holds of every proposition at r, and at every h_n of its transport.
  • Not every proposition has it at s. The set of even numbers is neither eventually everything nor eventually empty, so P fails of it there.

So \forall p\,.\,\Box Pp holds at r but \Box\forall p\,.\,Pp does not.

At a relational type \tau. The target has an operation L_\tau, of type \tau\tau, that sends b to Sb. Its profile reads b at the next coordinate and does not depend on the arrow, so S fixes it. Let E_\tau at r have target part L_\tau. Then:

  • Every entity at r necessarily has \Diamond(E_\tau Y=Y), since its transports are eventually fixed by the shift.
  • A target entity alternating between even and odd coordinates never does.

So the Barcan Formula fails at \tau.

At e it holds necessarily, there being one individual.
