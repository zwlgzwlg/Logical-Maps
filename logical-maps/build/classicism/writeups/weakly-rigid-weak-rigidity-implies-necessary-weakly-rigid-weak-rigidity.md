## Premises

- **Weakly Rigid Weak Rigidity.** Being weakly rigid is a weakly rigid property of relations, including propositions.

## Conclusion

- **□Weakly Rigid Weak Rigidity.** Every closed instance of Weakly Rigid Weak Rigidity is necessary. Retain the same type range.

## Proof

Fix the type tuple. The principle gives Rigid Rigidity and □Tame Rigidity at that tuple (recorded results, the first using the principle also at the next type). Both are necessary by 4, since Rigid Rigidity and □Tame Rigidity have the form $\Box\phi$.
