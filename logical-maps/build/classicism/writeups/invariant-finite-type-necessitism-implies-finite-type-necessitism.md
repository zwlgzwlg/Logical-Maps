# Invariant Finite-Type Necessitism ⇒ Finite-Type Necessitism

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Kodsi, Finite-Type Necessitism (2026); produced by Daniel Kodsi, with assistance from GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI); recorded by ChatGPT, at Daniel Kodsi’s request.</p>

## Premises

- **Invariant Finite-Type Necessitism.** The variant of Finite-Type Necessitism obtained by requiring invariance and inextensibility, rather than persistence and inextensibility, throughout the witnesses and represented powers.

## Conclusion

- **Finite-Type Necessitism.** Every possibly realizable finite arithmetic hierarchy has a rigid realization at the same carrier type, with a full rigid represented power of its last stage.

## Proof

Whenever an instance is activated, the invariant representative carrier and Boolean-cancellation argument yield necessary 5. Under necessary 5 ordinary and invariant rigidity coincide; the invariant witness is therefore an ordinary FTN witness. If no hierarchy is possible the instance is vacuous. [Paper (PDF)](https://github.com/dkodsi/Logical-Maps/blob/classicism-next-batch-2026-10-30/logical-maps/topics/classicism/papers/finite-type-necessitism.pdf); [source](https://github.com/dkodsi/Logical-Maps/blob/classicism-next-batch-2026-10-30/logical-maps/topics/classicism/papers/finite-type-necessitism.tex).

## Notes

The proof does not infer that ordinary represented powers follow by simply weakening the carrier definition; it uses the equivalence under necessary 5.
