# Atomicity by necessarily-world propositions ⇒ □Plenitude

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by OpenAI Codex, prompted by Daniel Kodsi; recorded by OpenAI Codex, prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 30 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Atomicity by necessarily-world propositions.** Every possible proposition is entailed by a proposition that is necessarily a world.

## Conclusion

- **□Plenitude.** Necessarily, every total single-valued binary relation is represented by an operation. Its output type is relational, as required by the type system.

## Proof

See [the common proof](../writeups/necessary-world-atomicity.md), §§1–2. Coverage makes a true necessarily atomic proposition necessarily available. Such a proposition witnesses Actuality and forces current propositional ND. Necessitating the closed implication yields necessary Actuality and necessary propositional ND. The recorded type-transfer theorem gives C5; the native C5 plus necessary Actuality theorem gives Atomicity, and C5 plus Atomicity gives boxed Plenitude.

## Notes

No optional premise is necessitated. Necessary nonzeroness is used in deriving ND; strong atomhood alone does not suffice. The all-type boxed Gallin consequence follows through existing results.
