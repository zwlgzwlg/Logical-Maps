Symmetric Cohen model: Rosser-selected component

GPT-6 Astra, prompted by Daniel Kodsi. 30 October 2026.

There is a countable classical Henkin model of C satisfying Second-Order Analytic Necessitism but failing Necessity of Arithmetic. The arithmetic guard \Box I^*_e holds. All analytic possible-real antecedents are false.

This changes the ground component of the arithmetic-growth Cohen construction. An elementary extension of its standard-arithmetic component preserves every first-order arithmetic truth. Here an effective theory preserving NoReal lets Rosser incompleteness select a component that disagrees with standard arithmetic. The root still has standard current arithmetic. The new combination is this arithmetic truth disagreement with the all-host NoReal construction. The old named-number-above-all-numerals package is not imposed on the new component.

1. The effective component theory

The base construction in Symmetric Cohen model: arithmetic growth, §§2–5 gives a countable ground classical Henkin model A_0 of

C5+\Box I^*_e+ \{\Box\neg\exists\mathfrak R\,\operatorname{Real}_\tau(\mathfrak R): \tau\text{ an admitted type}\}.

Its current canonical numbers and arithmetic graphs are standard. It also supplies the exact whole-object equations I^*_e=\top and \exists\mathfrak R\operatorname{Real}_\tau(\mathfrak R)=\bot. The NoReal theorem and Boolean-Henkin reduction in that proof are essential inputs. Compactness or incompleteness alone would not establish them.

Fix a countable many-sorted first-order language containing a sort for each admitted type; application, Boolean proposition operations, typed equality and quantifier operations; a current-truth predicate T; and an operation for every finite lambda template with its finite typed parameter list. The effective value translation of a typed term a is written \operatorname{val}(a). Let T_H contain the following effective schemes, universally closed over their finite parameter contexts:

  • The proposition Boolean-algebra equations, T(\top), \neg T(\bot), \top\ne\bot, and the Boolean truth clauses.
  • T(\operatorname{Eq}_\sigma(x,y))\leftrightarrow x=y and T(Q_\sigma F)\leftrightarrow\forall x^\sigma\,T(Fx).
  • The original H axioms and the beta, eta, substitution, renaming and logical-constant compatibility equations for all parameterized templates.
  • The literal equality of the two abstraction values for every certified H-proof of the equivalence of their bodies. The proof certificate selects precisely an instance of C’s Logical Equivalence; equivalence under T_H or under any optional hypothesis is not an admissible substitute.
  • The truth translations of C5, \Box I^*_e, and each boxed NoReal instance.

Finite syntax, conversion certificates and H-proof certificates can be enumerated. Include finite enumeration-stage certificates when checking an axiom occurrence, so ordinary finite proofs are effectively recognizable. Thus T_H is computably enumerable. It contains neither A_0’s elementary diagram nor its full true arithmetic theory.

Expanding A_0 by its actual term operations and truth predicate gives a model of T_H. Conversely the displayed truth, equality, quantifier and lambda clauses make every first-order model of T_H an eligible classical C5 Henkin component with the required boxed infinity and NoReal statements. This uses all parameterized lambda operations, not just an application reduct. No current-extensionality quotient is taken. These two semantic facts suffice; no equivalence between two different proof calculi is required.

2. Selecting an arithmetically disagreeing component

For an ordinary arithmetic sentence \varphi, let \varphi^e be its canonical individual-counting translation. Variables range over \mathbb N_e at \nu=(et)t; identity, zero and successor are the native terms; eliminate function symbols for addition and multiplication through the native \operatorname{Sum} and \operatorname{Prod} graphs.

Under I^*_e this is a recursive interpretation of PA. Zero is not a successor and successor is injective by the guard. The impredicative least-inductive property of canonical numbers gives every first-order induction instance by applying it to the lambda predicate of the translated formula, with parameters. The native recursion definitions give total unique Sum and Prod graphs, so function-term elimination and first-order logical proof translation are valid. These are the current relational arithmetic arguments; they require no choice of an operation from its graph. Consequently T_H proves T(\operatorname{val}(\varphi^e)) for every PA theorem \varphi, and translation commutes with Boolean operations and negation.

Let U be the arithmetic theory with axioms

\{\varphi:T_H\vdash T(\operatorname{val}(\varphi^e))\}.

Dovetail over arithmetic sentences and finite T_H proofs to enumerate it. A finite arithmetic proof from these axioms translates back into T_H, using the interpretation and truth clauses. Hence

U\vdash\varphi\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad T_H\vdash T(\operatorname{val}(\varphi^e)).

The standard arithmetic of A_0 makes every U axiom true in \mathbb N. In particular U is a consistent computably enumerable extension of PA. Rosser’s first incompleteness theorem gives an ordinary sentence G neither proved nor refuted by U. Its usual proof-comparison fixed point is standard-true: there is no actual proof of G, so the antecedent of each standard comparison is false. Finite axiom-enumeration certificates permit this use of Rosser for the computably enumerable theory. No internal consistency statement, omega-consistency assumption or second-incompleteness argument is needed.

Thus T_H+\neg T(\operatorname{val}(G^e)) is consistent. First-order completeness and countable downward Löwenheim–Skolem yield a countable ground model A of it. Construct its domains and operations before any auxiliary Cohen evaluation and give them the trivial symmetry action.

The boxed hypotheses give the whole-object equations

(I^*_e)_A=\top_A,\qquad (\exists\mathfrak R\operatorname{Real}_\tau(\mathfrak R))_A=\bot_A.

C5 makes equality propositions crisp: identity gives necessary identity, and inequality gives necessary inequality. If g=\operatorname{val}_A(G^e), current falsity gives g\ne\top_A. We do not need or infer g=\bot_A. The NoReal bottom equations come from their separate boxed hypotheses.

3. The hybrid and its C interpretation

Use the same Boolean hierarchy and symmetry group as the original construction:

B=\operatorname{RO}((2^\omega)^\omega),\qquad B_{\rm fs}=\bigcup_{J\subseteq\omega\text{ finite}}B_J,

with finite products of finite-prefix permutations, and trivial action on A. The hybrid has

D_e^r=\omega\times D_e^A,\qquad D_t^r=B_{\rm fs}\times B_A.

At a relational type, an entity is a finitely supported current Boolean profile paired with an admitted A-component. Application, equality, quantifiers and lambda use exactly the hybrid clauses in §7. Projection \pi_\tau to A is onto and commutes with all term operations. The component’s admitted quantifiers and lambda operations are kept intact; its nonstandard domains are not replaced by external full function spaces.

For propositions, native necessity has the whole value

\Box(b,a)= \bigl(\delta_{b=1\land a=\top_A},\ \delta_{a=\top_A}\bigr),

where the second delta is \top_A or \bot_A. In particular its current Boolean coordinate tests whether both coordinates of the original proposition are top. The original closure, support and C proofs require ground component data and the component’s C operations. They do not require that the component be elementary over A_0, so they apply to this A.

4. NoReal survives the component change

For a field supported on finitely many Cohen coordinates J, the original moving-object lemma supplies a generic-truth-filter-fixing prefix-transposition involution whenever an element has essential support outside J. At the classical evaluation this is an actual order automorphism of order two. An order automorphism of finite order must fix every element: otherwise repeated application to a strict inequality contradicts its finite order. Thus all field elements descend to the intermediate universe using only the coordinates J. Every subproperty in that universe is represented by an admitted J-supported predicate. A fresh Cohen coordinate supplies an admitted bounded rational cut whose supremum cannot belong to that intermediate field. This refutes completeness.

Sections 3–4 and 7.2 of the original construction prove the required moving-object and finite-coordinate descent statements for the whole typed hierarchy. Their component assumptions are only groundness, trivial symmetry action and the unchanged admitted operations. The new A satisfies these assumptions. Hence the current Boolean value of existential Real is zero at every host. Its component is bottom by §2, so the entire proposition is

(\exists\mathfrak R\operatorname{Real}_\tau(\mathfrak R))_r =(0,\bot_A).

Therefore necessarily no real structure exists at any admitted type. Every SOAN and AN possible-real antecedent is false. This is analytic vacuity at all hosts, not a nonvacuous real-field witness.

5. Current arithmetic, including its recursion graphs

Let K=\{\bar n^r:n\in\mathbb N\} be the set of whole standard root numeral objects. Its support-empty characteristic predicate is admitted with the appropriate arbitrary component. Boolean finite induction through each admitted inductive predicate, and this characteristic test in the opposite direction, give the exact identity

\mathbb N_r=(\chi_K,\mathbb N_A).

Crisp finite individual subsets distinguish all standard numerals. Successor is injective on them and omits zero. Together with the component equation, this gives I^*_e=(1,\top_A) and hence current \Box I^*_e.

Standard number objects alone do not prove standard arithmetic truth; verify Sum and Prod. Fix standard m. The support-empty current graph

\{(\bar i^r,\overline{m+i}^r):i\in\mathbb N\}

is admitted, paired with any A-component. It satisfies the initial and successor-closure premises of native Sum with Boolean value 1, and makes each incorrect standard output have value 0. Conversely take any admitted, possibly noncrisp recursion test R. Let b be the conjunction of the Boolean values of its initial and closure premises. Then b\le R(\bar0,\bar m); finite induction gives b\le R(\bar i,\overline{m+i}) for every standard i. Thus every correct Sum instance has defining implication value 1 for every test; their meet is 1. Incorrect outputs have value 0 by the displayed characteristic graph.

The same argument for \{(\bar i^r,\overline{mi}^r):i\in\mathbb N\}, using the verified Sum graph, proves standard Prod. The recursion quantifiers are guarded by the crisp \mathbb N predicate. These definitions are nonmodal in their current coordinate, so arbitrary component coordinates do not change the calculation. The identical argument works in the source base A_0 and supplies the standard arithmetic graphs used in §2’s soundness argument.

Induction on the native first-order arithmetic grammar now yields current value 1 exactly for the standard-true sentences, and 0 for the standard-false ones. Projection to A preserves the component value of every formula. In particular

(G^e)_r=(1,g),\qquad g\ne\top_A.

6. Failure of the exact guarded necessity schema

Since I^*_e is the entire top object, I_e=\Diamond I^*_e is also the entire top object. The native instance is

\Box(I_e\to G^e)\ \lor\ \Box(I_e\to\neg G^e).

The first alternative has current value 0 because the component g differs from \top_A. The second has current value 0 because the first coordinate of \neg G^e is 0. Both alternatives therefore fail while \Box I^*_e holds. This is the exact Necessity of Arithmetic failure. It uses equality-defined necessity on whole objects; it does not infer modal disagreement from mere nonstandardness or append an unverified accessible point.

7. A classical ground model

Apply the Boolean-Henkin reduction of the original proof, §5 to this hybrid. Choose a countable elementary substructure of the semantic algebra retaining the value map and all syntactic lambda operations. Meet the countably many dense witness requirements for its retained predicates. The resulting ground ultrafilter defines classical current truth with literal identity and the full Henkin quantifier truth lemma at every retained type. Whole propositions are not quotiented by present agreement.

The C axioms, boxed NoReal, analytic schemas and \Box I^*_e have Boolean value 1, whereas the displayed necessity instance has value 0. These endpoint values survive both restriction and the witness-filter evaluation. The final model is an actual countable classical Henkin model in ZFC, without assuming a countable transitive model of ZFC or an actual generic over the universe.
