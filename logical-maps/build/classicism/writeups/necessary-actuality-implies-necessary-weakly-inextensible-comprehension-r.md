# □Actuality ⇒ □Weakly Inextensible Comprehension

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 27 September 2026; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 27 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Actuality.** Necessarily, there is a true proposition that entails every true proposition.

## Conclusion

- **□Weakly Inextensible Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a weakly inextensible one.

## Proof

The recorded derivation of Weakly Inextensible Comprehension from Actuality uses no optional hypothesis, so for each closed instance $\phi$ of Weakly Inextensible Comprehension, Actuality $\to\phi$ is a theorem of C. Necessitate it and apply K to $\Box$Actuality.
