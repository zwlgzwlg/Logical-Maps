# B ⇒ ND

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Prior, Formal Logic, Lean `Classicism.Map.modal_b_implies_distinctness_necessary_r`; produced by A. N. Prior; recorded by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 17 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **B.** Every true proposition is necessarily possible.

## Conclusion

- **ND.** Distinct things of any type are necessarily distinct.

## Proof

Assume $x\ne y$. B gives $\Box\neg\Box(x=y)$. The necessitation of NI gives $\Box(x=y\to\Box(x=y))$. Within the box, contraposition yields $\neg(x=y)$, and K therefore yields $\Box(x\ne y)$. The argument is uniform at each type.
