## Premises

- **□Weakly Inextensible Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a weakly inextensible one.

## Conclusion

- **Weakly Inextensible Comprehension.** Every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a weakly inextensible one.
