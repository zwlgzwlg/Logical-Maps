# Inextensible Comprehension ⇒ Weakly Inextensible Comprehension

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.inextensible_comprehension_r_implies_weakly_inextensible_comprehension_r`; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 25 September 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 25 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Inextensible Comprehension.** Every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a inextensible one.

## Conclusion

- **Weakly Inextensible Comprehension.** Every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a weakly inextensible one.

## Proof

Inextensible is the necessitation of weakly inextensible, so T turns an inextensible coextension into a weakly inextensible one. Use the same witness.

## Notes

With the recorded implication from Rigid Comprehension to Inextensible Comprehension, this gives Rigid Comprehension ⇒ Weakly Inextensible Comprehension.
