# Finite-Type Necessitism ⇒ Second-Order Analytic Necessitism

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Kodsi, Finite-Type Necessitism (2026); produced by Daniel Kodsi, with assistance from GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI); recorded by ChatGPT, at Daniel Kodsi’s request.</p>

## Premises

- **Finite-Type Necessitism.** Every possibly realizable finite arithmetic hierarchy has a rigid realization at the same carrier type, with a full rigid represented power of its last stage.

## Conclusion

- **Second-Order Analytic Necessitism.** If there could be a real number structure, there is a rigid one, necessarily a real number structure, together with represented powers of every arity up to $n$: rigid properties of rigid relations among the structure's elements that necessarily represent every relation on them. One sentence for each type $\sigma$ and each $n\ge1$.

## Proof

Full FTN contains the height-one instance at every carrier type. By Theorem 4.3 this is equivalent to SOAN at that type. The proof uses same-carrier Dedekind cuts in one direction, Cantor subset codes in the other, and unary-to-finite-arity represented powers (Lemma 4.1). See the linked paper, Definition 1.1 and Theorem 4.3.
