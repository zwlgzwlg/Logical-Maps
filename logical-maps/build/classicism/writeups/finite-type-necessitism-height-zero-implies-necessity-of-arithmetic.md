# Finite-Type Necessitism: height 0 ⇒ Necessity of Arithmetic

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Kodsi, Finite-Type Necessitism (2026); produced by Daniel Kodsi, with assistance from GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI); recorded by ChatGPT, at Daniel Kodsi’s request.</p>

## Premises

- **Finite-Type Necessitism: height 0.** The exact height-0 fragment of Finite-Type Necessitism, at every carrier type. A possible Peano presentation has a rigid realization at the same type, with its full rigid represented power.

## Conclusion

- **Necessity of Arithmetic.** Every arithmetical sentence is either necessarily true or necessarily false, given that possibly zero is not a successor and successor is injective on numbers, where the numbers are the finite cardinalities of properties of a type $\sigma$. One sentence for each type $\sigma$ and each arithmetical sentence at $\sigma$.

## Proof

The possible canonical-arithmetic guard activates F0. Rigid recursion supplies addition and multiplication on its reference. Goodsell’s arithmetic interpretation lemma, Peano matching and the reference argument fix arithmetic truth; when the possibility guard fails it fails necessarily. [Paper (PDF)](https://github.com/dkodsi/Logical-Maps/blob/classicism-next-batch-2026-10-30/logical-maps/topics/classicism/papers/finite-type-necessitism.pdf); [source](https://github.com/dkodsi/Logical-Maps/blob/classicism-next-batch-2026-10-30/logical-maps/topics/classicism/papers/finite-type-necessitism.tex).
