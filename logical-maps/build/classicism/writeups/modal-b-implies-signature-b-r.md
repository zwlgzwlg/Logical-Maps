## Premises

- **B.** Every true proposition is necessarily possible.

## Conclusion

- **B for sentences of Σ.** The B instance for every closed sentence of the language of the fixed signature Sigma.

## Proof

Instantiate the universally quantified B at the proposition expressed by the closed sentence $P$.
