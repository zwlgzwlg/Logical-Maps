# Weak Rigid Comprehension ⇒ Weakly Inextensible Comprehension

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.weak_rigid_comprehension_r_implies_weakly_inextensible_comprehension_r`; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 25 September 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 25 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Weak Rigid Comprehension.** Every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a weakly rigid one, that is, with one that is weakly persistent and weakly inextensible.

## Conclusion

- **Weakly Inextensible Comprehension.** Every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a weakly inextensible one.

## Proof

Weak rigidity has weak inextensibility as a conjunct, so a weakly rigid coextension is already a weakly inextensible coextension.

## Notes

The other conjunct gives the recorded implication to Persistent Comprehension. Weak Rigid Comprehension is equivalent to Boolean Completeness with Actuality, and each of those alone already implies Weakly Inextensible Comprehension; see the recorded results.
