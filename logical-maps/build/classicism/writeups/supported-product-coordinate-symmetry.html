Supported-product action model: external coordinate symmetry

GPT-6 Astra, prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 30 October 2026.

Logical Necessity together with necessary propositional Boolean Completeness does not imply full Boolean Completeness, Weakly Inextensible Comprehension, or Rigid Power. One construction supplies all three separations. It also has necessary all-type Barcan, Countable Boolean Completeness and Tame Rigidity, and current Atomlessness.

The change to the free-monoid supported product is a second support condition on higher relations. It restricts represented families sufficiently to give propositional joins while preserving the higher-type obstructions. Its three joint separations use existing principles; the simpler separations from Logical Necessity alone already have the original product as a witness.

1. The two support conditions

Work in external ZFC. For a prescribed set-sized finitary signature choose an infinite cardinal \kappa bounding its syntax. Let |I|=\kappa^+ and let L be the free monoid on \kappa letters. The single object’s arrows are M=L^I, with coordinatewise concatenation. Write 1 for the empty-word arrow. Individuals form a singleton. The external group G=\operatorname{Sym}(I) acts by (gm)_i=m_{g^{-1}i}; its elements are not extra accessibility arrows.

At each relational type \tau=\sigma_1\cdots\sigma_nt, admit all profiles F:M\to\mathcal P(\prod_iD_{\sigma_i}) with both:

  • an arrow support S\subseteq I, |S|\le\kappa, such that F_m=F_{m'} whenever m|_S=m'|_S;
  • a permutation support T\subseteq I, |T|\le\kappa, such that every g fixing T pointwise fixes the whole profile under (gF)_m=g[F_{g^{-1}m}].

These domains and group actions are defined recursively. Transport is (hF)_k=F_{kh}; equality at the object is literal whole-profile identity. Truth of a scalar means membership of 1. Every scalar depending on at most \kappa coordinates satisfies both conditions, so the original scalar algebra is unchanged.

The action is covariant: g(hF)=(gh)(gF), where gh on the right denotes the permuted arrow. Transport hF retains arrow support S and has permutation support S\cup T: if g fixes that union, gh agrees with h on S. This works for arbitrary arrows, without restricting their supports.

For a term with finitely many parameters, take the unions of their two kinds of support. Arrows agreeing on the first union transport all parameters to identical whole entities. Evaluation of a formula, including its quantified subformulas over the full admitted domains, is then identical. Equivariance under G follows by induction, since it bijects both arrows and every domain. Thus abstraction has the two supports. Application, with (Fa)_m(\bar b)=F_m(ma,\bar b), does too. Constant singleton predicates have empty arrow support and the permutation support of their parameter, and distinguish unequal whole entities. Transported haecceities are admitted as well.

The ordinary action semantics therefore validates the lambda rules and H. An H-theorem-level equivalence agrees at every arrow and tuple and identifies the corresponding abstraction profiles. This verifies C with its theoremhood restriction. Pure closed sentences have constant truth across this one-object model; a verified pure closed instance consequently holds necessarily.

2. Scalar joins by a support hull

Two scalar support facts will be useful. If S,R both support p, then S\cap R supports it: patch an arrow taking the required values on S and R. If p is invariant under all permutations fixing T, move S\setminus T to fresh coordinates disjoint from S\cup T. The moved support and S intersect inside T. Thus such a p depends only on T.

For an admitted X:tt, put E=X_1. A permutation support T of X makes E invariant under \operatorname{Fix}(T). Define the scalar

J=\{m:\exists n\in\bigcup E\ (n|_T=m|_T)\}.

It has support T and contains each member of E. To prove leastness, let b be any admitted upper bound, with arrow support R. For m\in J choose p\in E and n\in p agreeing with m on T. If S supports p, choose g fixing T and moving S\setminus T off R\cup T. Then gp\in E, and its support gS meets R only inside T. Patch an arrow k agreeing with gn on gS and m on R. It belongs to gp, hence b, so m\in b. The empty-family case gives J=\varnothing.

This is the least upper bound among all admitted scalars. The union \bigcup E need not itself be admitted. Every represented scalar family has a join, proving \Box\mathrm{BC}_t.

3. Necessary Barcan and countable completeness

Let F have arrow support S and permutation support T. To lift F through an arbitrary h, define G_m=F_{r(m)} if every m_i, i\in S, ends in h_i, where r(m)_i removes that suffix on S and is empty elsewhere. Set G_m=\varnothing if a suffix test fails. Free-word cancellation gives hG=F. The new profile has arrow support S and permutation support S\cup T. Every transport is therefore onto at every type, yielding \Box\mathrm{BF}.

The pointwise union of at most \kappa profiles retains both supports. For any counting type \sigma, the external orbit of the closed terms \operatorname{Suc}_\sigma^n(\mathbf0_\sigma) has an admitted constant characteristic profile with both supports empty. It is currently inductive. Finite induction gives the converse, so native numberhood has exactly this countable orbit as its actual extension. No distinctness of its iterates is assumed. A native countability witness assigns each actual family member a nonempty set of numeral labels, with disjoint label sets for distinct members. Assigning externally the least iteration index of a label injects the family into \mathbb N. Its pointwise union is admitted and is its whole-profile least upper bound. This proves \Box\mathrm{CBC} at every counting type and relational output type, without requiring an admitted choice operation.

4. Higher weak inextensibility and completeness fail

Let q_i=\{m:m_i=\varepsilon\}. The constant profile with extension E=\{q_i:i\in I\} has both supports empty. Suppose Y:tt has current extension E, and choose j outside an arrow support of Y. Let h have a nonempty word only at j. Then Y_h=E. The admitted test

Z_m=\begin{cases}D_t,&m_j=\varepsilon,\\ D_t\setminus\{q_j\},&m_j\ne\varepsilon \end{cases}

has both supports \{j\}. Every transported current member q_i satisfies Z: it remains q_i or becomes bottom. Yet the fresh q_j\in Y_h fails Z_h. Thus every current coextension of E fails weak inextensibility. This refutes WIC at tt.

The existing same-type proof of BC implying WIC takes a represented family of haecceities at the output type. Applied here at output tt, a least upper bound would give the impossible weakly inextensible coextension (intersect with the original relation). Hence BC fails at tt, although BC at t holds necessarily.

5. Exact images and Tame Rigidity

For E a set of tuples write (H_E)_m=mE. If this profile is admitted, it and all its transports are weakly persistent and weakly inextensible: all target instances are precisely transported current ones. Thus it is rigid. Conversely let Y be weakly rigid, E=Y_1. Persistence gives mE\subseteq Y_m. If T_0 supports E under permutations, then for each small Q the profile

B_Q(E)_m=\bigcup\{nE:n|_Q=m|_Q\}

has arrow support Q and permutation support Q\cup T_0. Each current E-member necessarily satisfies it, so Y\le B_Q(E). For b\in Y_m choose Q containing the arrow supports of Y and the entries of b. Find n,a with n|_Q=m|_Q, a\in E and na=b. Set r_i=u_i=\varepsilon on Q and r_i=n_i,u_i=m_i elsewhere. Then mr=un; Y_r=E, weak persistence puts ra\in E, and u fixes b. Therefore b=u(na)=m(ra)\in mE. Thus Y=H_E.

Weakly rigid admitted profiles are therefore rigid, and the closed pure schema holds necessarily. This is \BoxTame Rigidity. It does not assert that every external exact-image profile is admitted.

6. Rigid Power fails

Fix o\in I. For j\ne o, let y_j:tt be the constant profile \{q_j\}. For z\in\mathbb Z, define e_{z,j}:tt with r=|m_o|, n=|m_j| by

(e_{z,j})_m=\begin{cases} \{q_j\}\cup\{\bot:\chi(n+z)=1\},&r=0,\\ \{q_j\},&r=1,\ n+z\ge0,\\ \varnothing,&r=1,\ n+z<0,\\ \{q_j\},&r\ge2, \end{cases}

where \chi(k)=1 exactly at nonnegative squares. Its two supports are \{o,j\}. Square tails distinguish z, and q_j distinguishes j. For one-letter arrows a at o and b_j at j,

b_je_{z,j}=e_{z+1,j},\qquad a^2e_{z,j}=y_j,\qquad ae_{z,j}=y_j\ \Longleftrightarrow\ z\ge0.

Set E=\{e_{z,j}:z\in\mathbb Z,j\ne o\} and B_0=\{y_j:j\ne o\}. The exact images F=H_E and X=H_{B_0} are admitted with support \{o\} of both kinds; arbitrary shifts in the other coordinates permute the integer indices. Both are rigid and X\le aF.

If a rigid Y\le F satisfied aY=X, the exact-image lemma gives Y=H_A for A\subseteq E and aA=B_0. Choose j outside its arrow support. Then b_jA=A. The nonempty j-section of A, needed to supply y_j, is invariant under the bilateral shift of \mathbb Z, so contains every integer, including -1. But ae_{-1,j}\notin B_0, contradiction. The canonical rigid-power property of F has the fresh member X at a and is not an exact image. It is not rigid. The failed bound type is (tt)t.

7. Gluing the fixed signature

Fix these pure domains before choosing interpretations. Select one interpretation J_\alpha for every signature sentence satisfiable over them. There are at most \kappa sentence/constant pairs. Choose j outside the union of both supports of every selected constant denotation. Interpret each relational constant c by the profile whose row at m is J_\alpha(c)_m when m_j ends in the letter for \alpha, and empty when no such letter occurs. Its supports are the corresponding unions together with \{j\}. A one-letter arrow h_\alpha at j recovers the whole interpretation J_\alpha simultaneously; transporting any interpretation still gives an interpretation on the fixed pure domains.

A signature sentence is consequently possible exactly when it is satisfiable by an interpretation on these domains. For pure P and distinct matching constant symbols \bar c, this gives \Diamond P[\bar c/\bar x]\leftrightarrow\exists\bar x P, and by negation \Box P[\bar c/\bar x]\leftrightarrow\forall\bar x P. This is current Logical Necessity, including the empty tuple. It is not a claim of necessary Logical Necessity or of Maximalism.

8. Atomlessness and the choice obstructions

A nonzero scalar with arrow support S splits by q_j for fresh j. For any current true p with support S, an arrow nonempty only at fresh j still satisfies p but fails the current truth q_j. This refutes Actuality. Since \Diamond q_j=q_j, the same example refutes Vicinity.

For Relational Choice at input/output (t,t) consider the admitted serial relation

D(p,q)=(\neg p\land\neg q\land\neg(q\le p))\lor(p\land q=\bot).

At a false p, use q=\neg q_j for j outside its support. At a true p, use bottom. Suppose a functional subrelation has permutation support T. Put b_T=\neg\{m:\forall i\in T\ m_i=\varepsilon\}. It is false and fixed by \operatorname{Fix}(T). Uniqueness forces its selected output q to be fixed by that group, hence T-coordinate-supported by §2. Since q is false, it cannot contain an arrow empty on T; thus q\le b_T, contradicting D(b_T,q).

For Zorn at carrier t, let P(p)=\neg p and put R(p,q)=\neg P(p)\lor(P(q)\land p\le q). True scalars lie below all false scalars; false scalars are ordered by inclusion. Fresh-coordinate escape shows there is no maximal element. If X is an admitted R-chain with permutation support T, every involution fixing T fixes each false member: applying it to a strict comparison with its image would reverse that comparison. Every permutation fixing T is a product of two such involutions, by reflecting each finite cyclic or bilateral infinite orbit. Thus every false member depends only on T and lies below b_T. This false scalar bounds the chain, including the empty chain. Zorn’s native represented-chain hypothesis holds but its conclusion fails.

The construction uses the original supported-product witnesses and signature gluing, the support-moving hull method, and the support obstruction to selectors. The added permutation condition is responsible for the new joint Logical-Necessity/scalar-completeness separations. No all-type completeness, positive choice, current ND or analytical verdict is inferred.
