Henkin compactness: full-current surjective cover

GPT-6 Astra, prompted by Daniel Kodsi. 30 October 2026.

For every set-sized free fixed signature \Sigma, one general Henkin model of C(\Sigma) satisfies Maximalism for \Sigma, strict Forcing Possibilism, and current Barcan at every type. The same model satisfies the exact signature Possibility+ schema, current Actuality, Truths are ND-necessary, Transversal Choice and the quantified Axiom of Infinity at e.

The connection is joint compatibility of these principles. The construction combines the surjective-chain-name interpretation cover with a full-current product, then uses Henkin compactness for larger signatures. The finite-pin cardinal bound and the whole-target Barcan transfer are the steps that make the combination work. All modal qualifications are current. The final compactness model is not asserted to have full external powersets or continuum-sized domains.

1. A countable complete family of whole action presentations

Work in external ZFC. Use the set-sized action-model completeness theorem for C (Bacon and Dorr, Theorem 3.23). The pure language has countably many finite expressions. For each consistent pure closed sentence choose a pointed action model realizing it. We need to reduce the entire semantic presentation, not merely the domain at its evaluation point.

Encode such a model as a countable many-sorted first-order structure. Include objects and arrows with source, target, identities and partial composition; for each admitted type \sigma, a sort E_\sigma of tagged local entities with its location map; transport, application, current truth, and the sections for logical constants. Include the graph of local denotation for every finite pure term template with every finite typed parameter context. In particular, all parameterized lambda abstractions are retained. Partial operations can be graph relations with their guarded existence and uniqueness clauses.

The following first-order clauses hold, with location guards understood:

\begin{aligned} T(Q_\sigma(V)F)&\leftrightarrow \forall x\in E_\sigma(V)\,T(Fx),\\ T(\Box p)&\leftrightarrow \forall h\ (\operatorname{source}(h)=V\to T(\rho_h p)),\\ T(\operatorname{Eq}_\sigma(x,y))&\leftrightarrow x=y. \end{aligned}

Retain category laws, transport and term naturality, beta/eta and the term-template composition clauses. Also retain a profile-separation clause for every fixed relational type \tau=\sigma_1\cdots\sigma_k t: if a\ne b at V, some h:V\to W and target tuple \bar x distinguish the truth of (\rho_h a)[\bar x] and (\rho_h b)[\bar x]. Include k=0 for propositions. This clause is first-order for each fixed finite type. It prevents distinct whole entities from becoming identical when arrows or argument domains shrink.

Take a countable elementary substructure containing the designated object and one witness in each sort. The usual iterative existential-witness closure constructs it: at each stage close under the countably many operations and choose witnesses for all existential formulas with parameters already chosen. The union is countable and elementary. If a presentation includes designated symmetry groups, include their action, inverse and composition relations too.

The retained objects and arrows form a category; every retained typed fiber is nonempty and countable. Represent retained relations by their rows on all retained future arrows and all retained argument tuples. Profile separation makes this representation injective. Type recursion identifies application with slicing and transport with reindexing. The quantifier clause now ranges over the entire retained fiber, and the modal clause over all retained arrows. Elementarity supplies a retained counterexample whenever a retained universal clause is false. The term-template clauses prove preservation of interpretation by structural induction. Thus this is again a C action model, with literal identity and the original H-theoremhood restriction on Logical Equivalence. No closure under every externally supported relation is required of its old Henkin domains.

The distinguished sentence remains true. Performing this reduction separately for each consistent pure sentence gives a countable complete family with countably many total semantic objects and arrows and countable typed fibers.

2. The cover and its proposition cardinality

Apply the construction in Interpretation cover: surjective chain names, §§1–8 to this family. At each type use C_\sigma=\{\sigma\}\times\mathbb N\times\mathbb N. The root loops preserve chains and act on each chain by nondecreasing surjections; retain all typed interpretations into every old object. The root domains are the finite-pin profiles whose whole sections at old targets land in the unchanged old domains. Its construction proves term closure, C, current all-type BF, and the named-lift property. Denote this root by K.

Let \mathfrak c=2^{\aleph_0}. For a finite typed pin S, a whole proposition is determined at a root loop by the tuple of name values on S, and at an old interpretation by its target object and its assignment on S. There are only countably many such patterns: S is finite, all relevant names and old fibers are countable, and the old objects form a countable set. An S-pinned proposition is therefore a bit table on a countable set, giving at most \mathfrak c possibilities. Whole-old-domain landing only restricts these tables. There are countably many finite pins, so

|D_t(K)|\le\mathfrak c.

This counts whole propositions, including their future rows. It neither bounds all higher-type domains nor assumes the interpretation arrows themselves form a countable set. Their finite-pin patterns are what have been counted.

3. The full-current root and its signature

Take K as one component and terminal full singleton-individual models T_n for n\in\mathbb N as the others. Add a root R with no return from a component, with D_e(R)=C_e, identity individual entrance into K, and the unique individual entrance into each T_n. At a flattened relational type admit

D_{\sigma_1\cdots\sigma_k t}(R)= \mathcal P\!\left(\prod_j D_{\sigma_j}(R)\right) \times D_{\sigma_1\cdots\sigma_k t}(K) \times\prod_nD_{\sigma_1\cdots\sigma_k t}(T_n).

The first coordinate is the unrestricted current extension; the remaining coordinates are entire admitted old entities. Application uses current membership and component application. Transport is projection followed by old transport. Quantification uses ordinary current quantification and the original component quantifiers. A lambda abstraction has the current extension defined by its formula and its component denotations in the old domains. Induction on terms therefore gives closure, naturality and the original C equations, including Logical Equivalence with its H-theoremhood side condition. Equality is literal identity at the current root. This is the full-current coalesced construction; every old future and old typed domain remains unchanged.

For countable \Sigma, inject its constants typewise into the auxiliary names of K. Their named lifts are the denotations at K. Interpret individual constants at R by the same names; give a relational constant its K denotation as its K coordinate, any current row and arbitrary terminal coordinates. Every constant projects correctly.

To realize a consistent closed signature sentence, replace its finitely many constants by variables and existentially close. Free constants have no nonlogical or unique-name axioms, so the resulting pure sentence is consistent. A complete old component supplies its witnesses. An interpretation assigns these whole typed witnesses to the designated names, proving signature Max at K and hence at R.

For signature Possibility+, fix a current individual tuple satisfying the native distinctness and freshness guard for P. Its names are distinct and disjoint from the individual constant names occurring in P. A pure old realization of the existential closure of P supplies values for its constant slots and its displayed variable slots. Assign them to those names and extend to a total typed interpretation. Relational constant names receive their entire old values; no free relational variables are allowed by this schema. This realizes P in the full old target domains. The identity individual entrance R\to K preserves the freshness guard, so the same proof holds at R. Distinct old values are not required for distinct names.

4. Current BF survives the product

Each entrance object satisfies current all-type BF: this is the cover theorem for K and immediate at a terminal model. Each individual entrance is onto. Each relational entrance projection is onto as well, since a prescribed whole component entity can be paired with any current row and arbitrary other coordinates.

Use dual BF. If \Diamond\exists x^\sigma Fx holds at R, a current witness already gives \exists x\,\Diamond Fx. Otherwise some component K_i satisfies \Diamond\exists x\,F_i x. Its current BF supplies a current whole entity b and a future witnessing F_i of its transport. Onto projection supplies a root a mapping to b. The composite future witnesses Fa. This covers every admitted type, including e, and every future quantified entity. It does not assume later transports or their composites are onto; the component’s BF handles that part.

5. Strict Forcing Possibilism

The whole root proposition algebra is

B_R=2\times B_K\times2^{\mathbb N}.

It has cardinality exactly \mathfrak c: the upper bound follows from §2 and the last factor supplies the lower bound. Each isolated terminal coordinate is a Boolean atom, so there are infinitely many distinct atoms.

Full current rows externalize all the native tests at every carrier: every external subset of a proposed Boolean algebra is admitted, and all graphs needed for injections, Dedekind finiteness and mixed-type isomorphisms are admitted. In ZFC every infinite complete Boolean algebra has at least \mathfrak c elements. Indeed choose a countable disjoint sequence of nonzero elements (infinitely many atoms, or successive splittings in an atomless part); the joins indexed by all subsets of that sequence are distinct. Hence no infinite complete Boolean algebra is strictly smaller than B_R.

An eligible algebra in strict FP is consequently finite. If it has m atoms, choose m of the displayed atoms of B_R and let p be their join. A further displayed atom ensures p\ne\top, and the interval below p is isomorphic to the given finite Boolean algebra. Its isomorphism graph is admitted by full current rows. The degenerate one-element algebra uses p=\bot. This proves the exact finite-or-strictly-smaller version of FP, also at individual carriers. It makes no completeness assertion about B_R itself.

6. Further current properties of the same root

Let w be true only at R, with bottom in every component coordinate. It is below every current truth, so witnesses Actuality. There is no return to R; therefore \Diamond w=w as a whole proposition. For every current truth p, q=w witnesses \exists q(q\land\Box(\Diamond q\to p)), giving Truths are ND-necessary. This checks all futures inside K, not just its entrance.

For Transversal Choice, apply external choice to the equivalence classes of any current admitted equivalence relation on whole root entities. The selected set is an admitted current row with bottom old coordinates. It selects exactly one literal entity from each class, at every carrier.

For quantified Infinity at e, write Z_n for the whole root denotation of \operatorname{Suc}_e^n(\mathbf0_e). The set \{Z_n:n\in\mathbb N\} is the current extension of an admitted predicate W with bottom old coordinates. It is inductive, so the impredicative finite-cardinality predicate holds only of these standard numeral objects. Finite induction proves it holds of all of them. A second induction using arbitrary current properties and the admitted deleted-member term shows Z_n(X) exactly when X has n current members. The universal individual property has infinitely many members, so no finite cardinality applies to it. This is the quantified Infinity axiom. The map also derives it from signature Max and current BF; no duplicate argument flag is needed.

7. Arbitrary set-sized free signatures

Use a many-sorted first-order Henkin presentation with one nonempty sort per admitted type, application, logical operations, current truth T, and an operation for every finite parameterized pure lambda template. Terms containing signature constants are represented by substituting them as parameters into these templates. Include conversion and interpretation clauses, current quantifier truth, and T(\operatorname{Eq}_\sigma(x,y))\leftrightarrow x=y. Propositions remain whole entities, distinct from their current truth values. Do not impose extensionality from current coextension.

Include the translations of all C(\Sigma) instances. Logical Equivalence instances are selected externally by H theoremhood, never equivalence under optional packages. Add every instance of the eight principles stated above. Each is a finite first-order sentence at each fixed type or syntax instance. FP’s completeness, cardinality and isomorphism tests quantify over the admitted sorts; they are not external powerset quantifiers in this presentation.

A finite fragment mentions only finitely many signature constants. Put them in a countable free signature, adding fresh individual names as needed. Free signature extension is conservative: models expand by arbitrary choices in nonempty sorts, and a finite proof’s extra constants can be replaced by fresh variables after avoiding capture. The parameterized-template axioms already hold under all assignments. Thus the H-theoremhood, C-theoremhood and consistency side conditions selecting the fragment’s Max and Possibility+ instances stay the same. For an open P, consistency is that of an assignment, equivalently its existential closure, not its universal closure.

Sections 1–6 give a model of the entire countable-signature package and hence of the fragment. Unused constants can be interpreted arbitrarily. Many-sorted first-order compactness gives a model for the entire set-sized signature. The Max reference theory remains C(\Sigma), without the optional current packages. The final model satisfies those packages because their native formulas were expressly included. Its cardinality, current fullness and standard arithmetic need not match the finite-fragment witnesses.
