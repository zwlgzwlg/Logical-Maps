# Possibly Witnessed Possibility ⇒ Witnessed Possibility

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.possibly_witnessed_possibility_r_implies_witnessed_possibility_r`; produced by Cian Dorr, suggestion of 22 September 2026; proof written out by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 22 September 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 22 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Possibly Witnessed Possibility.** For pure formulas P with free variables among a finite tuple x and distinct matching constants c, a possible witness to P entails that P at those constants is possible. Equivalently, P necessary at the constants is necessary universally.

## Conclusion

- **Witnessed Possibility.** For pure-formulas P with free variables among a finite tuple x, and distinct matching nonlogical constants c, a witness to P entails that P at those constants is possible.

## Proof

By T, $\exists\bar x\, .\,P\to\Diamond\exists\bar x\, .\,P$; compose with the principle.
