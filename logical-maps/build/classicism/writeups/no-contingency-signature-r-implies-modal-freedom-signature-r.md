# No Contingency (signature Σ) ⇒ Modal Freedom (signature Σ)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.no_contingency_signature_r_implies_modal_freedom_signature_r`; produced by Cian Dorr, suggestion of 22 September 2026; proof written out by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 22 September 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 22 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **No Contingency (signature Σ).** Each closed sentence of the language of the fixed signature Sigma, if true, is necessary. A sentence schema, standing to No Pure Contingency as Possibility Maximalism (signature Σ) stands to Possibility Maximalism (pure).

## Conclusion

- **Modal Freedom (signature Σ).** For pure formulas P and Q with free variables among the disjoint tuples x and y, and distinct constants c and d of matching types, if P is possible at c and Q is possible at d then P and Q are jointly possible there.

## Proof

Applying the schema to the closed sentences $\neg P[\bar c/\bar x]$ and $\neg Q[\bar d/\bar y]$ and contraposing gives $\Diamond P[\bar c/\bar x]\to P[\bar c/\bar x]$ and likewise for $Q$. So the antecedent of Modal Freedom (signature Σ) yields $P[\bar c/\bar x]\land Q[\bar d/\bar y]$, and T gives its possibility.

## Notes

The signature analogue of the equivalence between the empty-constant instance of Modal Freedom and No Pure Contingency.
