# Possible Infinity (type t) ∧ No Pure Contingency ⇒ Axiom of Infinity (type t)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.possible_infinity_t_and_no_pure_contingency_imply_axiom_of_infinity_t`; produced by Cian Dorr, suggestion of 20 September 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 20 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Possible Infinity (type t).** Possibly there are not finitely many propositions: the Axiom of Infinity at this type, under a diamond.
- **No Pure Contingency.** Each closed sentence of the pure language, if true, is necessary. This is a sentence schema, not a quantifier over propositions.

## Conclusion

- **Axiom of Infinity (type t).** There are not finitely many propositions. No finite cardinality holds of the universal property, so the domain of the type cannot be exhausted by finitely many steps from the empty property.

## Proof

The negation of the Axiom of Infinity at type $t$ is a closed sentence of the pure language. If the Axiom failed, No Pure Contingency would make its negation necessary, contradicting Possible Infinity at type $t$, which is the Axiom's diamond.
