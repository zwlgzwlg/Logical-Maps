# Maximalism (pure) ∧ Separated Structure ⇒ Maximalism (signature Σ)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Classicism (2024); produced by Andrew Bacon and Cian Dorr; recorded by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 17 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Maximalism (pure).** Every closed pure identity not provable in C is false. The non-theorem condition is metalinguistic.
- **Separated Structure.** For each typed nonlogical constant c and closed same-typed terms F,G not containing c, equality after application to c implies equality of F and G.

## Conclusion

- **Maximalism (signature Σ).** The Maximalism schema for the fixed nonlogical signature Σ: every closed identity of its language not provable in C(Σ) is false.

## Proof

Iterating Separated Structure gives the corresponding conditional for a finite tuple of distinct constants. Abstract the constants occurring in a closed A,B to write them as iterated applications of pure closed F,G. If A=B is not a theorem, F=G cannot be a theorem either. Pure Distinctness Maximalism gives F≠G; contraposition of the iterated Separated Structure conditional gives A≠B.
