# □Gallin Extensional Comprehension ⇒ □Rigid Comprehension

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 20 September 2026, from Cian Dorr's suggestion of the same day; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 20 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Gallin Extensional Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation is coextensive with one that is persistent and has a persistent pointwise negation.

## Conclusion

- **□Rigid Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a rigid one.

## Proof

The boxed premise gives □ND, by necessary-gallin-comprehension-implies-necessary-nd, and □ND gives □BF, by necessary-distinctness-necessary-r-implies-necessary-barcan-r. By gallin-comprehension-and-bf-imply-rigid-comprehension, each closed instance of Rigid Comprehension follows in C from the corresponding closed instances of Gallin Extensional Comprehension and of BF, so the conditional is a theorem of C and necessitates; K then needs the boxes of the antecedents, which the boxed premise and □BF supply. Hence every closed instance of Rigid Comprehension is necessary.

## Notes

No BF premise is needed at all in the boxed setting: the box of Gallin Extensional Comprehension already yields □ND, hence □BF. The unboxed arrow needs BF as a premise because Gallin Extensional Comprehension alone gives only ND, not its box.
