# Finite-height independence: omit height 1

## Package

- **Finite-Type Necessitism: height 0.** The exact height-0 fragment of Finite-Type Necessitism, at every carrier type. A possible Peano presentation has a rigid realization at the same type, with its full rigid represented power.
- **Finite-Type Necessitism: height 2.** The exact height-2 fragment of Finite-Type Necessitism, at every carrier type. A possible presentation of numbers, subset codes and codes for subsets of those codes has such a rigid realization, with a full rigid power of its last carrier.
- **¬ Analytic Necessitism.** If there could be a real number structure, there is a rigid one that is necessarily a real number structure: a rigid property with necessarily distinct instances, with rigid order, addition, multiplication and natural numbers, which necessarily form a complete ordered field. One sentence for each type $\sigma$.

## Definition

Use the full two-point model of Finite-Type Necessitism, Sections 6.1–6.2, with A⊆B, |A|=aleph_0 and |B|=beth_1, evaluated at the root. Individual transport is inclusion; relational domains, paired-profile operations and full quantifiers are exactly equations (6.1)–(6.3). The common construction is given once in the linked finite-type guide and in the paper.

## Arguments

### Holds Finite-Type Necessitism: height 0, Finite-Type Necessitism: height 2. Fails Analytic Necessitism.

Theorem 6.1 with j=1 gives every exact height except 1: the individual-type capacity is the only obstruction; every relational-type instance holds by the projection-section argument. F0 and F2 hold. A real field on individuals is possible at the terminal point but cannot fit into the full countable root domain, so Analytic Necessitism fails (Corollary 6.2).
