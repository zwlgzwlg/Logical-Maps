Vicinity implies 5-necessities are two-hat necessary

Conjectured by Cian Dorr, 14 October 2026. Proved by Christopher Sun, in a handwritten argument sent to Cian Dorr on 19 October 2026: he showed that if there is a strongest 5-necessary proposition, every 5-necessary proposition is two-hat necessary, and, from the 5-necessity form of Vicinity, the latter is his argument recorded on the page of Vicinity. The passage from that form to a strongest 5-necessary proposition (Step 1) was added by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr when recording it, 19 October 2026.

Facts used

All are theorems of C (Background, Hats, two-hat necessity and 5-necessity):

  • \Box\le\Box_{\ne}\le\hat{\hat\Box}\le\Box_5\le\lambda p\,.\,p, the order being necessary pointwise implication. Dually \Diamond_5\le\Diamond and \Diamond_{\ne}\le\Diamond.
  • \Box_5 is an S5 operator: N, K, T, 4 and B. So it also obeys the Barcan formula for propositional quantification, by Prior’s derivation from B and the converse Barcan formula.
  • \Diamond_{\ne}x\leftrightarrow\forall r\,.\,r\to\Diamond(\Diamond r\land x), the dual of \Box_{\ne}p:=\exists q\,.\,q\land\Box(\Diamond q\to p); it is monotone under \le.
  • \hat{\hat\Box} is the hat of \Box_{\ne}: \hat{\hat\Box}p\leftrightarrow\exists q\,.\,q\land\Box(\Diamond_{\ne}q\to p).
  • Theorem D9 of The Bounds of Possibility, Appendix D: \Box_5 materially implies every operator that is \mathrm{NKJF}_{\langle\rangle}. Here \mathrm N X says \Box\le X, \mathrm K is the K axiom, \mathrm J X:=\forall p\,.\,p\to X\Diamond p, and \mathrm F_{\langle\rangle}X:=\forall Y^{tt}\,.\,(\forall z\,.\,X(Yz))\to X(\forall z\,.\,Yz). The definition of \Box_5 there is necessarily equivalent to the map’s.

Step 1: a strongest 5-necessary proposition

By the 5-necessity form of Vicinity (on the page of Vicinity), let t be a truth with \forall q\,.\,\Box_5 q\to t\le q, and put s:=\Diamond_5 t.

  1. s is true, as t is (T for \Box_5).

  2. \Box_5 s, by B for \Box_5.

  3. If \Box_5 q then s\le q. By 4, \Box_5\Box_5 q, so t\le\Box_5 q, that is \Box(t\to\Box_5 q). Let s hold at a world. There \Box(t\to\Box_5 q) still holds, by 4, so \Box_5(t\to\Box_5 q), as \Box\le\Box_5. With \Diamond_5 t this gives \Diamond_5\Box_5 q, and so q, by B for \Box_5.

  4. If s\le q then \Box_5 q, by (b), \Box\le\Box_5 and K.

So \forall q\,.\,\Box_5 q\leftrightarrow s\le q: in Sun’s terms, 5-necessity is principal.

Step 2 (Sun): \Box(\Diamond_{\ne}s\to s)

Suppose otherwise, put A:=\Diamond_{\ne}s\land\neg s, so that \Diamond A, and let O:=\lambda p\,.\,A\le p.

  • \Box_5 s, but not O s, since \Diamond(A\land\neg s). So \Box_5 does not materially imply O, and by Theorem D9, O is not \mathrm{NKJF}_{\langle\rangle}.
  • O is N and K, as \le is necessary implication.
  • O is J. Let q be true. Then \Box_5\Diamond_5 q (B), so \Box_5\Diamond q; by (c), s\le\Diamond q; so \Diamond s\le\Diamond q by 4; and A\le\Diamond_{\ne}s\le\Diamond s. So A\le\Diamond q.
  • So O is not \mathrm F_{\langle\rangle}: there is an X^{tt} with \forall p\,.\,A\le Xp and \Diamond\exists q\,.\,A\land\neg Xq.

Each A\le Xp is necessary by 4, hence 5-necessary, so the Barcan formula for \Box_5 gives \Box_5 T, where T:=\forall p\,.\,A\le Xp. By (c), s\le T, and so \Diamond_{\ne}s\le\Diamond_{\ne}T.

Now take a world, and a q, with A\land\neg Xq. There \Diamond_{\ne}s holds, hence \Diamond_{\ne}T; applying the characterization of \Diamond_{\ne} to the truth r:=A\land\neg Xq, that world sees one where \Diamond(A\land\neg Xq) and T hold. But T gives A\le Xq there, that is \Box(A\to Xq): a contradiction.

Step 3: 5-necessities are two-hat necessary

s is true and \Box(\Diamond_{\ne}s\to s). If \Box_5 p, then s\le p by (c), so \Box(\Diamond_{\ne}s\to p), and s witnesses \hat{\hat\Box}p. \blacksquare

Remarks

  • The argument is a derivation in C from Vicinity, so its necessitation gives the boxed form, □Vicinity implies □5-necessities are two-hat necessary.
  • Actuality, and Vicinity with BF at type t, were already known to suffice. As the notes of the conjecture explain, the Actuality proof does not adapt; Sun’s argument works through Theorem D9 instead of an atom.
  • Sun’s first page derives a strongest 5-necessary proposition from a strongest \Box_{\ne}-necessary one, by a different route: s:=v\land\forall p\,.\,p\to v\le\Diamond p. Step 1 above needs only Vicinity.
