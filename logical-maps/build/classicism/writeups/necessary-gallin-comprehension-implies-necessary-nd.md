# □Gallin Extensional Comprehension ⇒ □ND

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.necessary_gallin_comprehension_implies_necessary_nd`; produced by Cian Dorr, suggestion of 20 September 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 20 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Gallin Extensional Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation is coextensive with one that is persistent and has a persistent pointwise negation.

## Conclusion

- **□ND.** Necessarily, distinct things of any type are necessarily distinct.

## Proof

The recorded proof of gallin-comprehension-implies-nd derives each closed instance of ND at a type $\sigma$ from the closed instance of Gallin Extensional Comprehension at $\lambda x^\sigma\, .\,x=a$, using only theorems of C, so the conditional from that instance to the ND instance is a theorem of C. C is closed under necessitation, and K carries the box across the conditional; the boxed premise supplies the box of the antecedent. Hence every closed instance of ND is necessary.

## Notes

The boxed premise is what makes the argument available at every world; the unboxed principle gives ND itself.
