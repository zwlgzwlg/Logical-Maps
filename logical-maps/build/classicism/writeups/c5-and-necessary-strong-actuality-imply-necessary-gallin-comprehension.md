# □ND ∧ □Strong Actuality ⇒ □Gallin Extensional Comprehension

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 11 October 2026, from a suggestion of Cian Dorr; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 11 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□ND.** Necessarily, distinct things of any type are necessarily distinct.
- **□Strong Actuality.** Necessarily, there is a true strong world proposition: a true proposition that necessarily entails every proposition or its negation.

## Conclusion

- **□Gallin Extensional Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation is coextensive with one that is persistent and has a persistent pointwise negation.

## Proof

The argument of nd-and-strong-actuality-imply-gallin-comprehension is a C-proof of each instance of Gallin Extensional Comprehension from ND and Strong Actuality. Necessitate it and apply K with the boxed premises.
