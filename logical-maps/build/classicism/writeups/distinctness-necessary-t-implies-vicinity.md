# ND (type t) ⇒ Vicinity

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.distinctness_necessary_t_implies_vicinity`; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 27 September 2026; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 27 September 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **ND (type t).** ND (type t) in the displayed closed propositional formulation.

## Conclusion

- **Vicinity.** There is a true proposition that entails the possibility of each true proposition.

## Proof

In C, $\Diamond q$ is $q\ne\bot$. Let $q$ be true. By T, $q\ne\bot$, so ND at type $t$ gives $\Box(q\ne\bot)$, i.e. $\Box\Diamond q$, which is $\top\le\Diamond q$. So $\top$ witnesses Vicinity.

## Notes

With Vicinity and Weakly Inextensible Comprehension ⇒ Actuality, this gives Actuality from ND (type $t$) and Weakly Inextensible Comprehension, in particular under C5.
