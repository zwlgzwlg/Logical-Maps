# Finite-Type Necessitism: height 0 ⇒ Arithmetic Necessitism

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Kodsi, Finite-Type Necessitism (2026); produced by Daniel Kodsi, with assistance from GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI); recorded by ChatGPT, at Daniel Kodsi’s request.</p>

## Premises

- **Finite-Type Necessitism: height 0.** The exact height-0 fragment of Finite-Type Necessitism, at every carrier type. A possible Peano presentation has a rigid realization at the same type, with its full rigid represented power.

## Conclusion

- **Arithmetic Necessitism.** Given that necessarily zero is not a successor and successor is injective on numbers, the numbers at $\sigma$ form a rigid property with necessarily distinct instances. One sentence for each type $\sigma$.

## Proof

Under the native necessary canonical-arithmetic guard, apply F0 to its Peano presentation. The top represented power makes the rigid reference necessarily Peano. Peano matching transfers rigidity and necessary distinctness to the canonical numbers. [Paper (PDF)](https://github.com/dkodsi/Logical-Maps/blob/classicism-next-batch-2026-10-30/logical-maps/topics/classicism/papers/finite-type-necessitism.pdf); [source](https://github.com/dkodsi/Logical-Maps/blob/classicism-next-batch-2026-10-30/logical-maps/topics/classicism/papers/finite-type-necessitism.tex).
