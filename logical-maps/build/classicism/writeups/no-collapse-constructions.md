# No Collapse constructions

These notes retain three results: consistency of ordinary No Collapse; consistency of necessary Extensional No Collapse; and consistency of pure Maximalism together with current Extensional No Collapse, under the stated action-model completeness input. They add no map nodes or model verdicts.

The [ordinary No Collapse argument](no-collapse-finite-depth-and-fanout.md) gives the finite-depth and fanout construction. The [one-object construction](extensional-no-collapse-one-object-route.md) gives the stronger necessary extensional result. The [gluing argument](max-current-enc-gluing-gate.md) gives the pure-Maximalism conjunction and its separate incompatibility with necessary Extensional No Collapse. The gluing result does not assert signature Maximalism or necessary Extensional No Collapse at the glued root.
