# Pure Maximalism with current Extensional No Collapse

## 1. Statement

In the same external ZFC/action-model completeness metatheory as the reviewed Max interpretation covers, there is a C model whose root satisfies **pure Maximalism and current Extensional No Collapse** simultaneously.

The root is the one-object leaf set Z with monoid M=product_d End(B_d). Arbitrary old C-complete Henkin/action components are attached below it, with no arrows back. The old components and all their higher domains remain unchanged. The new root domains are rebuilt with finite individual pinning and whole old landing; they are not asserted to equal the domains of the isolated Z model.

The result is current. It does NOT give necessary ENC with Max. A one-world full extensional C model falsifies ENC (Box and every hat are the identity operator), so pure Max itself requires a possible failure of ENC and hence refutes necessary ENC. No signature-Max, BF, BC, Choice, Strong Possibility, or new negative primitive flag is proposed.

## 2. The category and recursive domains

Choose a set-sized family of old C action models complete for closed pure C-consistent sentences. Make their categories disjoint and preserve each entire model: objects, outgoing arrows, typed whole domains, transports, designated symmetries, and evaluations. Such a family is the same input used in the source interpretation-root construction. No uniform bound, fullness, standard arithmetic, or surjective transport is required of the old models.

Adjoin the new root Z. Its endomorphisms are exactly M from the accepted one-object construction. For every old object V and EVERY function f:Z->D_e(V), add an arrow `(V,f):Z->V`. For h in M define `(V,f)h=(V,fh)`. For an old arrow k:V->W define `k(V,f)=(W,k_e f)`, where k_e is its individual transport. All these arrows exist because all individual maps are included. Composition is associative and has the specified identities. There are no old-to-root arrows and no arrows between different old components.

Only individual maps and pins are used. We introduce no auxiliary relational names and claim no ability to lift arbitrary old higher-type entities to the root. Distinct old postcompositions can give the same individual-map arrow. That creates compatibility requirements on the admitted root profiles, not an enlargement of any old domain; whole old landing below is essential.

Put D_e(Z)=Z. Recursively for relational tau=sigma_1...sigma_k t, a candidate root entity A assigns every outgoing arrow a:Z->V a row

```text
A_a subset D_sigma_1(V) x ... x D_sigma_k(V).
```

At root targets these argument domains are the previously constructed lower-type GLOBAL root domains; at old targets use their existing full old domains. The arrow set is independent of this recursion, so the definition is well founded. Admit precisely profiles satisfying both conditions:

1. **Finite individual pin.** Some finite S subset Z has the property that a,b with the same target and the same individual map on S have A_a=A_b. This applies to all root loops, including the identity, and separately to the maps into each old object.
2. **Whole old landing.** For every f:Z->D_e(V), the ENTIRE old-future profile `k |-> A_(k f)` is an element of the unchanged D_tau(V), including all its old symmetry/admission requirements.

There is no additional root symmetry condition. For a root h, set `(rho_h A)_a=A_(a h)`. The finite set h[S] pins rho_h A. Its old section at f is the already admitted section of A at fh, so it lands. Transport along an old f is the old entity required by landing. Subsequent old transport agrees with reindexing. The individual transports and all these relational transports are functorial.

Root-only Boolean profiles are particularly simple: any finite-pinned subset p of M, extended by FALSE at every old arrow, is admitted at t. All its old sections are the old whole bottom proposition. This gives every finite-pinned root-loop truth set without assuming arbitrary higher-type old lifts.

## 3. C closure with full target quantifiers

This is the same whole-environment verification as Dorr's interpretation cover and the reviewed surjective-name cover, restricted to individual pins. No onto lemma from those constructions is used.

Suppose a,b from Z have the same target and agree on a pin S of A. For every further arrow k from that target, ka and kb still agree on S, since the individual transports are functions. Their A rows therefore agree. Consequently `rho_a A=rho_b A` as WHOLE entities at the common target. For an individual parameter z, including z in S directly ensures equal transported values. This remains true when old arrows are identified by equal composite individual maps: every admitted parameter has coherent whole old sections by definition.

Given a term with finitely many free root parameters, let S contain the pins of all relational parameters and the individual parameters themselves. Evaluate its prospective profile row at each a in the target with the whole transported environment. If a,b agree on S, these entire environments coincide, so the rows agree. Bound variables in a lambda or quantifier range over the FULL target domains, including arbitrary old entities that are not transported root parameters. The equality of environments holds independently of such bound arguments, and therefore proves finite pinning of the term. Modal evaluation uses every further arrow and the same transported whole environment, so it is covered too.

For any old f, structural induction on the term shows that the entire section over kf is exactly the old term denotation under rho_f of the free parameters. The old model is already C, so this denotation belongs to its unchanged type domain. This is a whole-entity landing proof, not just agreement of current extensions. In particular neither an external root subset nor the new root's quantifier range enters an old quantifier.

Application, Boolean operations, and abstraction therefore preserve admission. The usual profile proofs of beta and eta apply. For Logical Equivalence, use only an actual H-theorem equivalence: the abstractions have equal extensions at every row and every full argument tuple, hence are the same root profile; old validity is inherited. No new optional principle or root-only hypothesis is used to license an identity.

Current Leibniz identity agrees with literal root entity identity: for any root entity x, the profile with constant root-loop row {x} and empty old rows is an admitted singleton predicate. It is empty-pinned and has whole old bottom sections. Such predicates distinguish different root individuals or profiles. Old logical identity remains the unchanged old identity. Transport preserves identities by functoriality.

These checks establish the mixed C action model under the same action-model soundness/representation assumptions as the prior cover proofs. The full-category finite-pin theorem alone would not suffice: the additional old-landing calculation is indispensable and has been supplied.

## 4. Endpoint facts and the root-only cylinder basis

Write `O_0=Box`, `O_(n+1)=hat O_n`, and `D_n(q)=not O_n(not q)`. At EVERY point of any of the C action models under discussion,

```text
O_n(top)=top,       O_n(bottom)=bottom,       O_n(p) -> p.    (E)
```

These facts are for the native iterates, not an arbitrary operator X. Box has them. Any hat sends top to top, taking q=top as its true witness. If O_n is factive and q is currently true, then O_n(not q) is false, so D_n(q) is currently true. A current witness to `hat O_n(p)` includes `Box(D_n(q)->p)`; its identity instance forces p. Thus the next hat is factive and sends bottom to bottom. This proves (E) by induction, with the statements valid throughout the old models and the new root. In particular `D_n(bottom)=bottom` everywhere.

For finite S subset Z define U_S to be true at root loops h fixing S and false at ALL old targets. It is S-pinned, true at the root, and has bottom old sections, hence is admitted. Every admitted current true proposition q contains U_S in the WHOLE proposition order for some pin S of q: loops fixing S agree with the identity, and U_S is false on the old branches. The native iterates and their duals are monotone; for hats this follows directly from their implication-consequent definition, and for Box it is the ordinary modal clause. Thus a true witness q in a hat can be replaced by such a U_S.

For any old f, `rho_f U_S=bottom` as an entire old entity. By (E), D_n(rho_f U_S) is bottom. Therefore old arrows impose no constraint in `Box(D_n(U_S)->p)`, for ANY p. This is the precise reason they disappear from positive hats; it is not because old target domains have been restricted.

## 5. Exact positive-hat transfer; the O_0 exception

Let R_n^M(S) be the self-return game of the accepted isolated Z monoid:

```text
R_0^M(S)=M;
h in R_(n+1)^M(S) iff every finite T subset Z has k in R_n^M(T)
                                      with kh fixing S.      (R)
```

In the glued model, for every admitted root proposition p and EVERY n>=1,

```text
O_n(p) is currently true iff some finite S has
                         R_n^M(S) subset {h in M : p_h}.     (H+)
```

The analogous all-proposition statement for n=0 is FALSE: ordinary Box at the glued root also sees all old arrows. We do not assert it.

Here is the corrected base. For a root h, rho_h U_S has false old rows. Hence

```text
D_0(rho_h U_S)=not Box(not rho_h U_S)
```

is true exactly when some ROOT loop k satisfies kh fixing S. Old arrows cannot witness that possibility. Since R_0^M(T)=M is independent of T, this condition is equivalent to `h in R_1^M(S)`. At old arrows the corresponding D_0 is false by (E). Replacing true q by U_S in the hat definition now proves (H+) at n=1.

Suppose (H+) holds at n>=1. For root h, apply it to the arbitrary admitted proposition `not rho_h U_S`. The negation of that condition says

```text
for every finite T, some k in R_n^M(T) has (rho_h U_S)_k true,
```

which is exactly the return clause `h in R_(n+1)^M(S)`. At every old f, D_n(rho_f U_S) remains false. The hat definition and the same cylinder basis therefore give (H+) at n+1. This proves the full induction.

All quantifiers over arbitrary admitted root propositions in this reasoning are handled by their actual finite pins; all old quantifiers are handled by the old C semantics and the endpoint fact. No general factorization of higher domains, BF pullback, or surjectivity is asserted. The raw game is the same individual-map game as before, even though the actual operator entities now have additional old sections.

## 6. Current separators survive, including the initial step

For n>=1, use exactly the finite-pinned loop predicate from the accepted one-object proof:

```text
p_n(h) iff h in R_(n+1)^M(S_0),        for h in M,
p_n(f)=false,                         for old-target f.
```

For n=2j+1 choose S_0={a} in a finite-parameter top node U_j(m) of coordinate B_(j+1). For n=2j+2 choose it in an infinite-parameter top node of that coordinate. The capacity table makes these root rows depend only on h(a); bottom old sections establish admission. The already reviewed finite-trace maps, m to m+1 at the odd step and infinity to a sufficiently large finite parameter at the even step, give for every finite T a root loop in R_n^M(T) falsifying p_n. Conversely R_(n+1)^M(S_0) is contained in p_n by definition. Equation (H+) therefore yields

```text
not O_n(p_n),           O_(n+1)(p_n)                    (n>=1).
```

For n=0 choose distinct a,b in a size-two B_1 block. Let p_0 test h(a)!=h(b) on root loops and be false on old arrows. It is admitted. R_1^M({a,b}) is contained in its true root loops, so (H+) gives O_1(p_0). A total root collapse of that coordinate falsifies p_0, so ordinary Box p_0 is false even without using an old branch. Thus the initial adjacent separation survives as well, without the invalid O_0 transfer claim.

These are current truth-value differences on admitted propositions at the single glued root, for every external n. The operators are the actual glued-model closed terms O_n, not borrowed entities from the isolated model.

## 7. The family lands in arbitrary old Henkin domains

Let `Q={O_n^Z : n an external natural}` be the set of WHOLE glued-root operator entities. Define a profile F of type (tt)t by

```text
F_h = Q                  for every root loop h;
F_f = D_tt(V)            for every f:Z->D_e(V).
```

This profile is empty-pinned: root rows are the same set Q, and old rows depend only on the target. At old f, its ENTIRE section is the universal property of operators at every old future. That is the old admitted closed top entity of type (tt)t. Therefore F satisfies whole old landing even if the external set of hat iterates at V is not an admitted old family. We do not ask that set to be admitted there.

At the root, F contains Box. Each current member X is literally some glued-root O_n, so its hat is O_(n+1) and again belongs. Its p_n from section 6 witnesses current noncoextension with its hat. These statements quantify over the full root D_tt and D_t. Q is formed only after D_tt exists; there is no internal enumeration, standard-number assumption, or circular domain definition.

The old universal rows can contain fixed operators and need not satisfy any of these family clauses. That is allowed: ENC's clauses are current. Closed O_n terms are fixed by root endomorphisms by naturality, but this does not force the chosen F to have the same selected-member interpretation at old targets. No necessary ENC claim has slipped into the family definition.

Hence this admitted F witnesses the exact current ENC formula in the glued model.

## 8. Pure Maximalism

For every C-consistent closed PURE sentence phi, choose its witnessing old object V from the complete family. Since D_e(V) is nonempty, at least one f:Z->D_e(V) exists, and every such f is an outgoing arrow. Evaluation of phi along f is the unchanged old evaluation: it is closed and pure, and closure/landing established the usual term transport. Thus phi is possible at Z. This is pure Max in its native possibility form and hence, by the recorded equivalence in C, its official distinctness form. The reference theory remains C, not C augmented by ENC.

The argument combines the current capacity separators with the finite-pin, whole-landing interpretation-cover construction. Individual-only maps suffice because no named relational witnesses, signature assignments, or BF are requested. The arbitrary old domains create no remaining closure gap under the stated admission definition.

The conclusion is **pure Max + current ENC**. The distinct all-one-object theorem still gives necessary ENC when no C-complete old branches are attached; that stronger scope is intentionally not transferred here.
