# No Pure Contingency ∧ BF (relational types) ⇒ □BF (relational types)

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 11 October 2026, from a suggestion of Cian Dorr; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 11 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **No Pure Contingency.** Each closed sentence of the pure language, if true, is necessary. This is a sentence schema, not a quantifier over propositions.
- **BF (relational types).** The Barcan Formula at every relational type, that is, at every type other than e.

## Conclusion

- **□BF (relational types).** Necessarily, BF holds at every relational type.

## Proof

Every closed instance of BF (relational types) is a closed sentence of the pure language. Given the principle, each is true, and No Pure Contingency makes a true closed pure sentence necessary.

## Notes

One of the uniform family by which No Pure Contingency carries a pure principle to its boxed form.
