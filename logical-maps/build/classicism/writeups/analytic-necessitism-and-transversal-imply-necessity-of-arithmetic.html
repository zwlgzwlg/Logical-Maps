Analytic Necessitism and Transversal give Necessity of Arithmetic

GPT-6 Astra, prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 30 October 2026. The proof combines Current Transversal supplies a literal real field, §§7–8, and Nonvacuous Analytic Necessitism forces Arithmetic Necessitism, “Arithmetical truth corollary” (both unpaginated). Goodsell’s arithmetic guard lemma and the given-field stability proof of Claude Fable 5.1, prompted by Zachary Goodsell, are included below with their original roles preserved.

1. Exact target and the current field-existence lemma

Work in C. Assume the current Analytic Necessitism and Transversal schemas. Fix an external counting type \sigma and a native arithmetical sentence A_\sigma. Put

\nu=(\sigma t)t,\qquad \rho=\nu t,\qquad I_\sigma=\Diamond I^*_\sigma.

The target is exactly

\Box(I_\sigma\to A_\sigma)\ \lor\ \Box(I_\sigma\to\neg A_\sigma). \tag{1}

It does not assume \Box I^*_\sigma. Types and arithmetic sentences are external schema indices.

The current cut construction in [[analytic-necessitism-and-transversal-imply-arithmetic-necessitism]], §§1–3, proves the discharged C theorem

I^*_\sigma\land\mathrm{Tr}_{\nu} \ \longrightarrow\ \exists\mathfrak R\, \operatorname{Real}_{\rho}(\mathfrak R). \tag{2}

It uses one current Transversal witness, of type (\nu t)t, selecting literal representatives of admitted rational cuts. Its relational field operations and its least-upper-bound proof cover every admitted bounded family. No future correctness of the selector is needed. We use (2) only at the current evaluation point.

2. The arithmetic guard facts are theorems of C

Goodsell’s Lemma 5, reproduced in [[countable-boolean-completeness-implies-necessity-of-arithmetic]], gives

I_\sigma\to I^*_\sigma. \tag{3}

This part uses no Countable Boolean Completeness. Here is the guard argument. Canonical numberhood is persistent by its least-inductive definition. Double number induction proves

m,n\in N_\sigma\land m\ne n \ \longrightarrow\ \Box(I^*_\sigma\to m\ne n).

The zero case uses non-successorhood, and the successor step uses injectivity under I^*_\sigma, with persistence of numberhood. If current I^*_\sigma fails by a successor being zero, numberhood persistence and necessary identity preserve that obstruction. If it fails by equal successors of distinct numbers, necessary identity preserves their successor equality, while the displayed conditional distinctness says that any future I^*_\sigma would preserve their difference. Thus \neg I^*_\sigma\to\Box\neg I^*_\sigma, which is (3). All these statements are discharged C theorems and can be necessitated.

Also, S4 gives

\neg I_\sigma\to\Box\neg I_\sigma: \tag{4}

indeed \Diamond I_\sigma\to I_\sigma is \Diamond\Diamond I^*_\sigma\to\Diamond I^*_\sigma.

3. A given rigid real field fixes arithmetic truth

Suppose, at an admitted host \tau, that a tuple \mathfrak R satisfies \operatorname{RigidStr}(\mathfrak R) and \Box\operatorname{Real}_\tau(\mathfrak R). We prove (1) from this given tuple without assuming \Box I^*_\sigma.

Write B=N_{\mathfrak R} for its number property and define the relational successor

\operatorname{Next}(y,z):=By\land Bz\land +_{\mathfrak R}(y,1_{\mathfrak R},z).

Under current Real, its least-inductive number property and the ordered field axioms give the Peano conditions for (B,0_{\mathfrak R}, \operatorname{Next}): successor is total, functional and injective, and cannot be zero. The last assertion follows by induction that all members of B are nonnegative and 0<1.

Current matching and preservation of arithmetic

Under current I^*_\sigma and Real, define E^{\nu\tau t} as the intersection of all admitted relations containing (0_\sigma,0_{\mathfrak R}) and closed under

E(x,y)\land\operatorname{Next}(y,z) \ \Longrightarrow\ E(\operatorname{Suc}_\sigma x,z).

This means one impredicative lambda formula, not an external intersection assumption. Its base pair and closure follow from its defining universal quantifier. For its unfolding, let U consist of the base pair and one-step paired successors of E. Closure gives U\subseteq E; this inclusion makes U itself pair-inductive, so minimality gives E\subseteq U. These are pointwise implications, with no inference from current coextension to identity of arbitrary relations.

The product N_\sigma\times B is pair-inductive, hence supports E. Number induction gives a unique image of each canonical number: at zero, the successor alternative of the unfolding is impossible; at a successor, injectivity identifies the predecessor, and the induction hypothesis and functionality of Next identify the image. The range predicate is inductive in B, so it contains every B-member. Induction in B, using injectivity of Next and the zero case, gives unique preimages. Thus E is a current bijection preserving zero and successor. These inductions apply to all admitted numbers, including nonstandard ones, and use arbitrary typed parameters.

It also preserves the native Sum and Prod graphs. For a fixed first argument m, the canonical sum graph is the least pair relation starting at (0,m) and closed under successor in both coordinates. The product graph starts at (0,0) and advances its second coordinate by addition of m when its first coordinate advances by one. Pull an admitted relation on B back through E by lambda abstraction; push a canonical relation forward by existential quantification through E. Current bijectivity transfers these base and closure clauses in both directions. Their universal defining clauses therefore transfer Sum and Prod. Internal induction on B identifies the resulting graphs with restricted field addition and multiplication. In particular, the product base pair is (0,0), not (0,m).

Translate A_\sigma to a first-order analytical sentence A^{\mathfrak R}: number quantifiers become field quantifiers guarded by B, arithmetic predicates become the corresponding field graphs, and each successor term is eliminated by finitely many relational successor equations. Numberhood, equality and zero are preserved as well. Finite induction on the syntax gives the discharged C theorem

\forall\mathfrak R\, [I^*_\sigma\land\operatorname{Real}_\tau(\mathfrak R) \to(A_\sigma\leftrightarrow A^{\mathfrak R})]. \tag{5}

No modal persistence of E is needed for this current categoricity argument. No numerical operation of forbidden output type is extracted.

Necessitate (5), instantiate the fixed tuple by boxed universal instantiation, and use \Box\operatorname{Real}_\tau(\mathfrak R). Necessitate (3) as well. Modal K then gives

\Box[I_\sigma\to (A_\sigma\leftrightarrow A^{\mathfrak R})]. \tag{6}

Stability on the given field

We use the argument of Lemmas 1–2 of [[analytic-necessitism-implies-necessity-of-analysis]], composed by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 23 October 2026, after Barton–Goodsell and Bacon.

For a rigid field relation S, a true atomic instance persists. If S\bar a is false at field elements \bar a, every tuple satisfying S differs from \bar a in a coordinate; necessary distinctness of field members makes that difference necessary. Inextensibility of S, applied to the complement of the haecceity of \bar a, gives \Box\neg S\bar a. Equality is persistent by necessary identity, and its negation by the carrier’s ND clause.

An induction on first-order analytical formulas now preserves both truth and falsity. Boolean steps use K. If \forall x\in X\,\varphi(x,\bar a) is true, the induction hypothesis gives \forall x(Xx\to\Box\varphi(x,\bar a)); inextensibility of X gives the necessary universal statement, including all future members. If it is false, a current counterexample persists in X and its negated formula persists by induction, yielding the necessary counterexample. No exchange of an arbitrary universal quantifier with Box is used.

At the sentence A^{\mathfrak R}, excluded middle therefore yields \Box A^{\mathfrak R}\lor\Box\neg A^{\mathfrak R}. Combine either sign with (6) to obtain the corresponding disjunct of (1). This proves the given-field arithmetic-truth lemma.

4. Obtain the reference field with the exact current premises

Return to current ANA and Transversal, and split on current I_\sigma.

If it holds, (3) supplies current I^*_\sigma. Apply (2) using the one Transversal instance at \nu; a current real field at \rho exists and is therefore possible. ANA at that same host supplies a rigid necessarily-real tuple there. Apply §3 with \tau=\rho to obtain (1). The chosen tuple is eliminated because (1) does not mention it.

If I_\sigma is false, (4) makes it necessarily false. Both guarded disjuncts of (1) then hold vacuously.

These cases prove the required instance. Since the counting type and arithmetical sentence were arbitrary external indices, this is the full Necessity of Arithmetic schema. The argument never necessitates ANA, Transversal, a current selector or an existential witness choice. Its truth guard remains I_\sigma inside each box; the separate Arithmetic Necessitism result retains its own \Box I^*_\sigma guard.
