# Finite-height independence: omit height 2

<p class='cert'>Model — Source: Kodsi, Finite-Type Necessitism (2026); produced by Daniel Kodsi, with assistance from GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI); recorded by ChatGPT, at Daniel Kodsi’s request.</p>

## Package

- **Finite-Type Necessitism: height 0.** The exact height-0 fragment of Finite-Type Necessitism, at every carrier type. A possible Peano presentation has a rigid realization at the same type, with its full rigid represented power.
- **Second-Order Analytic Necessitism.** If there could be a real number structure, there is a rigid one, necessarily a real number structure, together with represented powers of every arity up to $n$: rigid properties of rigid relations among the structure's elements that necessarily represent every relation on them. One sentence for each type $\sigma$ and each $n\ge1$.
- **¬ Finite-Type Necessitism: height 2.** The exact height-2 fragment of Finite-Type Necessitism, at every carrier type. A possible presentation of numbers, subset codes and codes for subsets of those codes has such a rigid realization, with a full rigid power of its last carrier.

## Definition

Use the full two-point model of Finite-Type Necessitism, Sections 6.1–6.2, with A⊆B, |A|=beth_1 and |B|=beth_2, evaluated at the root. Individual transport is inclusion; relational domains, paired-profile operations and full quantifiers are exactly equations (6.1)–(6.3). The common construction is given once in the linked finite-type guide and in the paper.

## Arguments

### Holds Finite-Type Necessitism: height 0, Second-Order Analytic Necessitism. Fails Finite-Type Necessitism: height 2.

Theorem 6.1 with j=2 gives every exact height except 2: the individual-type capacity is the only obstruction; every relational-type instance holds by the projection-section argument. F0 and SOAN (=F1) hold, hence all seven existing principles. F2 fails, so full FTN fails (Corollary 6.2).
