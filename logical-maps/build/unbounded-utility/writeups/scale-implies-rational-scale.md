# Scale Invariance ⇒ Rational Scale Invariance

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude (Fable 5.1), 2026-09-24; recorded by Claude (Fable 5.1), 2026-09-24.</p>

## Premises

- **Scale Invariance.** For every real $a>0, X \succeq Y$ iff $aX \succeq aY$.

## Conclusion

- **Rational Scale Invariance.** For every rational $a>0, X \succeq Y$ iff $aX \succeq aY$.

## Proof

Restrict the real factor $a>0$ of Scale Invariance to rational values.

## Notes

Immediate restriction. Premises are sufficient; minimality is not claimed. No translation checker is asserted.
