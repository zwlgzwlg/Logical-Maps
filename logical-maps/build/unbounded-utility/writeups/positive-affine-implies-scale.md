## Premises

- **Positive Affine Invariance.** For all real $a>0$ and $b, X \succeq Y$ iff $aX+b \succeq aY+b$.

## Conclusion

- **Scale Invariance.** For every real $a>0, X \succeq Y$ iff $aX \succeq aY$.
