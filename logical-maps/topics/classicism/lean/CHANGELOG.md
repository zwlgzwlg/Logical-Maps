# Changelog

What changed in the Lean formalization of *Classicism*, by day: coverage and design, not
every step. The steps are in the git history (`git log -- topics/classicism/lean`, which
since the move includes the history of `Cian/` in `zwlgzwlg/Lean-Classicism`); what was
checked at each, and why, is in `VERIFICATION.md`.

## 5 October 2026

- **Moved into the map.** The project is now `topics/classicism/lean/`, with its history
  merged in; the separate repository is retired. The records carry the Lean fields
  (`lean_def`, `lean_ref`, each variant's `lean`); `pmap lean` writes the statements;
  `pmap lean-check` audits results and variants alike, sets their statuses, and writes the
  index from which the viewer links each result, principle and variant to its proofs,
  definitions and certificate. 234 results and 56 variants verified.
- `AGENTS.md` (how to work on the Lean) and `TODO.md` (open work and questions) replace the
  working handoff notes; the vectorization plan and the coverage survey move to `history/`.

## 4 October 2026

- **Files by the map's categories**: `Principles/<Category>.lean` and
  `Results/Records/<Topic>.lean`; the principles in the paper's notation, their terms
  checked unchanged.
- **Forms** (the map's variants) defined beside their principles with the two directions of
  the equivalence: the duals of 26 principles and three LUB forms, 58 theorems; two
  classical laws added to `Pointwise`, and the order at a type parameter to `Lattice.lean`.
  With the polyadic forms, 56 of the map's 77 variants certified.
- **An adversarial audit** (Astra) and its fixes: the sentence schemas restricted to the
  paper's language (no type variables in an instance); model statements asking for a
  consistent complete theory; the model in `Prop` built on `Unit`, so that every
  certificate rests on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` only.
- Statements regenerated from the map of 4 October: 266 of its 316 results stated.

## 2 October 2026

- **The map's Lean side drafted**: statements generated by the map's own generator, one
  certificate per result (`Map.lean`), an index of where each proof is.
- **Principles at every signature** (`Certified/Signatures.lean`); statements over the
  signatures the Background admits; the **conservativity** of `C(Σ)` over `C`.
- New principles: the Axioms of Infinity and Possible Infinity, Countable Boolean
  Completeness, Witnessed Possibility and its kin, Possibility+, Ordinary Comprehension,
  Separated Structure, Independence, Strong Possibility, the Infinity schemas: 95 of the
  map's then 97 principles defined. 234 of its 270 results certified.
- General lemmas moved into the library; "schema" adopted for axiom set; `boxImp`
  renamed `incl`.

## 1 October 2026

- **Vectorization**: type variables in the object language and the vectorization theorem,
  so that a result proved at one argument type holds at every list of argument types
  (`history/VECTORIZATION-PLAN.md`). List forms of every principle with a type parameter;
  the results at every arity as unary proofs vectorized.
- The Boolean Completeness and Plenitude records at every arity; the Strong Leibniz
  results; the map's principles and results of 25–28 September. 202 of 270 results.
- Proposition 2.11 refuted (Cian's countermodel).

## 28 September 2026

- **The C5 results** of §§2.1–2.3: Propositions 2.3–2.6, 2.10, 2.12, 2.14, 2.16 and n. 41.
- Twelve results proved at every arity; a bulk track of 42 routine results; record
  theorems named by the map's ids. 147 of 230 results.
- **Appendix D** completed for its eight parts: Boolean Completeness failures, BF without
  □BF, the parts with a point adjoined.
- Proposition 2.11 reduced to a restriction principle; a second gap in its note recorded.
- The paper's notation through the shallow layer.

## 26 September 2026

- **Intensional action models** (the form of Cian's *Boolean Completeness without Rigid
  Comprehension*): premodels, soundness, full models, the M-set examples; the results
  cite them.
- **Appendix D**: pinning, ideally full models, Proposition D.4 by induction on terms, D.6,
  and Parts 1 to 8 with their consistency facts.

## 25 September 2026

- `simp` admitted through the gate with the library's closed identities.
- Actuality, Boolean Completeness, Atomlessness, Plenitude and their boxed forms, with
  seventeen records among them.

## 24 September 2026

- **Certification moves off the strict layer**: a translator from gated shallow proofs to
  derivations; entailment between axiom sets; principles as schemas, records as
  entailments; a metalogical proof with object-level steps.
- The project rearranged: mathematics apart from metaprogramming, the strict layer a
  sideline.
- Distinctness and Possibility equivalent; No Pure Contingency necessitates every pure
  schema; incompatibilities from the models.

## 23 September 2026

- **Action models** (the paper's §3): premodels, models, soundness, full models,
  truncation, the facts about ND, BF and the Fregean Axiom, two M-set models.
- The relational operations as constants with δ-rules, their laws by induction on the
  type; the translator over the whole library. Mathlib enters, for the model theory only.

## 22 September 2026

- **The metalogical layer**: types, terms, substitution, conversion, derivability, `C` as
  an axiom set, the denotation and soundness, the consistency of `C`; the quoter and the
  first translator.
- Principles as families of formulas indexed by types.

## 21 September 2026

- The formalization as first committed to the map: the shallow layer under the gate
  (`propext` and `funext` only at closed arguments), the strict layer deriving the Boolean
  algebras from the eleven identities, the transformer (Appendix A on Lean proof terms),
  and the type-system check; 29 of 77 principles defined, 27 records proved. Then moved
  to `zwlgzwlg/Lean-Classicism` as `Cian/`, with the gate's bypasses closed.
