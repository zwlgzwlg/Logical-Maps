# Contributions and proof guide

**[Finite-Type Necessitism — paper (PDF)](../papers/finite-type-necessitism.pdf) · [LaTeX source](../papers/finite-type-necessitism.tex)**
Daniel Kodsi, with assistance from GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), 30 October 2026.

## Principal contributions

**Finite-type realization and independence.** The new FTN, not the former formulation, requires a rigid same-carrier realization of every possibly realizable finite arithmetic hierarchy, with a full rigid represented native power. F₀ entails Arithmetic Necessitism, Necessity of Arithmetic and Necessity of Analysis; F₁ is equivalent to SOAN at each carrier type. Each exact height is independent of all the others together, so the cumulative hierarchy is strict. F₀ ∧ F₁ supplies the seven existing principles, but their conjunction does not entail full FTN. See Definition 1.1, Theorems 3.1, 4.3–4.4 and 6.1, and Corollaries 4.5 and 6.2. The [finite-type guide](finite-type-non-contingency-guide.md) gives the construction and map correspondence. The three height records are instances of one construction; the paper proves the arbitrary-height theorem.

**Analytical separations.** The [Suslin proof](suslin-analytic-truth-separation.md), relative to Con(ZFC), gives AN and necessary positive CH while a second-order analytical sentence changes truth on a fixed necessarily real field. It refutes the exact boxed-universal schema, not only a sentence about a named parameter. [Repeated forcing](full-boolean-valued-repeated-forcing.md) separates second-order truth non-contingency from AN and separates AN plus that truth principle from SOAN, with C5 and necessary Relational Choice. The classical forcing theorems and inherited Boolean interpretation are credited inputs, not new forcing theorems.

**Power versus arithmetic.** The [Rosser-selected full present](full-current-c5-arithmetic-root.md) satisfies necessary Weakly Rigid Power but fails Arithmetic Necessitism and Necessity of Arithmetic, with the arithmetic guard active. Necessary Rigid Power and Tame Rigidity follow by existing implications.

**Forcing and CH.** The [fixed-signature forcing cover](henkin-compactness-full-current-surjective-cover.md) supplies the joint Maximalism, strict FP and Barcan model. The [CH-contingency theorem](necessary-rigid-comprehension-intensional-choice-and-forcing-possibilism-incompatible-with-non-contingency-of-ch.md) keeps all additional size and necessary-infinity hypotheses explicit. Bacon's forcing argument and Dorr's cardinality suggestion retain their credits; the contribution is the repaired implication, exact size comparison and structural bridge, with omission countermodels. The original four-premise conjecture is not promoted to a theorem.

## Why the supporting models remain

These are different joint verdicts, not interchangeable versions of one counterexample. Each retained new record has a signed profile not subsumed by another recorded model. The table identifies the useful distinction rather than repeating the full construction.

| Construction | Distinctive joint result |
| --- | --- |
| [Covariant surjection cover](interpretation-cover-covariant-surjections.md) | Signature Maximalism, Atomlessness and unboxed Inextensible Comprehension together; Barcan also holds. |
| [Henkin/full-trace cover](interpretation-cover-henkin-full-traces.md) | Signature Possibility+ and unboxed Barcan with failed Rigid Power; choice, Vicinity and Atomlessness are preserved. |
| [True-theory nonstandard full present](full-present-true-theory-nonstandard.md) | Second-order analytical truth is fixed while **both** Arithmetic Necessitism and AN fail. The separate failures were already witnessed; their joint realization is the additional result. |
| [Two repeated-forcing models](full-boolean-valued-repeated-forcing.md) | Opposite AN verdicts, with different SOAN conclusions, under the stated shared choice/C5 package. They cannot be combined into a single model. |
| [NoReal Cohen constructions](symmetric-cohen-arithmetic-growth.md) | Preserve SOAN while canonical arithmetic grows or, in the [Rosser-selected variant](symmetric-cohen-arithmetic-contingency.md), arithmetic truth varies. Their analytic antecedents are false; this qualification is essential. |

The positive-only [named-permutation construction](https://github.com/dkodsi/Logical-Maps/blob/classicism-next-batch-2026-10-30/logical-maps/topics/classicism/writeups/interpretation-cover-named-permutations.md) is retained as a source proof, not a second model record. Every one of its recorded positive and negative consequences is covered by the augmented Henkin/full-trace record. Failure of Rigid Power is asserted only for the augmented construction, not retroactively for every named cover.

The small exact-height family is not replaced by more elaborate forcing examples: its j = 0, 1, 2 instances separately witness the missing heights and the failure of the seven-principle package to imply FTN. F₁ is displayed on the SOAN node; F₂ is retained to make the first further-height obstruction queryable.

## Other connections and attribution

The positive additions include AN + unboxed Countable Boolean Completeness → Arithmetic Necessitism; AN + unboxed Transversal → Arithmetic Necessitism and Necessity of Arithmetic; and unboxed Gallin Extensional Comprehension → every self-atom is strong. The [Henkin completeness examples](elementary-henkin-presents.md) retain their separate completeness/SOAN profiles. Existing implications such as AN → Necessity of Analysis and SOAN → Necessity of Second-Order Analysis are connections used by the new paper, not new discoveries claimed by it.

**Daniel Kodsi suggestions:** [Atomicity by necessarily-world propositions](necessary-world-atomicity.md) and Finite-Type Necessitism. The paper retains its supplied authorship; the standing framework and classical/earlier proofs retain their individual source credits. Appendix A remains in the paper without separate map records.

The scoped [completeness/choice](completeness-and-choice-model-class-obstructions.md), [single-cone CBC](single-cone-countable-completeness-obstruction.md), [protected-numbers](protected-numbers-and-numerical-growth.md) and [finite-calibration](finite-numerical-calibrations.md) notes remain supporting arguments, not unrestricted new arrows. The mathematical proofs are informal. Data and build checks are not independent human or Lean verification.
