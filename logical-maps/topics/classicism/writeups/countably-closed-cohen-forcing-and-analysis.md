# Fixed analytical truth and a missing represented power

Two instances of the same Boolean-valued construction separate three principles. In both, Analytic Necessitism holds and the continuum hypothesis remains true. In the first, every sentence of second-order analysis is fixed, yet Second-Order Analytic Necessitism fails. In the second, a specific second-order analytical sentence changes truth value. Both constructions give relative consistency over ZFC.

## Adding a subset of the ground continuum

Work in a ground universe $W$ satisfying CH. Let $Q$ consist of countable partial functions from $\omega_1$ to $2$, ordered by extension. Unions of descending countable chains are lower bounds. Deciding one coordinate of a name at each step shows that $Q$ adds no countable sequence from a ground set. In particular the reals and $\omega_1$ are preserved. CH also gives $|Q|=\aleph_1$, so $\omega_2$ is preserved. The generic union $H$ is a new subset of $\omega_1$. Given a condition and a proposed ground subset, extend the condition at a coordinate outside its domain with the opposite bit.

Use the [full Boolean-valued construction over a designated atom](boolean-valued-collapse-without-new-reals.html), with $A=\operatorname{RO}(Q)$ and $B=\mathbf2\times A$. The individual type is a singleton. The proposition type is $B$, and relational types are full ground function spaces. Identity is crisp ground identity. Truth is projected to the $\mathbf2$ coordinate at the designated point, and $\Box p$ tests whether $p=1_B$. For each fixed ground tuple set, a name for a subset is represented by the ground function taking each tuple to its Boolean membership value. The same holds at every finite arity. Thus an extensional real structure and second-order quantification over its numbers and reals are interpreted with all available subsets and relations in the corresponding universe. This is the [Boolean-valued interpretation of Bacon, online appendix §B.2](https://andrew-bacon.github.io/papers/Could%20mathematics%20have%20been%20different.pdf).

Every relational carrier contains a ground copy of $B$ by constant functions. Conditions assigning all bits on a fixed countably infinite set give a ground antichain of size continuum. At each relational type we can therefore choose disjoint ground labels for the naturals and the reals. Give the usual number, successor, real and membership relations crisp characteristic functions on the labels. A crisp ground relation is rigid. Its members persist, and if a tester necessarily covers each member, its value there is top, so the boxed inclusion of the whole row is top. Both conditions continue to hold in the Boolean semantics. Distinct labels have crisp distinctness. Since $Q$ adds no sets of numbers, every new name for such a set takes ground-real values locally on a maximal antichain. The Boolean join saying that some old label represents the set is top. Thus the structure is necessarily real. At the singleton individual type no Peano structure is possible. This proves all-type Analytic Necessitism. In either coordinate every actual real structure computes CH in its universe, so CH holds necessarily.

### Failure for every proposed power

Work at propositional type, where the displayed structure already witnesses possibility. Consider *any* proposed rigid, necessarily real structure. Its real carrier and defining relations are crisp ground rows. Indeed each relation is coextensive at the designated atom with the crisp predicate of its actual row; two coextensive weakly rigid relations are identical. To see uniqueness, weak persistence of either relation makes each of the other's actual members necessarily an instance. Weak inextensibility gives both inclusions in the entailment order.

For a proposed unary represented power $\mathcal P$, the same argument makes the property $\mathcal P$ crisp. Each member of its ground extension is itself rigid by the native definition of a represented power, hence crisp by the same uniqueness argument. The actual real carrier $X$ of this arbitrary structure has continuum many members in $W$. Its number part is standard by full current predicates, and fullness and extensionality identify the reals with the subsets of that number part. Take a ground injection $i:\omega_1\to X$.

The forcing-coordinate predicate selecting $i(\xi)$ precisely when $\xi\in H$ has a ground Boolean-valued representative. For any particular ground member of $\mathcal P$, its restriction to the labels $i(\xi)$ is a fixed ground subset of $\omega_1$. No positive condition forces agreement with $H$. Each candidate therefore has Boolean value zero for the required agreement on those labels. Candidates outside the ground extension already have membership value zero. Even the Boolean existential over condition-dependent choices has value zero. The native boxed coverage clause fails, although it asks only for agreement on the numbers and reals. This refutes the unary instance of Second-Order Analytic Necessitism.

## The example fixing every analytical sentence

Begin in $V=L$. First force with the countable partial binary functions on $\omega\times\omega_1$, obtaining $W=V[G]$. This forcing $P$ is countably closed, preserves CH, and is weakly homogeneous. Given two conditions, flipping the bits on their disagreements produces an automorphism making the first compatible with the second. In particular the top condition decides each fixed parameter-free sentence of set theory.

Since $P$ adds no countable sequences of ground objects, $Q^W$ is the same poset as $Q^V$. Their iteration is consequently forcing-equivalent to $P\times Q$. Reindexing the countably many columns together with the extra column gives an isomorphism $P\times Q\cong P$. Thus $W$ and every further $W[H]$ have the same parameter-free set-theoretic theory. For each sentence, its truth in both extensions is the sign decided by the top of the original $P$. This uses only the ordinary product and homogeneity arguments for forcing. [Shoenfield, *Unramified Forcing*, §§8–9](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~sbuss/CourseWeb/Math260_2012F2013W/Shoenfield_UnramifiedForcing.pdf)

Each fixed closed second-order analytical sentence has a parameter-free set-theoretic translation in the standard structure of numbers and reals. Full local subsets force every realized real structure's number system to be standard, and extensionality and fullness make its real part precisely the subsets of that system. Full relations give the same interpretation for quantifiers of every finite arity. Consequently at each carrier all real structures in both coordinates satisfy the same sign of the analytical sentence. This is Necessity of Second-Order Analysis. The unary represented power still fails by the preceding argument.

## The example changing one analytical sentence

Instead use $W=L$ directly, without the preliminary forcing. A second-order analytical sentence can express that $H_{\mathfrak c^+}$ satisfies $V=L$, where $H_{\mathfrak c^+}$ consists of the sets whose transitive closure has size at most continuum. Here is how to obtain one fixed sentence without naming a cardinal, a well-order of the reals or the generic.

Use a unary predicate to select a domain of real labels, a binary edge relation on it for membership, and a root. Require well-foundedness by quantification over unary subsets and extensionality by comparison of immediate predecessors. Require every vertex to lie in every predecessor-closed subset containing the root. This gives precisely the downward cone of the root. Such a code collapses to the transitive closure of a set together with the set as root. Conversely every hereditarily size-at-most-continuum set has such a code by choosing enough real labels. Equality of coded sets is existence of a pointed isomorphism, expressible by a binary relation. Membership is isomorphism to the subcode rooted at an immediate predecessor; its downward cone is definable by the same closed-subset test. Relativize the quantifiers of a fixed first-order set-theoretic sentence to these codes. The result uses only the real carrier, element variables, and unary and binary relation quantifiers of second-order analysis. Apply this translation to the single sentence $V=L$.

In $L$ we have $H_{\omega_2}=L_{\omega_2}$. For the nontrivial inclusion, put a set's transitive closure of size at most $\aleph_1$ into an elementary submodel of a sufficiently large limit $L_\theta$, using at most $\aleph_1$ elements. By condensation its transitive collapse is $L_\beta$ for $\beta<\omega_2$, and it fixes the set in question. Each $L_\beta$ below $\omega_2$ itself has size at most $\aleph_1$. The coded sentence is therefore true. In $L[H]$ the new set $H\subseteq\omega_1$ lies in $H_{\omega_2}$ and is not constructible. This $H_{\omega_2}$ computes its levels $L_\alpha$ correctly for $\alpha<\omega_2$: those levels and the sequences constructing them have hereditary size at most $\aleph_1$, and the constructible hierarchy is absolute. The same analytical sentence is false there. The rigid labelled structure at type $t$ thus witnesses a failure of Necessity of Second-Order Analysis while CH stays fixed and Analytic Necessitism holds.

## Other verdicts in the two models

The designated atom gives arbitrary joins of actual ground families at each relational type, computed pointwise. In the forcing coordinate a countable subfamily of any fixed ground carrier still belongs to the ground because the forcing adds no countable sequences. Native Frege numbers lie in the countable ground orbit of the standard numerals: that crisp orbit is an admitted inductive test. Hence necessary Countable Boolean Completeness holds at all types.

Full propositional Boolean Completeness fails to be necessary. Choose a disjoint family of positive $d_\xi\in B$ indexed by $\omega_1$ from the antichain of countable conditions. At the forcing coordinate take the family $\{\neg d_\xi:\xi\in H\}$. Its ground greatest lower bound $y$, if one existed on a positive condition, would determine $\xi\notin H$ exactly by $d_\xi\le y$ on that condition. Disjointness makes $d_\xi$ itself a lower bound when $\xi\notin H$, and when $\xi\in H$ it is incompatible with $y$. Each putative ground $y$ therefore has Boolean value zero for being the greatest lower bound.

Full ground functions at the designated atom give Functional Choice. Crisp identities give necessary ND, and joins over constant ground carriers give all Barcan instances. For Intensional Choice, well-order the ground carrier and remove earlier Boolean values from each candidate's value, obtaining pairwise disjoint values with the same join. Ground well-orders remain well-orders and furnish Modal Well-Ordering. The designated atom gives Strong Actuality, while the forcing factor is atomless. No interval of $B$ has exactly four elements: an interval meeting the forcing factor nontrivially is infinite, and one disjoint from it has at most two elements. Thus revised Mathematical Possibilism fails already for the finite four-element algebra.
