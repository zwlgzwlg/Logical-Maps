# Free words over four infinite arithmetic shifts

Both the individual and propositional Axioms of Infinity hold necessarily, together with necessary Strong Atomicity at every relational type. Accessible components nevertheless disagree on arithmetic and the continuum hypothesis. The increase in propositional size also makes a countable family of scalar atoms at the root lack a join.

## Objects and interpretations

Use the four set-theoretic interpretations of [free words over disagreeing arithmetics](free-words-over-disagreeing-arithmetics.html). One is the ordinary hierarchy on the naturals. A second is internal to a countable model $H$ of Zermelo set theory with Choice and $\neg\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZC})$. The other two are internal to countable models of ZC with opposite answers to CH. The cited construction gives the consistency assumptions in ZFC. In each interpretation use one object with arrows its own additive monoid $(\omega,+)$. Give it its naturals as individuals, acted on trivially. At every relational sort use the internal powerset of the product of the monoid and the argument domains. The row at zero is current. An arrow $k$ reads the old rows at $l+k$. At each fixed sort and formula this is the full action construction inside the indicated interpretation. Its intensional identity is externally faithful since distinct internal sets have a differing internal member. Term evaluation is absolute for the external reading of this fixed typed structure by induction on the formula.

Let $W$ be the free monoid on $\max(\aleph_0,|\Sigma|)$ letters. At its root keep the original word arrows and an entrance $h_{i,u}$ for each component $i$ and word $u$. The arrows into $i$ are now all $k h_{i,u}$ for $k$ in its monoid. A root word $v$ changes the address $u$ to $uv$, and a following component shift adds to $k$. A root individual's array has one individual entry for each $(i,u)$; its entrance reads that entry. At a relation sort root word rows are unrestricted, with one independent whole component entity for each $(i,u)$. The arrow $k h_{i,u}$ projects to that coordinate then shifts it by $k$. A differing word row is observable there; a differing component entity is observable after its entrance at some shift and tuple. In computing an abstract, use all root arguments at each word and evaluate the body in the component on the transported parameters and all of its arguments at each entrance. These are precisely allowed coordinates, and precomposition gives the term transport. The original final-letter gluing of witnesses works unchanged on these whole entities. A possible component witness of a pure matrix is a tuple at that object, so its dedicated entrance can use shift zero. This proves Possibly Witnessed Possibility for the supplied signature.

## Cardinalities and necessary modalities

Inside each component and at $\sigma=e,t$, define $C_n$ for an internal $n$ to have, at each shift, the row of $\sigma$-properties with exactly $n$ current members. A shift fixes $C_n$. The empty-property and one-member-removal calculations give $0=C_0$ and $S C_n=C_{n+1}$ as whole entities. An internal property selecting these entities is admitted. Internal induction in the component on $n$, applied to the current row of every inductive property, gives exactly the $C_n$ as its Frege numbers. Since each of the two sorts is internally infinite, any prescribed finite size can be realized and no $C_n$ counts its universal property. At the root the current row selecting precisely the standard numeral denotations is unrestricted, and each root sort is infinite. The same standard-numeral argument gives both Axioms of Infinity there.

At the first two components the guarded individual arithmetics have opposite truth values for $\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZC})$. Thus Necessity of Arithmetic fails. For Arithmetic Necessitism choose a nonstandard natural $b$ in $H$. At one entrance to $H$ put the tester whose row at every internal shift consists of $C_n$ with $n\le b$, and use full rows elsewhere. All source numbers are standard numerals, so they satisfy this tester necessarily. At the entrance it omits the number $C_{b+1}$. The native number property at $e$ is therefore not weakly inextensible despite its necessary Peano guard. A pure closed guarded arithmetic statement true at either of these one-object components remains true at every continuation there. Taking the guard conjoined with the arithmetic statement, or its negation as appropriate, gives both a possibly necessary truth and its possibly necessary negation at the root, refuting pure B.

In each component the type $et$ contains distinct invariant copies of every internal individual subset, using the same extension at every shift. These include the individual singleton codes for numbers and the powerset codes for their subsets. One can give the number and real codes separate tags using the internal bijection of $\omega$ with two copies of $\omega$. Every internal row or graph on this family is admitted at the current point, including the arithmetic, membership and higher relations. Thus the arithmetic disagreement also occurs in real structures at $et$. At the two CH components the internal full powersets and graphs on the same codes give real structures with their respective answers to structural CH. In fact every real structure there has that component's answer, since it has all its current internal subproperties and graphs.

Word shifts at all root sorts are onto by copying onto a suffix. Every component coordinate is free, and every internal shift has an internal section by copying rows at $l$ onto $l+k$ and filling earlier rows with the empty set. Thus every future entity has a current preimage at every stage, proving necessary BF. Single tuple atoms at single addresses exist, including a specified internal tuple at one specified internal shift. By right cancellation each transports to a single-address singleton or bottom. Every occupied row contains one. The proposition supported solely at the identity never becomes possible again after a proper arrow, both at the root and in each component. This proves necessary Strong Atomicity and necessary ND-necessity of every truth. At the root the proposition supported solely at the zero shifts of two different entrances is a self-atom: whenever true its transport is a component singleton atom. The two entrances split it at the root. Fixed external well-orders on the root word rows and internal well-orders at every component shift give Modal Well-Ordering.

## Rigidity, powers and Boolean completeness

For a weakly rigid relation with current extension $E$, its row at each arrow $f$ is exactly $f[E]$. To see the upper bound, a tester can exclude one specified tuple outside $f[E]$ just at $f$. At a component entrance this uses the internal row omitting the tuple at one shift and full rows at other shifts. Weak persistence gives the lower bound. Conversely exact image rows give weak rigidity and are hereditary by composition.

Rigid relations at a word lift by the original suffix construction: lift their current tuples by copying the arguments into the suffix, with fixed data elsewhere. At coordinates within the suffix the resulting direct images are the prescribed component rows of the given rigid relation. At another component coordinate choose a fixed default argument for each lifted tuple, for example zero for individuals and bottom for relations. The images there are empty or singletons and give internal profiles at every shift. At a zero-shift entrance into a component, lift the current tuples of a rigid target relation by freely selecting that coordinate of each argument with constant defaults elsewhere. The varying coordinate produces exactly the given internally admitted rows after each of its shifts, by target rigidity. At a positive shift in the component first lift the rigid relation internally: use a section of that shift on each argument, take the internally formed set of selected preimage tuples and prescribe its direct images along all internal shifts. Composing the lifts treats all entrances. Consequently the full and weak rigidity predicates themselves have exact image rows at all stages.

For failure of the powers use the correlation from the original free-word model. Write $h=h_{H,1}$ for the zero-shift entrance at the empty word and choose a letter $d$. At countably many distinct letters take the distinct unary relations constant in their argument whose truth is confined to that word; write $U$ for this set. At every component coordinate all of them become bottom. Therefore the relation $R_U$ with the exact image rows of $U$ is admitted and rigid.

In the component $H$ let $V$ be the external family of individual haecceities of its standard naturals, constant across its shifts. This cannot be an internal current row: pulling it back by the internally defined map $n\mapsto H_n$ would represent the standard cut in the nonstandard model. Choose a surjection $\beta:D_{r,et}\to D_{H,et}$ taking $U$ onto $V$. The component is externally countable and there are enough root profiles outside $U$. Form $D=\{a:ha=\beta(da)\}$. At every nonempty word or other independent entrance the image of $D$ is the full sort: lift the desired entry, then set the $h$ coordinate to $\beta(da)$. At $h$ its image is full by surjectivity of $\beta$ and of $d$. All internal shifts are also onto. Thus its exact-image relation $R_D$ is admitted and rigid.

At $d$ the rigid $R_U$ entails the transported $R_D$, which is top. A weakly rigid source subrelation lifting $R_U$ would have a current set $E\subseteq D$ with $d[E]=U$. Its row at $h$ would be $h[E]=\beta[U]=V$, which is unavailable. Omit $R_U$ only at $d$ in a tester, giving failure of weak inextensibility for both power predicates.

For the scalar failure select the single-shift atoms at $h$ supported at a standard natural $n$ in the monoid of $H$. Set all other coordinates to zero. Their countable family and its enumeration by standard Frege numbers have unrestricted current graphs. Each other single-arrow scalar atom is disjoint from the family, and its complement is an upper bound. Hence a least upper bound must have at $h$ the characteristic function of exactly the external standard cut. That set is not in $H$. Thus even the countable join is missing at propositional type.

Finally the root has all external current graphs, an infinite individual domain and at least continuum many scalar entities. As in the original free-word model the finite ordinal thresholds give a countably infinite subset of the ordinal benchmark, so any set strictly larger than it is uncountable. The atomless regular-open algebra of Cantor space, of size continuum, is consequently revised-small. Any uncountable subcarrier has a countably infinite subset with continuum many subextensions, too many to code on a set of strictly smaller size than that subcarrier. Every nonzero part of a root scalar contains a singleton-address atom. Thus no principal interval represents that atomless algebra, refuting revised Mathematical Possibilism.

## The variant with one individual

Replace the individual domain by a singleton at every object. The component monoids and the profile construction at relational types are unchanged. The sole individual realizes every required individual coordinate in the proof of Possibly Witnessed Possibility. The proofs by cancellation, suffix sections, singleton-address tests and row well-orders still apply. The nonstandard component family needed in the power argument can now be taken to consist of unary predicates whose only occupied row is at shift $n$, for standard $n$. The internal map from $n$ to the predicate supported just at $n$ would recover the standard cut from that family.

No property ever has two individuals. For arithmetic use type $t$ throughout. In each component the scalar domain is its internal powerset of its natural numbers. The calculation of the Frege numerals $C_n$ and the tester bounded by a nonstandard $b$ apply there. At the root the unrestricted current-row test still cuts the scalar Frege numbers down to the standard numerals. Thus the propositional Axiom of Infinity holds necessarily, while the two arithmetic principles fail. Pure B fails by the same pair of permanently opposite guarded arithmetical sentences.

For a real structure at $t$ in a component code the number $n$ by the scalar $\{2n\}$ and the set $S$ by $\{1\}\cup\{2n+2:n\in S\}$. These two internal injections have disjoint images. Every internal subset of the number codes and every internal graph is an admitted current row. The codes with their membership and successor relations therefore form the component's full real structure. The real-structure and CH clauses are evaluated at the current point. The guarded arithmetic sentence disagrees in the first two components, and the full real structures disagree on structural CH in the two CH components.

At the root, bound the inexact ordinal benchmark by the finite collection $Z$ of the uniform extensional truth tables on an order and a field of the sole individual. Each table tests only the two current extension bits and uses the same rule at every arrow. Such a whole entity belongs to the root domains, and transport gives the corresponding table. The unrestricted current row selecting these entities is admitted. Zero tests well-orderhood, and successor tests well-orderhood and an initial segment. On a singleton their outcomes are uniform functions of the same two bits, since all subsets are available at each point. A rigid subcollection with current extension contained in $Z$ equals the finite disjunction of the haecceities of those members. Persistence gives one inclusion and weak inextensibility applied to that disjunction gives the other. Its limit therefore tests well-orderhood and a fixed finite list of uniform tables, and belongs to $Z$. Induction bounds the ordinal carrier by this finite collection. [Ordinal constructors and the singleton bound](two-colour-multisets-with-a-word-guard.html)

If an actual algebra carrier were infinite, take a countably infinite subset $Y$ of it. All its current subsets and graphs are admitted. It strictly exceeds the finite ordinal benchmark, and a smaller subset of $Y$ is finite, so all of its subcollections can be coded on a finite subset of $Y$. Thus $Y$ is power-closed and the carrier fails revised smallness. For a finite Boolean algebra with $k$ atoms use the union of $k$ single-word scalar atoms (bottom when $k=0$). Its interval consists of their subunions and the required order isomorphism is a current graph. This proves revised Mathematical Possibilism at the root.
