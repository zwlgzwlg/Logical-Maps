# Tame Rigidity and Countable Boolean Completeness imply Arithmetic Necessitism

At the evaluation point, the countable join of the singleton predicates
of the current numbers makes the number property $\mathbb N_\sigma$ weakly
inextensible. Each current number is necessarily a number by induction.
These are the two clauses of weak rigidity. Tame Rigidity at the type of
number properties therefore makes $\mathbb N_\sigma$ rigid. For distinct
numbers $m,n$, Goodsell's Lemma 4 gives $\Box(I^*_\sigma\to m\ne n)$,
so the principle's antecedent $\Box I^*_\sigma$ gives $\Box(m\ne n)$.
The argument applies at every type $\sigma$, including $e$. The arithmetic
steps and the countable join are [Goodsell's Lemmas 3, 4 and 8](countable-boolean-completeness-implies-necessity-of-arithmetic.html).

In particular, ordinary Rigid Comprehension implies Arithmetic Necessitism.
Rigid Comprehension supplies [Tame Rigidity](rigid-comprehension-implies-tame-rigidity.html)
and [Boolean Completeness](rigid-comprehension-r-implies-boolean-completeness-r.html),
which [supplies its countable instances](boolean-completeness-r-implies-countable-boolean-completeness-r.html).
The premises in this argument are evaluated at the current point.
