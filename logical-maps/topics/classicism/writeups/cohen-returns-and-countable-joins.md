## The new countable family

At a root every internally countable family admits a coordinatewise join. The high ideal absorbs a countable union of null sets and a ground family of names admits a forcing name for its union. The high quotient has no supremum for the family of blocks in a ground partition of $I$ into $|I|$ disjoint positive blocks, by the deletion-of-one-point argument for that quotient.

At a forcing site fix a new Cohen real $r\subseteq\omega$. In the ground root choose pairwise disjoint nonzero propositions $q_n$, for instance on disjoint positive high-index sets. Let $b_n$ be the site proposition with false current bit and return $q_n$. There is a site property with current extension $\{b_n:n\in r\}$, and the graph taking $b_n$ to the $n$th Frege numeral is a represented countability witness. Both current rows exist in the extension. Entailment at the site is coordinatewise. If the family had a supremum with ground return $q$, then $q_n\leq q$ for every $n\in r$. For $n\notin r$, the complement of $b_n$ is a site upper bound, so $q\leq\neg q_n$. Nonzeroness of $q_n$ shows that $r=\{n:q_n\leq q\}$. The right side is defined from ground data, a contradiction. The failure of necessary Countable Boolean Completeness therefore already occurs at propositional type.

## Numbers, reals and CH

The original ground is $\omega$-standard. Every high map takes its $\omega$ onto its successor's $\omega$, by the ideal partition argument, and forcing preserves $\omega$. At each counting type the full internal current rows give precisely the standard Frege numerals, and the carriers are infinite. The numeral row has exact images along every arrow. Hence Arithmetic Necessitism, Necessity of Arithmetic and the necessary axioms of infinity at $e,t$ hold.

There is a real structure at propositional type both before and after a forcing entrance. A ground partition of $I$ into $|I|$ positive blocks supplies that many distinct scalar labels; the forcing site's return tags preserve them. Over a ground satisfying GCH the new continuum is $\aleph_1$ for $\mu=1$ and $\aleph_2=|I|$ for $\mu=\aleph_2$. In either case there are enough labels. Current successor and membership graphs on the labels, and all current subsets of their number parts, are available inside the corresponding set model.

The sentence “every real is constructible” is a first-order analytical sentence in the map's terminology. Constructibility of a real is projectively definable: one may quantify over a real coding a countable well-founded level of the constructible universe containing it. Thus only numbers and reals are quantified. Ground real structures satisfy the sentence, while at the forcing site a generic real witnesses its negation. This refutes Necessity of Analysis. A candidate ground structure witnessing Analytic Necessitism at $t$ has rigid carriers and rigid successor and membership relations. Each has its exact image through the forcing entrance by the characterization of $K_E$. Enumerate its number labels by the ground successor isomorphism with $\omega$. The new real determines a current subset of the transported number labels. A transported real label can only specify its old ground trace under the exact membership relation. Thus this subset has no code in the candidate, refuting Analytic Necessitism.

At every stage, full internal current rows make each actual real structure compute its universe's sign of CH. The high maps preserve the ground's GCH. A single Cohen real preserves CH by the count of nice names and the countable chain condition. For $\mu=\aleph_2$, the generic columns are distinct and cardinals are preserved, so the forcing site violates CH although the source satisfies it.
