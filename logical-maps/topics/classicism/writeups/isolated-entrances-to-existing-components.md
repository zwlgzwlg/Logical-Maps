# Atom and Barcan transfer at an isolated entrance

An isolated entrance can satisfy local Strong Actuality and Well-Ordering while an accessible component refutes Transversal or separates 5-necessity from two-hat necessity. For a single entrance the atom, self-atom and strong-atom conditions at the root can be read directly from the old point. Three of the settings also incorporate the Stone construction for revised Mathematical Possibilism.

## The common construction

Let \(w\) be the designated point of the pure reduct of the model named in the setting. Adjoin an object \(r\), its identity, an arrow \(h:r\to w\), and one distinct arrow \(kh\) for each old \(k\) from \(w\), with composition inherited on the old side. The individuals at \(r\) are those at \(w\) and \(h\) is the identity on them. A relation at \(r\) consists of an **arbitrary** current tuple row \(E\) and an independent old whole value \(U\) at \(w\). Abstraction takes the current satisfaction row and the old abstract on projected parameters. A difference in either coordinate is detected at the identity or after \(h\). All quantified old successors and all their old parameters remain available. This is the same entrance used in the two preceding modal examples. Work in the ordinary ZFC metatheory. For an old many-sorted Henkin model given by an ultraproduct one can take an action model of its pointed theory by completeness. In the choiceless S5 example first interpret the old typed structure in a model of the stated choiceless set theory. For standard finite types and formulas the pointed interpretation satisfies C, all the specified boxed sentences, the separate unbounded-size sentences and the particular counterinstances. Realize that consistent pointed theory as a set-sized action model in the ambient metatheory. It is only the new root that adds arbitrary external current rows; an old choice failure continues to quantify over precisely the admitted old relations. This uses the same relative consistency assumption as the [old S5 example](../index.html#model/full-infinite-s5-metatheory-no-countable-choice-reals).

The root-only proposition is a true strong atom and becomes bottom on every proper exit, so it guards the ND-necessity of every root truth. All current well-orders, transversals and selector graphs are available. To make a current relation inextensible, set every exit value to bottom. Its own current members are its only instances anywhere, so every tester necessarily accepting them includes it; at proper points bottom is inextensible. In all the settings \(D_t(r)\) is externally infinite, either by the old unbounded scalar domain or by the Stone factor. Take one \(tt\) name with bottom proper values and a current subset of \(D_t(r)\) outside the countably many denotations of closed pure terms. It is independent and never top. The pure matrix \(X=\top\) witnesses the failure of Possibly Witnessed Possibility.

## The transfer calculation

For a single entrance the root Boolean algebra at a relational type is the direct product of the full current powerset and the old algebra at \(w\). Its atoms are exactly the current-only tuple singletons and the old-only copies of old atoms. A nonzero self-atom instantiated at \(r\) must already be an atom at \(r\), hence a current-only singleton. A nonzero uninstantiated root relation with empty current row is a self-atom just when its old coordinate is one: the old instantiations and continuations are exactly the proper ones. Such an old-only relation is a strong atom at \(r\) exactly when it is a strong atom at \(w\). Indeed, the current factor does not affect entailment from it and every old tester lifts. Current-only singleton atoms are always strong. Consequently all seven local density and bridge questions for atoms, strong atoms and self-atoms have their old answers. When the old condition holds on the entire cone, the corresponding boxed condition holds at \(r\).

The map \(h\) is onto at every sort. If a root tester necessarily accepts every current argument, then at \(w\) it necessarily accepts every old argument, each of which has a preimage. Local Barcan at \(w\) makes this tester universally true throughout the old cone. Conversely, an old countertester to Barcan extends with full current row. This transfers local and boxed Barcan at each input sort. For Modalized Relational Choice combine a selector on the root row with the old necessarily functional selector given by that same principle at \(w\). For Modal Well-Ordering combine an external root well-order and the old modal well-order. Boxed Well-Ordering, Transversal, the choice principles about current relations, and the positive modal comparisons transfer whenever they hold throughout the old cone and the root satisfies their local parts. A failure anywhere in the old cone still refutes the boxed conclusion.

At \(r\) the unrestricted current predicate selecting the usual standard numeral iterates makes them exactly the Frege numbers. At any externally infinite root sort every standard finite size is realized and the universal property has no Frege finite count. When all individual domains throughout the old cone are singletons, the uniform extensional order/field tables on one individual give a finite inductive bound on the revised ordinal benchmark. A rigid collection of entries in this finite list equals the finite union of their haecceities by weak inextensibility and persistence. Its limit is another uniform table. Every infinite root carrier has a countably infinite power-closed subcollection above this finite bound, since all external actual rows and graphs are available. If there are infinitely many scalar atoms, every finite Boolean algebra is then represented below a finite join of these atoms. This proves revised Mathematical Possibilism for the single entrances to truncated pairs and to partial class isomorphisms. The former has an infinite atomic old scalar algebra; the latter has its countably many old terminal atoms. [Singleton ordinal calculation](free-words-over-infinite-arithmetic-shifts.html)

## The two ultraproducts

In the ultraproduct of finite S5 cliques with growing finite individual sets, every one of the fixed standard types is finite in each factor. Each factor satisfies throughout both the failure of its Peano guard and the absence of a real structure at that type. These are fixed sentences, so they hold throughout the old ultraproduct. At \(r\) the individual and scalar domains are externally infinite by the unbounded finite-size sentences. All current rows are available, so at a suitable higher type, for example \(et\), there are copies of the standard numbers and all their subsets with the required successor and membership graphs. Analytic Necessitism therefore fails: the old point admits no real structure there. At every type the antecedent of Arithmetic Necessitism fails because an old Peano guard fails. At any type all real structures in the cone occur at the actual root, where they read the same full analysis and all its relations. This gives Necessity of Second-Order Analysis. The two versions of Potential Infinity fail necessarily to be universal, witnessed in the old finite-factor cone. For the countable-world factors the individuals still grow through finite sizes, while the scalar domain remains infinite in every factor. ## Adding the Stone catalogue

In the three catalogue settings keep the old entrance and add an independent singleton-individual classical terminal at each point of the Stone space \(X\) of the product of representatives of all nontrivial complete Boolean algebras of size below a strong limit \(\mu\). Each relation has an additional locally constant section \(X\to F_\tau\) of whole classical values. At \(r\) the current row is still arbitrary. To bound the ordinal benchmark before choosing \(\mu\), take the candidates specified by an extensional current order/field table on \(D_e(w)\), one arbitrary old \(\rho\)-coordinate and a single constant terminal \(\rho\)-value, where \(\rho=(eet)(et)t\). This collection has size independent of \(X\). For a rigid limit family in it, give a tester taking every input at the root and old entrance and exactly the set of current members’ terminal values, uniformly on \(X\). Persistence and weak inextensibility give the same set at each terminal, so the limit stays constant there. Zero and successor preserve the condition directly. Choose \(\mu\) above this bound. The usual power-closure argument using arbitrary current graphs makes each revised-small algebra have external size below \(\mu\). Its factor of the Stone algebra is a principal scalar interval, with the other coordinates bottom. [Catalogue construction and ordinal bound](stone-terminal-maximalist.html)

Constant terminal sections preserve the surjectivity needed for the Barcan calculation. One modal well-order or selector at the finite classical hierarchy can be used locally on each clopen piece of a given section. All local well-orders and inextensible coextensions are still available at the root, and all classical terminals satisfy Strong Actuality and necessity of all truths. Atomlessness of a scalar interval follows by choosing the factor of any nontrivial complete atomless Boolean algebra of size below \(\mu\). Thus the root fails scalar Atomicity. The unit supported on two isolated terminal atoms is a self-atom at the root, since it is instantiated only at classical terminals, but it has proper nonzero parts. An old-only atom retains its old instantiation and transport failures. These facts give the catalogue verdicts without applying the single-entrance density equivalence to the enlarged product.

## Lifting subrelations over a single entrance with necessary inequalities

Consider only the settings with a single unrestricted old coordinate. Write $h$ for the entrance. Let $F=(E,U)$ be a weakly rigid source relation. Weak persistence gives $h[E]\subseteq U_1$. The source tester $\lambda x.\Box F x$ necessarily accepts every old $E$-member, so weak inextensibility gives hereditary persistence on all later members of $F$. Also, every old tester necessarily accepting $U_1$ lifts with full source row to a tester necessarily accepting $E$. Hence $U$ is weakly rigid.

If old inequalities persist at the old object, each tuple $b\notin h[E]$ is excluded by the tester with full source row and old coordinate $\lambda x.x\ne b$. Thus $U_1=h[E]$. When the old cone satisfies Tame Rigidity, $U$ is rigid, and so is $F$ through every source arrow.

For any rigid $Z\le U$, the relation with source row $E\cap h^{-1}[Z_1]$ and old coordinate $Z$ is a rigid subrelation of $F$. Its source row projects onto $Z_1$. A tester necessarily accepting that source row therefore necessarily accepts every old member of $Z_1$ from the entrance onwards. Weak inextensibility of $Z$ now supplies the full entailment, and persistence follows from that of $Z$. Old weak subrelations are rigid by Tame Rigidity. Thus an arbitrary source power tester which necessarily accepts the current eligible subrelations accepts every old eligible subrelation necessarily. The old power principle gives coverage of all further members. This proves the source powers and, if the old cone has them necessarily, their boxed versions.

The finite and countable S5 ultraproducts and the choiceless full S5 component satisfy the needed hypotheses. Their invertible transports have necessary distinctness and both hereditary powers. Only the free source's construction needs to form the indicated preimage row.
