# Signature Possibility+ and Mathematical Possibilism without Transversal

There is a pointed C model of signature Possibility+ and revised Mathematical Possibilism in which coextension classes of properties of propositions have no transversal. The two choice principles also fail, and some countable family of propositions has no join. Work in ZFC.

## The root and its exits

Fix an old signature Possibility+ point $v$, for example the [signature-definable root](coalesced-definable-root-signature.html). Its individual domain $I$ is the individual domain at the new point. All core arrows act by the identity on $I$. The entrance into $v$ acts by the identity on individuals and absorbs every preceding core arrow. Include its composites with all old arrows. There is also an absorbing entrance to a classical, full, singleton-individual terminal for every point of the Stone space described below.

Take an auxiliary set of $\kappa^+$ equivalence classes of $\kappa^+$ sites each, with class-preserving permutation group $G=\operatorname{Aut}(A,E)$ (classes can be exchanged). A core arrow consists of a partial injection $f$ of sites, a tag $k\in\omega\cup\{\infty\}$ and a word $w$ in a countably infinite alphabet. At a finite tag require $|\operatorname{dom} f|\le k$. Compose partial injections and words and take the minimum of tags. Core rows of relations are equivariant for target postcomposition by $G$. For each profile, their dependence on the arrow factors through $f|_P,\min(k,b),w$ for some $|P|\le\kappa$ and finite $b$. The pin and bound are specific to the profile. [Partial-injection action](bounded-partial-injections.html)

At each relational type add two independent coordinates to the core rows, one whole old entity at $v$ and one locally constant section of the finite discrete terminal carrier $F_\tau$ over the Stone space. Every core transport preserves these two coordinates. The interpretation of a relational name has its prescribed old value, constant terminal value and bottom core rows; interpret individual names by their old values. Parameter transport in the core depends on the common pin, cutoff and word because agreement there persists under left composition. At an old target abstraction is ordinary old abstraction. At terminals the finite list of parameter values is locally constant and all terminals have the same structure. Thus the profiles are closed under typed abstraction, including quantification over the entire target domains. Identity of profiles is detected at an arrow and tuple. This gives the C action model. The old entrance preserves each eligible tuple of individuals and its distinctness from the names in the instance. Signature Possibility+ therefore transfers. [Stone exits and signature transfer](stone-terminal-maximalist.html)

We will use the following fact about current rows. For a set $V$ of tuples invariant under $G_P$, the pointwise stabilizer of $P$, assign core row $\pi[V]$ whenever $f|_P=\pi|_P$ for some $\pi\in G$, and the empty row otherwise. Two matching permutations differ on the right by an element of $G_P$, so this is well defined and equivariant. Take bottom at both kinds of exits. In particular, every subset of a set of at most $\kappa$ typed entities is represented in a current row, since the union of their pins has size at most $\kappa$. This also applies to a graph between two such sets.

## A bound independent of the number of sites

At $\rho=(eet)(et)t$ consider the set $Z$ of profiles with a single extensional truth table on the order and field subsets of $I^2$ and $I$ at every core arrow, arbitrary whole old coordinate, and one constant terminal value. Every member of $Z$ is fixed by every core transport. Its cardinality is at most
$$
B=2^{|\mathcal P(I^2)\times\mathcal P(I)|}\,|D_\rho(v)|\,|F_\rho|.
$$
This estimate precedes the choices of $\kappa$ and the Stone space. The set $Z$ is an admissible current test by the preceding fact for the empty stabilizer pin.

Zero and ordinal successor preserve the test. Every subset of $I$ and every current relation on its elements is represented, since all individuals are fixed by $G$. Hence actual well-orderhood is extensional at every core arrow. Taking the initial segment below a specified point also uses only its current order and field. At every singleton terminal the same calculation yields the same value.

For a rigid family with current member set $U\subseteq Z$, use the tester whose core row is $U$ at every arrow and whose old and terminal coordinates are top. This profile is equivariant since its members are fixed by the core. Every current family member necessarily satisfies it. Inextensibility therefore bounds every core row of the family by $U$, while persistence and fixedness give the reverse inclusion. At terminals instead use a tester with top core and old parts and with constant terminal predicate the set of terminal values of members of $U$. Inextensibility and persistence give equality there as well. The ordinal limit now tests the same extensional table on orders and fields across the core, and has one constant terminal value. Thus $Z$ contains all inexact ordinals by induction under the native three constructors. [Ordinal constructors](stone-terminal-maximalist.html)

Choose an infinite strong limit $\mu>B$, then choose $\kappa\ge\mu$. Let $L$ be the Boolean product of representatives of all nontrivial complete Boolean algebras of cardinality below $\mu$ and use the Stone space of $L$ for the terminals. For a carrier of size at least $\mu$, choose a subset $Y$ of cardinality $\mu$. A single pin fixes $Y$ pointwise together with all actual ordinal codes. All graphs comparing these sets are admitted. Thus $Y$ strictly exceeds the ordinal benchmark. An internally strictly smaller subextension $A\subseteq Y$ has external size $<\mu$, since an external bijection between it and $Y$ would be admitted. Choose inside $Y$ a set of $2^{|A|}$ codes for the subsets of $A$. Both the code set and the membership graph are admitted by the same pin. Consequently $Y$ is power-closed and the original carrier fails revised smallness.

Every revised-small CBA therefore has external size $<\mu$. Every subset of its carrier is admitted, so it is externally complete. Clopens of the terminal space form $L$ under entailment. A proposition with empty core and old parts and terminal part the unit of the corresponding factor has a principal interval isomorphic to the given algebra. The one-element algebra uses bottom. The interval and the given carrier have size $<\mu$ and their isomorphism is an admitted graph. This verifies revised Mathematical Possibilism at every type.

## The missing selections and join

For each site $a$ let $p_a$ be the proposition testing that $f(a)$ is defined, with old and terminal values bottom. The codes are distinct and $\pi p_a=p_{\pi a}$. For each class $C$ there is a property with current row $\{p_a:a\in C\}$, obtained by pinning one member $c$ and taking the row of codes in the class of $f(c)$ whenever that value is defined. A putative classifier with pin $P$ selects a representative $Y$ of this row for a class $C$ disjoint from $P$. Uniqueness fixes $Y$ under the joint stabilizer of $P$ pointwise and $C$ setwise. Enlarge its pin $S$ to include $P$. Move $S$ by such a permutation to $S'$ disjoint from $S$ off $P$, so both pin the same $Y$. The infinite-tag partial identity on $S$ forces the current row on that arrow to remain the codes of $C$. After postcomposition by a permutation fixing $P$ and exchanging $C$ with a fresh class, the restrictions on $S'$ still agree. The rows must coincide by pinning and differ by equivariance. Hence Transversal fails at propositional argument type.

The invariant relation requiring a code to choose a different code in its class, and noncodes to choose themselves, has no choice function. A permutation fixing its pin and a fresh input code could move the chosen classmate. Likewise there is no transversal of the equivalence relation with these code classes and singleton noncode classes. Both current relations exist by the current-row construction.

The propositions supported at exactly the even finite tags, one proposition per tag with bottom at both exits, form an admitted current family. Its indexing graph by standard Frege numerals is invariant, and these numerals are pairwise distinct because there are arbitrarily large finite sets of codes. The invariant current predicate of the standard numerals excludes additional Frege numbers by induction. Each odd-tag complement bounds the family. Its supremum would distinguish all sufficiently large even and odd tags on the empty coefficient and empty word, contrary to its finite cutoff. Finally, given any currently true proposition with pin $P$, the infinite-tag partial identity on $P$ preserves its truth. At this arrow a currently true live-input test for a fresh site is false. No current truth therefore entails every current truth.
