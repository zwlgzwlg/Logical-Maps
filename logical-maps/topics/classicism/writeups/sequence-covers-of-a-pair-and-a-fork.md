# The types and modal scopes of three atom principles

Two covers have Strong Atomicity at every type at the designated root. In the first, scalar atoms stay strong everywhere but an accessible individual-property atom fails selfhood. In the second, every atom at every type is necessarily a self-atom, but an accessible scalar atom fails to be strong.

Both use the [sequence cover of a periodic entrance](sequence-cover-of-periodic-integer-entrance.html). Given an old object $u$, its terminal $t$, and a specified arrow $k:u\to t$, freely adjoin a root $r$ with an arrow $a:r\to u$ and countably many independent arrows $b_n:r\to t$. Include all postcomposites. A root relation consists of an arbitrary current row $R$, an old whole entity $X$ at $u$, and whole entities $Y_n$ at $t$, with $Y_n=kX$ except at finitely many indices. Individuals have the same old-coordinate and sequence specification. Each entrance projects to its corresponding entity. For a finite tuple of parameters the exceptional sets have finite union, so the old lambda abstractions at $b_n$ and $ka$ agree as whole entities off one finite set, uniformly on all newly bound arguments. This and arbitrary current rows verify the root term clauses. Entailment is inclusion in every displayed coordinate. Use the fixed individual and top relations for the names.

## The terminal pair

At $t$ take the classical full type hierarchy on $\{*,0,1\}$. Its two automorphisms are the identity and the swap of $0,1$ fixing $*$. They act on the higher types by direct image. The only other old object $u$ has one individual and one arrow $h:u\to t$, with $\pi h=h$ for the terminal swap. A relation at $u$ consists of an arbitrary current row and an invariant terminal row. The terminal abstract on the transported parameters is invariant, since every transported parameter is fixed by $\pi$ and $\pi$ preserves application and quantification. Thus these are action-model domains. Use $k=h$ in the cover.

At $u$ the individual property with empty current row and terminal row $\{0,1\}$ is an atom. Its two terminal members form a single orbit, so an admitted subproperty has both or neither. At $h$ it is instantiated and split by the terminal singleton $\{0\}$. Thus an accessible atom fails selfhood. Nevertheless the scalar algebra at $u$ consists of two independent bits. Each of its atoms transports to bottom or the sole terminal atom, so every scalar atom is strong. At the terminal, every relational algebra is a powerset. At $u$ each is a powerset of the current tuples and terminal tuple orbits, giving Atomicity at every type.

For density of strong atoms at $r$, use a singleton current row with bottom elsewhere, a singleton current $u$-tuple with terminal bottom and every $b_n$ bottom, or a singleton confined to a single $b_n$-coordinate. The first two kinds disappear outside their indicated current observations. The third yields a singleton under both terminal automorphisms. Each is strong. Any nonempty limit row at $h$ appears at cofinitely many $b_n$, so these cases exhaust the possible nonzero bounds. This proves local Strong Atomicity at every type, necessary Atomicity at every type, and necessary Strong Atomicity at propositional type.

Every entity at $u$ lifts along $a$ and every terminal entity along each $b_n$. At $ha$ any possible witness for a given predicate therefore lifts along a $b_n$ outside the finite exception set of that predicate. This proves local Barcan at every type. It holds for scalars at both old points. The tester at $u$ that accepts all current individual properties and exactly the swap-invariant ones at $t$ shows that relational Barcan fails there.

## The fork with a merged composite

Use the [October three-object fork](full-three-object-fork.html), with old objects $u,s,t$, an arrow $d:u\to s$, and two arrows $p,q:s\to t$ with $pd=qd=k$. All three old individual domains are singletons and the old relation domains contain all profiles. An atom over any old object is a single tuple at a single outgoing arrow $l$. It is instantiated only at $l$. Its transport at $l$ is the current-only singleton, since $ml=l$ forces $m$ to be an identity. Hence all old atoms at every type are self-atoms. The scalar atom at $u$ whose sole true address is $k$ transports along $d$ to the two-address scalar $\{p,q\}$. It therefore fails to be strong.

Again root current singletons and isolated $b_n$ singletons are strong atoms. A singleton in the old $X$-profile confined to $1_u$ or $d$ has bottom $k$-projection and can be taken with all $Y_n$ bottom. Such an entity is strong. At $a$ it is the same one-tuple old atom; at $da$ the second kind becomes a current-only singleton; all remaining proper observations are bottom. A nonempty $k$-row repeats at cofinitely many isolated $b_n$. This proves local Strong Atomicity at all types, and gives necessary Self-Atomicity and necessary selfhood of all atoms, while necessary strength fails even for scalar atoms.

In this version propositional Barcan fails even at $r$. At $u$, any scalar transported along $d$ has equal bits at $p$ and $q$. Take a root tester on scalars with full root row and full $b_n$ rows. Give its $u$ component full rows at $1_u$ and $k$, and at $d$ exactly the $s$ scalars with equal $p,q$ bits. This is an admitted old profile, with full $k$-projection as required. Every root scalar passes it necessarily. At $da$ a scalar true at $p$ and false at $q$ fails it.

There is also a version with local all-type Barcan. Add independent entrances $c_n:r\to s$ and an extra sequence of whole $s$ entities eventually equal to $dX$. Term verification uses the additional finite exception set for each parameter. Every $s$ atom is strong since it is a singleton at $1_s,p$ or $q$ and the latter two end at the terminal. Such an atom can be placed by itself in one $c_n$ coordinate. In the earlier density argument a nonempty $d$-row now occurs at cofinitely many isolated $c_n$ entrances, and all the other cases are unchanged. Each entity at $u,s,t$ lifts at $a,c_n,b_n$ respectively. On $da$ and $ka$ transfer a given tester to one of the respective isolated entrances where it agrees. At $pc_n$ and $qc_n$, any terminal entity lifts by first prescribing its row at $p$ or $q$ in the full $s$-profile and then lifting that profile at $c_n$. This proves local Barcan at every type. Necessary propositional Barcan still fails at the old $u$ by the equal-bit tester.
