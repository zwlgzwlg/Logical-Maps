# Mathematical Possibilism with Functional Choice and infinite individuals

The [mixed reduced-power retraction](mixed-reduced-power-retraction.html) can have necessarily infinitely many individuals and can realize every complete Boolean algebra below a prescribed cardinal as an actual principal interval. Its proposition algebra still has a countable family without a join. The construction uses ZFC.

## The Boolean quotient

Fix the root individual set $\mathbb N$ and the target individual set $\mathbb N\sqcup\{\star\}$. All entrance maps are the standard inclusion, and the return is the identity on $\mathbb N$ and sends $\star$ to $0$. Choose an infinite strong-limit cardinal $\mu>2^{2^{\aleph_0}}$. One may make $\mu$ exceed any further prescribed cardinal before constructing the algebra. Let $L$ be the product of representatives of all nontrivial complete Boolean algebras of size less than $\mu$, up to isomorphism. This is a set-sized complete Boolean algebra.

Write $A=\operatorname{Ult}(L)$. The Stone embedding sends $L$ into $\mathcal P(A)$. The inverse on this embedded copy extends to a Boolean homomorphism $\pi:\mathcal P(A)\to L$ by Sikorski's extension theorem. Explicitly, for a partial extension $u:D\to L$ and a new subset $X\subseteq A$ choose
$$
b=\bigvee\{u(d): d\in D,\ d\subseteq X\}.
$$
This lies below every $u(d')$ with $X\subseteq d'$ and is disjoint from every $u(d')$ with $d'\cap X=\varnothing$. These two compatibility bounds give the extension on the algebra generated by $D\cup\{X\}$, sending $(d_1\cap X)\cup(d_2\setminus X)$ to $(u(d_1)\wedge b)\vee(u(d_2)\wedge\neg b)$. Zorn's lemma extends the map to all subsets. It is onto because it retains the inverse on the embedded copy. For $K=\ker\pi$ this identifies $\mathcal P(A)/K$ with $L$ as an ordered Boolean algebra. The proof uses finite Boolean operations of the homomorphism. [Sikorski's homomorphism extension theorem](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-symbolic-logic/article/abs/on-the-strength-of-the-sikorski-extension-theorem-for-boolean-algebras/CAF32A78ECEA8302980A05658C124A08)

Take a disjoint countably infinite set $B$. Use the ideal $J$ of subsets $X$ of $A\sqcup B$ such that $X\cap A\in K$ and $X\cap B$ is finite. At each relational sort quotient whole entrance-row sequences by equality outside a set in $J$. Use exactly the iterated stages of the mixed model, with the individual maps above and with all ultrafilters disjoint from $J$ available, chosen independently at each outer index. Any $J$-positive set belongs to one such ultrafilter: its class in the quotient is nonzero and extends to an ultrafilter there. This gives precisely the separation of distinct row classes used in the term and identity proofs. Finite unions of exceptional sets still belong to $J$, giving the required uniformity in bound arguments. In particular the root proposition algebra is
$$
\mathbf2\times L\times(\mathcal P(B)/\mathrm{Fin}).
$$

## Ordinals and small algebras

The countable $B$ part still forces every weakly rigid root relation to have finite current extension. Indeed its free ultrafilters disjoint from $J$ make each fixed old member occur on cofinitely many $B$-indices. If the current row were infinite, choose the escaping diagonal from the [finite-bound proof](reduced-power-retraction.html) on $B$ and leave every $A$-row of its tester full. The common left inverse prevents a fixed old tuple from being excluded infinitely often. All old tuples necessarily pass; a first free $B$-entrance omits a member of the bound. This contradiction applies at all argument types, independently of the cardinality of $A$.

At the inexact ordinal type let $Z$ be the entities having an extensional Boolean table on current order/field arguments at $r$ and a single extensional Boolean table at $s$ on every entrance outside a $J$-small set. On a countable individual set there are $2^{\aleph_0}$ pairs of order/field extensions, so at most $2^{2^{\aleph_0}}$ Boolean tables. A pair of such tables determines one entire root entity. Thus $|Z|\le 2^{2^{\aleph_0}}$, calculated before any use of $L$'s cardinality. $Z$ is available as the current extension of an induction tester.

Ordinal zero and successor preserve the two-table condition by the extensional well-order and initial-segment computations in the [countably many sections model](countably-many-sections.html). At a rigid limit, the actual family in $Z$ is finite by the $B$-diagonal just proved. It equals the union of its actual members' haecceities by persistence and weak inextensibility. Its entrance rows consequently are the transported finite list outside one $J$-small set. Removing also the finitely many $J$-small exceptions of the listed members, a single conjunction of their common target tables and the well-order test computes the target limit. The current limit is extensional as well. This gives $H\subseteq Z$, so the ordinal benchmark has size less than $\mu$.

With all current rows unrestricted, a carrier of size at least $\mu$ has a subset $Y$ of size $\mu$, strictly larger than $H$. For every $D\subseteq Y$ of cardinality $\lambda<\mu$, choose $2^\lambda<\mu$ members of $Y$ and a bijection from these code points to the subsets of $D$. The incidence graph realizes the required power code. Consequently every revised-small complete Boolean algebra has size less than $\mu$. Its coordinate in $L$ gives the required principal ideal; the one-element algebra is realized below zero. The isomorphism graph is an admitted current row. This proves all instances of revised Mathematical Possibilism.

## The other verdicts

The choice, inextensibility and power proofs of the mixed model use $jh_i=1$, all free ultrafilters on $B$, and unconstrained row prescriptions. All three remain available. In particular Functional Choice and Gallin Extensional Comprehension hold, as do necessary Tame Rigidity and both necessary powers. Barcan fails at every input sort. At $e$ the old image excludes $\star$. At relational types a new current row above a fixed old return coordinate separates a target from the old images. A countable partition into infinite subsets of $B$ gives the same missing join as before, and the same fully inextensible root bounds must be finite. Thus Countable Boolean Completeness and Inextensible Comprehension fail.

At both base kinds the individual and proposition carriers are infinite. The full current extensions force the Frege numbers to be the standard numeral denotations there and realize every standard finite size. By Łoś both infinity axioms and their Peano guards hold throughout. The infinite root number rows cannot be weakly rigid, so Arithmetic Necessitism fails both at $e$ and at $t$. A standard real structure exists at the root proposition type, but its infinite number row prevents any rigid real structure there. Both analytic necessitisms fail. The two base kinds agree on the guarded sentence-level theories of arithmetic and second-order analysis by the same full-current-extension argument.

The calculations for strong actuality, self-atoms and two-hat necessity pass unchanged. For a root atom that splits, choose an atom of $L$, for example the unit supported on the coordinate of the two-element Boolean algebra. A representing subset of $A$ belongs to some ultrafilter disjoint from $J$. At that entrance the atom is true but also has its nonzero return coordinate; it is no longer an atom. The $B$-factor contains nonzero elements with no atom below them. Well-orders at the two bases yield a necessary well-order as before. A single propositional name may be chosen outside all the countably many closed pure denotations and transported along each history.
