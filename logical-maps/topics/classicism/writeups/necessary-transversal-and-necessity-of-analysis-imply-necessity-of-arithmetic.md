# Necessary Transversal and Necessity of Analysis imply Necessity of Arithmetic

Fix a counting type $\sigma$, let $\nu=(\sigma t)t$ be its Frege-cardinality type and let $\tau=\nu t$. Fix an arithmetical sentence $A$ counted at $\sigma$. Denote by $A^\dagger$ its number-only analytical translation, using the number sort, zero, successor, addition and multiplication of a real number structure. The type $\tau$ and the sentence $A^\dagger$ are independent of the evaluation point.

At any accessible point satisfying the guard $I_\sigma$, its Peano form $I^*_\sigma$ holds by the equivalence in C. Write $N$ for the canonical finite cardinalities counted at $\sigma$. For $n\in N$ use $h_n=\lambda m^\nu.(m=n)$ as a number of type $\tau$. Application at $n$ gives injectivity. Use $h_0$ for zero and pair $h_n$ with $h_{Sn}$ for successor. Pullback by $h$ takes inductive predicates to inductive predicates, so this is a Peano system, including for nonstandard current numbers.

At this point necessary Transversal provides a representative for each class of coextensive properties on $\nu$. Use as reals the selected properties whose current extensions are contained in $N$. Put $h_n$ in the membership relation to a selected real $x$ just when $xn$. Two such reals with the same members agree everywhere and their selection gives identity. For any admitted property $Y$ of elements of type $\tau$, the representative of $\lambda n.(Nn\land Y(h_n))$ shows fullness. This is a real number structure at $\tau$. Under the bijection $n\mapsto h_n$, comparison with zero and successor is immediate. Addition corresponds because both graphs satisfy the same recursion, with uniqueness by Peano induction on the second argument. Multiplication corresponds by its recursion using addition. Equivalently the inductive relations defining the graphs pull back and push forward along the bijection by lambda abstraction. All number-sorted terms denote numbers. Induction on the remaining syntax, including bounded number quantification by surjectivity of the bijection, now identifies the truth of $A$ with that of $A^\dagger$ in this structure.

At the designated point apply Necessity of Analysis to $A^\dagger$ at $\tau$. Either it holds in every real number structure at all accessible points, or its negation does. At each accessible point satisfying $I_\sigma$ the structure just constructed transfers that same sign to $A$. Thus $\Box(I_\sigma\to A)\lor\Box(I_\sigma\to\neg A)$. Vary $A$ and $\sigma$ to obtain the full schema.
