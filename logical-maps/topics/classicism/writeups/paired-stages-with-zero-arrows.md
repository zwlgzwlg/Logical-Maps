# Paired stages with zero arrows

At the designated point, Logical Necessity and revised Mathematical Possibilism both hold. There is a 5-necessary proposition that fails two-hat necessity and a two-hat necessary proposition that fails distinctness-preserving necessity. The same two separations occur at every stage.

## The action

Start with the external coordinate sets and block-respecting maps of the [integer stages of paired individuals](integer-stages-paired-individuals-glued-constants.html). Each stage $n\in\mathbb Z$ has a coordinate for each $i\in\mathbb Z$. Each coordinate has $\omega_1$ doublets, and has an additional singleton just when $i\le n$. Call a coordinate-preserving map taking each block into a block an ordinary coefficient. Adjoin one further coefficient $0_{nm}$ for each source $n$ and target $m$. In any composable pair a zero coefficient gives the zero coefficient with the resulting endpoints. Independently decorate **every** coefficient, including each zero, with a finite word. Composition of $(g,w)$ followed by $(h,v)$ has coefficient $hg$ and word $vw$.

The individual type has just one member everywhere. The external coordinate sets serve only to index maps and their restrictions. For a relational type at $n$ take profiles giving a set of argument tuples at the target of each decorated arrow. Require a countable set $S$ of external points at $n$ such that ordinary coefficients agreeing on $S$ with the same word and target have equal rows. At a zero coefficient each word and target has an unrestricted row. A proposition has a truth value for its row. Transport precomposes profiles. The image of $S$ pins the transport by an ordinary coefficient. Transport by a zero has empty pin, since all continuations with a common target and word now give the same composite. Furthermore, two ordinary coefficients agreeing on a parameter's pin transport its **whole entity** equally, by comparison on every continuation. Thus the union of the parameter pins works for each lambda term, by term induction including the quantifiers at the target. This gives the typed action semantics of C. Take the identity at stage 0.

Every external current row is represented by a profile using that row on all arrows with the chosen target, and empty rows elsewhere. Translation by one on stage and coordinate labels preserves the pure interpretation. To interpret any set-sized admissible signature, fix an alphabet of size $\max(\aleph_0,|\Sigma|)$. In the pure reduct choose at stage 0 one realizing tuple for each realizable pure matrix and ordered type list. Give each instance with matching distinct constant names a private last letter. On the cylinder ending in this letter give each participating relational constant the witnessing profile with the final letter removed, on ordinary and zero coefficients alike. Give unused rows the empty value. There are only countably many matrices and ordered type lists, so each constant uses only countably many witness pins, even when the signature is uncountable. An individual name always denotes the unique individual. The loop with that letter realizes the named tuple. Conversely, any pure matrix that holds of the names somewhere is existentially witnessed at 0 by translation. This proves Logical Necessity. The rooted unfolding with transported names gives the equivalent presentation by coherent sections.

## Mathematical Possibilism and the ordinary Barcan formula

Since there is one individual everywhere, the inexact ordinal benchmark has at most the three closed thresholds for a well-order, a nonempty well-order, and impossibility. Successor stays within these thresholds. Persistence and a tester for the finite disjunction of the current thresholds pin down the future members of any rigid subfamily, so its limit is again a threshold. The finite set of thresholds is an available current induction predicate. Current fullness supplies all subsets and injection and coding graphs. Any infinite carrier contains a countably infinite subcollection strictly larger than the benchmark, and its strictly smaller subsets are finite with finite powersets. Thus every revised-small algebra is finite. This is the bounded-individual argument of the map. The interval below their union is exactly their powerset, with the order isomorphism available as a current row. The empty choice covers the one-element algebra. This proves Mathematical Possibilism at every stage.

For ordinary Barcan at type $t$, fix one zero exit $a$ with empty word. Use a tester full everywhere except at $a$, where it accepts exactly the image under $a$ of the old proposition domain. It necessarily accepts every old proposition. Every proposition in that image has the same value on all continuations with the same word and target. An available target proposition distinguishes two ordinary endomorphisms by the image of one external point. Thus the tester misses a target proposition at $a$, refuting Barcan.

## The two separations

Use the two pure existential tests of the [integer-stage argument](integer-stages-paired-individuals-glued-constants.html). The adjustments of the guards at zero arrows are as follows.

Let $P$ be true on **all** zero arrows and on exactly the ordinary arrows ending at stages $m<1$. Give a property $Y$ of propositions full rows there. On ordinary arrows ending at $m\ge1$ let its row consist of the propositions admitting a pin that omits the singleton in coordinate 1. These rows use empty pin. At $m\ge1$ the proposition testing whether an ordinary endomorphism fixes this singleton has no pin omitting it, since moving just the singleton changes the test. Therefore $\forall z\,Yz=P$ as whole proposition entities.

Given $z$ at 0, extend its pin by partners in coordinate 1 and call the result $N$. Take the true guard consisting of the **ordinary** empty-word endomorphisms fixing $N$. An arrow at which the guard is distinctness-preservingly possible admits a return meeting the guard. Both arrows must have ordinary coefficient. Their composite fixes $N$, so the outgoing coefficient is injective on $N$. The two points of a pinned pair in coordinate 1 cannot have gone to a singleton. The transported $z$ therefore has a pin omitting that singleton wherever its omission is required. This proves two-hat necessity of $Yz$, hence its 5-necessity, for every $z$. The 5-Barcan theorem of C gives $\Box_5 P$.

For failure of two-hat necessity take the ordinary inclusion from 0 to 1. For any true $q$ at 0 and true $r$ at 1 choose a doublet in coordinate 1 away from a pin for $r$. Return to 0 by moving the new singleton into that doublet, and next go to 1 by collapsing the doublet back to the singleton. The first composite from 0 is the identity, so it satisfies $q$. The second composite from 1 is ordinary, has empty word and fixes the pin of $r$, so it satisfies $r$. Thus every true $q$ is distinctness-preservingly possible at the inclusion, where $P$ is false.

Let $T$ hold on every zero arrow and on the ordinary arrows ending at $m\ge0$. The true guard of ordinary empty-word endomorphisms fixing the coordinate-0 singleton $a$ witnesses its two-hat necessity. At a false arrow the image of $a$ is in a doublet. Any return to the guard collapses this doublet onto $a$, so the true test demanding that both members be fixed can never be met on the following return. For distinctness-preserving necessity itself, given a true $r$ at 0 choose a doublet in coordinate 0 off its countable pin. Send $a$ into it on the ordinary exit to $-1$ and collapse the doublet back onto $a$ on return. The composite fixes the pin and satisfies $r$, although $T$ fails at the exit. This defeats every candidate guard. Translation gives both strict separations at every stage.

## Completeness, actuality and analysis

An internally countable family is externally at most countable, since the set of denotations of the standard numerals is a current inductive row. The union of the pins of that family's members pins their pointwise join, giving necessary Countable Boolean Completeness at all relational types. Assign an external well-order of each local domain to all arrows with its target for Modal Well-Ordering.

The whole ordinary sector has a nonzero atomless interval. Any nonzero proposition in that sector contains an ordinary arrow and one may change the image of a doublet outside its pin. Testing the image of one member splits the proposition. In particular every currently true proposition admits such a split at the identity, refuting Actuality. Full Boolean Completeness at $t$ fails by the uncountable family of cylinders of the integer-stage argument. Restrict all cylinders to ordinary arrows. Any proposed join admits a countable ordinary pin, so the two ordinary endomorphisms from that argument agreeing on its pin still give opposite required answers.

Every relational type has a continuum of codes, indexed by subsets $A\subseteq\mathbb Z$. The code at stage $n$ has full row just on arrows whose target belongs to $A$. All stages are reachable, so each transport preserves these labels distinctly. Arbitrary sets of tuples of codes have target-only relations whose current rows map bijectively to the corresponding future rows. The same is true of the family of all such relations on each finite power of a chosen set of codes. These exact-image relations are rigid: every future member is the image there of a current one, after any further transport. On the codes choose the ordinary numbers and their full powerset with successor and membership. Its rigid powers represent every local restriction at every stage. At individual type a real number structure is impossible. This gives Second-Order Analytic Necessitism. [The represented-power argument](symmetric-integer-paired-stages.html)

At each stage every external subset of a current domain and every graph on it has a current representative. Consequently in any candidate real number structure the standard successor orbit exhausts its number part by the induction axiom; its set part is exactly the full powerset of that orbit by fullness and extensionality. Likewise full current relations give the ordinary semantics for the higher quantifiers on this structure by restriction and extension. All such structures agree on second-order analytical truth at all stages.
