# Maximalism with two distinct left inverses

OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 26 November 2026.

Modify the [Stone model](stone-terminal-maximalist.html) at the root only. In place of an identity loop or an involution, use the countable monoid M of maps on the natural numbers generated by s(n) = n+1 and l₀,l₁ with lᵢ(n+1)=n and lᵢ(0)=i. Composition is ordinary function composition. Both l₀ and l₁ are left inverses of s, whereas s has no right inverse. The natural numbers here index external endomorphisms. Root individuals can still be a singleton.

Take the designated symmetry subgroup to be trivial. Each exit to an old component or to a Stone terminal, including all its continuations, absorbs precomposition by any root loop. For a flattened relation at the root, take an arbitrary row on the target argument domains at each m ∈ M, alongside the old and continuous terminal coordinates. Transport of profiles along a root loop is right translation on row indices and leaves the external coordinates fixed. Evaluation uses transported arguments for partial applications. The term-closure proof is the same as for the involution: the new rows are unrestricted at every loop, transport composes by associativity, and leaf operations are functions of a finite tuple of parameters in the homogeneous finite structure. Use the identity individual entrance to the signature-definable component. All its Possibility+ witnesses remain available. A second pure Barcan-failing branch can be included as before.

Require a member of the ordinal closure test Z to depend only on actual input extensions at *each* loop row and to have a constant terminal value. This is closed under every right translation. For rigid Y with actual extension E at the identity, its row on loop m is precisely the direct image m[E]. Persistence gives inclusion from right to left. For the other inclusion take a test predicate with row m[E] at m and top everywhere else. Every member of E necessarily satisfies the test. Weak inextensibility of Y therefore gives the desired inclusion at m, even when m lacks an inverse. Thus every member encountered by OrdLim in a loop row still belongs to Z. The terminal separator and successor arguments go through unchanged. The bound on the number of possible extensional row assignments is the previous per-row bound raised to the power |M|. This cardinal, and the size of the independent old coordinates, are fixed before choosing μ. Revised MP holds.

Let a₀ be the whole proposition supported just at the identity row. It is true and is below every true proposition in the root order. If p is any true proposition, the transport of p along s is true at both l₀ and l₁. A proposition with a single row at l₀ witnesses that this transport entails neither it nor its complement. Thus no currently true proposition is a strong atom, proving failure of Strong Actuality itself.

For any root proposition p, the root truth of □≠p is equivalent to p holding on every left-invertible root loop. Indeed a₀ witnesses sufficiency in the definition ∃q(q ∧ □(◇q → p)): its transport can possibly be true after m precisely when some h satisfies hm=1, and no exterior component returns. Conversely any true q can return to truth after every such m. This characterization applies also to every transported parameter at the root object.

## Which truths are two-hat necessary?

For any proposition $p$, two-hat necessity tests exactly the unit loops. To see sufficiency, take the identity-only proposition $a_0$ for the true guard in
$\exists q\,(q\land\Box(\Diamond_{\ne}q\to p))$.
At an arbitrary loop $g$, the transported $a_0$ is $\Diamond_{\ne}$-possible exactly when some left-invertible $h$ has $hg=1$. This follows by dualizing the preceding characterization of $\Box_{\ne}$ at the root object. Such an $h$ has a left inverse and has $g$ as a right inverse, so $h$ and $g$ are units. The guard is impossible at every external exit. Thus the ordinary box in the displayed formula holds whenever $p$ is true on all units. Conversely, for any unit $g$ and any true guard $q$, the inverse of $g$ is left-invertible and returns $q$ to truth. The displayed box therefore requires $p$ at $g$.

The monoid used here has only the identity unit. Cancel each occurrence of $l_i s$ in a generator word. The remaining word starts with a power of $s$ followed by zero or more of the $l_i$. If any $l_i$ remains, the rightmost generator is noninjective. If only a positive power of $s$ remains, the composite misses $0$. Thus every current truth is two-hat necessary.

In particular $a_0$ is two-hat necessary and hence 5-necessary. It fails distinctness-preserving necessity because $s$ has a left inverse and $a_0$ is false there. This separates 5-necessity from distinctness-preserving necessity even with signature Possibility+, revised Mathematical Possibilism, and two-hat necessity of every truth at the same point.
