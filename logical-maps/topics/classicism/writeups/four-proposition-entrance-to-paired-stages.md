# Four propositions at an entrance to the paired stages

Atomicity at type $t$ can hold while a later point separates 5-necessity from two-hat necessity and two-hat necessity from ND-necessity. In this example the entrance has just four propositions. It has a true strong atom, full current rows at every type, and propositional Boolean Completeness.

## The old component

Use the integer-stage model on the paired coefficient sets. At stage $n$ the coefficient labels in coordinate $i$ form disjoint doublets, with an additional singleton when $i\le n$. Ordinary arrows are coordinate-preserving, block-respecting maps with free word labels. There is one actual individual. Each higher-type profile has arbitrary rows subject to a common countable pin in the source coefficient labels. One may take the version with additional zero arrows at every pair of stages. Its zero rows are independent of the ordinary rows. Translating $n$ and $i$ by one gives an automorphism of the pure model and preserves the unique individual. [Integer stages with zero arrows](paired-stages-with-zero-arrows.html)

At an old point a proposition can be 5-necessary without being two-hat necessary. Another can be two-hat necessary without being ND-necessary. The first test distinguishes stages depending on whether the designated coordinate has a singleton. Pinning both partners of any coefficient label bars a map that collapses them to that singleton, and the Barcan theorem for the 5-operator then gives 5-necessity. The inclusion of a new singleton coordinate defeats every two-hat guard. The second test uses a return that forgets where a fresh pair maps, defeating ND-necessity for a proposition with a two-hat guard. Only the old component is used to evaluate these tests. [Proof of both separations](integer-stages-paired-individuals-glued-constants.html)

## The entrance

Prepend an object $r$ with just its identity loop, an arrow $u$ to the old stage $0$, and the composites $hu$ for all old arrows $h$ out of $0$. Make the latter a separate copy of the old arrows, with composition given by old postcomposition. The old objects have no arrows back to $r$.

The individual at $r$ maps to the unique old individual. For each relational type, a root entity consists of an arbitrary current row on its argument domains at $r$ together with the value at $0$ of one closed term of that type in the old pure language with a marker for its unique individual. Root entities with the same two coordinates are identical. Transport along $u$ projects to the old coordinate, and transport along $hu$ then applies $h$. Take any nonlogical relational names in the original signature to denote top; individual names denote the unique individual. No signature principle is required here.

These domains admit the typed terms. For a lambda with root parameters, the current row is the extension of its body. On the old side, simultaneously substitute closed descriptors for its parameters, including the individual marker where necessary. This gives one closed old descriptor for the whole abstract. Quantification at an old point still ranges over its original domains. The same induction applies to the logical operations and their modal formulas. Present row and old trace distinguish every two distinct relational entities through some argument and continuation. This gives a C model. [The descriptor-root construction](coalesced-definable-root.html)

Every closed old proposition has the same truth at all old stages, since translations preserve the interpretation and the sole individual marker. Its transport to any target is the denotation of that same closed term. Thus its entire old propositional profile is top or bottom. Both occur among the descriptors, independently of either current bit. The propositional algebra at $r$ is therefore $2\times 2$. Its two atoms are the proposition true only here and the proposition true throughout the old cone. The first is a true strong atom. The second transports to top, which at the old stage can be split by newly available propositions.

The old stages and all their future domains retain their original semantics, so the two modal separations persist there. At $r$ this refutes the necessary implication from 5-necessity to two-hat necessity, the necessary implication from two-hat necessity to ND-necessity, and consequently the necessary implication from 5-necessity to ND-necessity.
