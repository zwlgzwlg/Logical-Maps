# Revised Mathematical Possibilism in the singular lottery

The [specified lottery cloud](modalized-lottery-cloud.html) fails the revised Mathematical Possibilism schema at its isolated root, both at $t$ and at $t\to t$. This concerns the strong-limit lottery of Definition 6.4 in the draft of 20 October 2026. Theorem 6.8 there proves representation for the earlier smallness test. ## The ordinal benchmark has size at least $\mu$

Write $r$ for the root, $H$ for its identity classes satisfying the inductively defined inexact ordinal predicate, and $T$ for its proposition carrier. The actual individuals are $\omega$. The full lottery contains a factor isomorphic to every nontrivial complete Boolean algebra of size below the strong limit $\mu$. At the root every subset and relation on the relevant identity classes is available as a current extension. The root proposition algebra is the product of the root bit and the lottery factors, of cardinality $\tau=2^\mu$ (Lemmas 6.5–6.7).

By external recursion construct an actual ordinal threshold $h_\alpha$ for each $\alpha<\mu$. Zero tests well-orderhood of the supplied order and field. At a successor use the ordinal constructor that asks whether a strict initial segment satisfies the preceding threshold. At a nonzero limit $\lambda$ take the rigid check-closure $Y_\lambda$ of the earlier root entities and apply the limit constructor to it. Definition 3.11 and Theorem 3.12 give a root-existing rigid family whose current extension consists exactly of the earlier identity classes. The three clauses of the impredicative ordinal definition therefore place every $h_\alpha$ in $H$. This recursion chooses model entities externally. [Ordinal constructors](https://github.com/andrew-bacon/Logical-Maps/blob/82133f4969047baa6ce02700483c9741c5857699/logical-maps/topics/classicism/verification/finite-power-ch/Classicism/Results/MathematicalContingency/InductiveOrdinals.lean)

On an atomless forcing component $D$, evaluate at any condition where the order and field exist. The value of $h_\alpha(R,A)$ is the Boolean value of the assertion that the pair is a well-order of length at least $\check\alpha$. For zero this uses the full Boolean-valued matrices for subsets and relations on $\omega$: the well-order test sees every subset in the Boolean-valued universe. Names for naturals are locally decided. The successor test thus takes the join over the possible cut points and uses the actual restricted field and relation at each cut.

At a limit, membership of an existing candidate $v$ in the check-family has value $\bigvee_{\gamma<\lambda}\|v=h_\gamma\|$. On each equality piece the value of the application of $v$ agrees with that of $h_\gamma$, by substitution. Consequently the meet of the values of the earlier applications implies every guarded $v$-instance in the limit test. In the opposite direction specialize to each $h_\gamma$ itself. The universal conjunct has exactly the meet of the earlier values, also for a mixed name that agrees with different $h_\gamma$ on different pieces. With the well-order conjunct this proves the induction. The same calculation can be restricted to the condition of local existence throughout.

For $\alpha<\beta<\mu$, some factor forces an order of type $\check\alpha$ on a subset of $\omega$. For countable $\alpha$ choose a ground order; otherwise use the Boolean completion of the poset of finite partial functions from $\omega$ to $\alpha$. Its generic union maps onto $\check\alpha$, and the completion has at most $2^{|\alpha|}<\mu$ elements. In that component the two thresholds give truth values one and zero on the same input. Root identity persists and respects application, so $h_\alpha\ne h_\beta$ at the root. This proves $\mu\le |H|$. The thresholds for $\omega_1\le\alpha<\mu$ all have empty current extension at $r$; it is their component values that separate them.

## A witness at propositional type

Let $D$ now be the completion of the finite partial functions $\omega\to\mu$. It has size at most $\tau$. Below every basic condition, splitting at a fresh coordinate gives $\mu$ disjoint nonzero refinements. Joins of subfamilies give $\tau$ elements below that condition. Hence every nonzero principal interval of $D$ has size $\tau$, including $D$ itself. Transfer this algebra to a carrier of root propositions of size $\tau$ by a bijection. Its order is an admitted current relation, and root fullness makes it a complete Boolean algebra in the object language.

It is revised-small. If a power-closed subcollection $Y$ were strictly larger than $H$, it would have a strictly smaller subset of size $\mu$. Exact coding of all its subextensions would require a subset of $Y$ of size $2^\mu$ strictly smaller than $Y$, impossible in this carrier. The cardinal arithmetic here uses the surjectivity and uniqueness clauses in the map's power codes and the availability of *all* current subextensions. [Power coding](https://github.com/andrew-bacon/Logical-Maps/blob/82133f4969047baa6ce02700483c9741c5857699/logical-maps/topics/classicism/verification/finite-power-ch/Classicism/Results/MathematicalContingency/RepresentedPower.lean)

Every nonzero principal interval of the lottery proposition algebra contains a nonzero principal subinterval supported on just one nonzero coordinate. That subinterval has size below $\mu$, as do the component algebras and the root bit. An order isomorphism preserves principal subintervals, so none can represent $D$. The zero interval is trivial.

## A larger witness by cardinality alone

For every $S\subseteq T$ use the property of propositions that outputs the isolated-root proposition on $S$ and bottom elsewhere. It is locally bottom throughout the cloud. Different subsets give different root identity classes. Root fullness admits both the carrier of these codes at type $t\to t$ and the graph of subset inclusion, presenting $\mathcal P(T)$ of size $2^\tau$.

If a power-closed $Y$ in this carrier had size strictly greater than $|H|$, the same test at a $\mu$-element subset would force $|Y|>\tau$. Applying it again to a $\tau$-element subset would force $|Y|>2^\tau$, impossible. Thus this algebra too is revised-small. Every propositional principal interval has size at most $\tau$, so it cannot represent $\mathcal P(T)$.
