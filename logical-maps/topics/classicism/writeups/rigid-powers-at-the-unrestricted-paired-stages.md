# Both rigid powers at the unrestricted paired stages

The integer stages with unrestricted maps on pairs satisfy necessary Rigid Power and necessary Weakly Rigid Power. Both conclusions also hold with absorbing zero coefficients and with the designated constants interpreted as top. The proof uses the countable-pin description of rigid relations. [Integer stages](integer-stages-paired-individuals-glued-constants.html) · [Zero coefficients](paired-stages-with-zero-arrows.html) · [Tame Rigidity at the paired stages](tame-rigidity-at-the-paired-stages.html)

Fix a stage. Write $E$ for the current extension of a rigid $F$. A pin $N$ can always be enlarged to a countable union of source blocks containing all singletons. At an ordinary arrow $h$, let $B_N(E)_h$ be the union of $d[E]$ over ordinary $d$ with the target and word of $h$ and the same values on $N$. At a zero arrow $h$, put $B_N(E)_h=h[E]$. The Tame Rigidity proof gives $F=B_N(E)$ whenever $N$ pins $F$. In particular $E$ is closed under empty-word endomorphisms fixing $N$.

We also need the converse. If $A$ is any current tuple set closed under the empty-word endomorphisms fixing $C$, then $B_C(A)$ has current extension $A$ and is weakly persistent. A tester necessarily covering $A$ covers its ordinary rows by the countable factorization in the Tame Rigidity proof, taking the first arrow to be the identity. The adjusted first arrow fixes $C$ and carries the chosen tuple back into $A$. At a zero arrow exact images give the same inclusion directly. This gives weak inextensibility. Tame Rigidity therefore makes $B_C(A)$ rigid.

Let $Z$ necessarily hold of every current rigid subrelation of $F$. Enlarge $N$ to pin both $Z$ and $F$. At any ordinary $h$, take a rigid $X\le hF$ and a block-closed pin $T$ of $X$ containing the target singletons. Build an arrow $j$ with the word of $h$, agreeing with $h$ on $N$, as follows. Enlarge $T\cup h[N]$ to a countable union $D$ of target blocks. Reserve a different fresh source doublet in the corresponding coordinate for every block of $D$, including its singletons. On each reserved doublet let $j$ cover the designated block. Let $C$ consist of $N$ and the reserved doublets. Outside $C$ use a block isomorphism to the complement of $D$. Each coordinate has uncountably many ordinary doublets and all singletons are accounted for. The map part of $j$ has a block-respecting section.

Write $V$ for the current row of $X$. At $j$, the saturation row of $F$ equals $j[E]$. Indeed, if $d$ agrees with $j$ on $N$, factor $d=ju$ with $u$ the identity on $N$ and a section of $j$ composed with $d$ elsewhere. Here $u$ has empty word. Thus $u[E]\subseteq E$. Also $jF=hF$ as whole entities by pinning, so $V\subseteq j[E]$.

Take $A=E\cap j^{-1}[V]$, using the typed tuple transport of $j$. Every empty-word endomap $u$ fixing $C$ fits a square $ju=vj$, where $v$ fixes $D$ and $v(jx)=j(ux)$ outside $D$. This is well defined by the block isomorphism there. Then $u[E]\subseteq E$ and $v[V]\subseteq V$ by persistence and pinning. Hence $A$ is closed under these $u$, and $Y=B_C(A)$ is rigid. The inclusion $C\supseteq N$ gives $Y\le F$, including at zeros by exact images. The same section factorization, now fixing $C$, gives $Y_j=j[A]=V$. The rigid relations $jY,X$ with that current row are equal, since each necessarily contains the present members of the other and each is weakly inextensible. Thus $Z_j$ accepts $X$, and so does $Z_h$ by its pin. This ordinary-arrow argument is also given for the equivariant submodel. [Symmetric integer stages](symmetric-integer-paired-stages.html)

For a zero $h$ one already has $F_h=h[E]$. Given a rigid $X\le hF$ with current row $V$, take $A=E\cap h^{-1}[V]$. Every empty-word ordinary endomap $u$ fixing $N$ sends $E$ into $E$ and satisfies $hu=h$. This proves the closure of $A$. The rigid $B_N(A)$ lies below $F$ and has row $h[A]=V$ at $h$. Rigid coextension again gives $hB_N(A)=X$.

This proves weak inextensibility for the property of being a rigid subrelation of $F$. Its membership persists because rigidity and necessary inclusion persist. Tame Rigidity upgrades it to rigidity, and identifies weakly rigid subrelations with rigid subrelations at every stage. Both power principles follow at every stage.
