# Isolated entrances and necessary Actuality

A root with an unrestricted present row and a single entrance into an old model can have Strong Actuality while a future point has no true atom. Two choices of the old model give different controls on necessity. Truncations preserve necessary Atomicity and make every truth ND-necessary at every point. The symmetric paired stages preserve the necessary Barcan Formula and make every truth 5-necessary at every point, while both converse comparisons between 5-, two-hat and ND-necessity fail at accessible points.

## The entrance

Fix an old pointed action model with distinguished object $s$. Add an object $r$ with its identity, an entrance $h:r\to s$ and distinct composites $kh$ indexed by the old arrows $k$ out of $s$. Composition uses old composition. Set the root individual carrier equal to the one at $s$, with $h$ acting as the identity on it. Recursively, a root relation has the form $(E,U)$, where $E$ is any subset of the current root argument tuples and $U$ is any whole old entity of that relational type at $s$. At $h$ it becomes $U$. This includes nullary relations, with an arbitrary current truth bit for $E$. For a fixed signature, choose old interpretations at $s$ and root relations with those old coordinates. The witnesses below are pure.

The root row of a lambda abstraction is computed on every root input. Its old coordinate is the old lambda abstraction evaluated at the transported parameters, with the quantifiers ranging over all old inputs. There are no arrows from old objects to $r$. This gives the expected truth at all composites and preserves ordinary intensional identity. The entailment ordering at a relational type is the product of current inclusion and old entailment.

The proposition supported only at $1_r$ is a true atom which becomes bottom after every proper arrow. It witnesses Strong Actuality. Its possibility also suffices as a guard for every current truth $p$, establishing $\Box_{\ne}p$. Every actual tuple set $E$ has an inextensible representative $(E,\bot)$. Unrestricted current rows supply ordinary well-orders, choice graphs and representatives of coextension classes.

## Truncations with words

Take the [truncation-word model](truncation-words.html) as the old object. Its arrows are $(g_\alpha,w)$ and $(1,w)$, with $g_\alpha(i)=\min(i,\alpha)$ on a regular cardinal $\lambda$. A pin for an old proposition $p$ has size below $\lambda$ and is bounded by some $\gamma<\lambda$. If $p$ is true, it remains true at the empty-word addresses with coefficient $1$ or $g_\alpha$ for $\alpha\ge\gamma$. Let $q$ hold exactly on those addresses. Testing the coefficient on $\gamma$ pins $q$. If $q$ is possible after an arrow, that arrow already has empty word and coefficient at least $\gamma$, since compositions concatenate words and take the minimum truncation. Consequently $q$ witnesses $\Box_{\ne}p$. This proves that every truth is ND-necessary in the whole new model.

At each type the old order is atomic. In particular, for each $\alpha<\lambda$ a single tuple supported on $(g_\alpha,w)$ is an admitted atom. The product at $r$ is atomic as well. At the old point, any true proposition contains all sufficiently high truncation addresses at the empty word, so Actuality fails there.

An externally countable family has an old join by rowwise union on the union of its pins, and the same product formula gives the join at the root. Internally countable families are externally countable at both objects because unrestricted current predicates admit the inductive set of standard numeral denotations. This proves necessary Countable Boolean Completeness. The old counterfamily to Boolean Completeness lifts with empty current coordinates: its current family is admitted at the new root, and a least upper bound would project to an old least upper bound. For Modal Well-Ordering choose an external well-order in each root carrier and pair it with an old constant-row well-order of the corresponding old carrier. Each of those orders well-orders every current subset at its object. There is one individual everywhere. The three singleton-individual thresholds bound the inexact ordinal benchmark by the finite rigid-limit argument in the old proof. Unrestricted current subsets and graphs then make every infinite carrier contain a countably infinite power-closed set above the benchmark. Finite unions of the old truncation atoms give every finite Boolean interval, so Mathematical Possibilism holds at the new root.

### The powers at the new root

Suppose $F=(E,U)$ is weakly rigid. At the root, weak persistence makes $\Box F(a)$ true of every $a\in E$, and thus makes $\Box\Box F(a)$ true as well. Weak inextensibility tested against $x\mapsto\Box F(x)$ gives persistence for all later members. In particular $hE\subseteq U_{1_s}$ and $U$ is weakly persistent. If an old tester $W$ necessarily holds of every member of $U_{1_s}$, the root tester $(\top,W)$ necessarily holds of every member of $E$. Weak inextensibility of $F$ yields $U\le W$. Thus $U$ is weakly rigid. In the truncation construction a weakly rigid old relation is rigid with rows exactly the images of its current row, and there is a single truncation $g_\gamma$ fixing every member of that row.

An old tester can have row $hE$ at the empty-word addresses of coefficient at least $\gamma$, and full row everywhere else. It is pinned by $\gamma$. Lift it to the root with full current row. Every image of a member of $E$ passes: at the indicated addresses all tuples of $hE$ are fixed. Weak inextensibility now gives $U_{1_s}=hE$. Hence $F$ has exactly the images of $E$ at every arrow, which makes it hereditarily rigid.

For an old arrow $g$ and a weakly rigid subrelation $Z\le ghF$, let $E'=E\cap(gh)^{-1}[Z_{1_s}]$. Then $ghE'=Z_{1_s}$, and all tuples of $hE'$ share the same fixing truncation. The old relation $H$ with rows $k[hE']$ at all old arrows $k$ is admitted by this common bound. The root relation $(E',H)$ is rigid, lies below $F$, and projects to $Z$ at $gh$ by rigid coextension there. Thus the eligible power members are exactly the images of the current eligible members throughout the cone. This proves both necessary powers and necessary Tame Rigidity. By contrast, a rigid coextension of the universal current predicate on propositions would project onto the entire old proposition carrier, which has no common fixing truncation. Rigid Comprehension therefore fails at the root.

## The symmetric paired stages

Take stage zero of the [symmetric integer stages](symmetric-integer-paired-stages.html) as $s$. The entrance is onto at every sort. If all root inputs necessarily satisfy a tester $X$, every old input at $s$ necessarily satisfies $hX$ by lifting it to the root. The old Barcan Formula gives the universal conclusion at all old successors. It already holds at $r$, so the Barcan Formula holds at $r$ too, and holds necessarily.

At the paired stages every true proposition is 5-necessary. At $r$ this follows from the ND-necessity of every truth. Yet at stage zero the coordinate-one singleton test gives a 5-necessary proposition which is not two-hat necessary. The coordinate-zero test gives a two-hat-necessary proposition which is not ND-necessary. Both tests retain the original quantification over higher types because the old forward cone and all old domains are unchanged. The necessary forms of both implications consequently fail at $r$. Actuality also fails at the old stage by its atomlessness. Individual infinity holds at every stage and at $r$, where the current numeral cut is admitted and the individual carrier is infinite.
