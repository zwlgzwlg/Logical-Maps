# An independent relation preserving a pointed model's pure theory

OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 27 November 2026.

Let $M$ be a pointed C-model. Suppose that for one of $\sigma=e,t$ the standard Frege numerals $0_\sigma,S_\sigma0_\sigma,\ldots$ are pairwise distinct entities at the evaluation point. There is a pointed model with exactly the same complete pure theory and one nonlogical relational constant $c$ in which Independence holds and Witnessed Possibility fails. If $M$ satisfies pure B, the new model satisfies signature B. If $M$ satisfies No Pure Contingency, it satisfies No Contingency for the signature and Modal Freedom.

**The infinite family.** Write $\nu_\sigma=(\sigma t)t$, and introduce $c$ at type $\nu_\sigma t$. For each external natural $j$ let $H_j=\lambda y^{\nu_\sigma}.(y=\bar j_\sigma)$. These are closed pure terms. The $H_j$ are pairwise distinct at the designated point, by application to one of the two numerals. Each $H_j$ is nonempty at every future point because $\bar j_\sigma$ belongs to its own singleton.

The numerical hypothesis follows if for each standard $k$ there can be at least $k$ distinct $\sigma$-entities. In a point with sufficiently many entities, take a property with exactly $j$ instances by a finite disjunction of haecceities. It satisfies $\bar j_\sigma$ and fails every different standard numeral. A present identity between two of the closed numerals would persist to that point, which is impossible. The Infinity Schema at $\sigma$ supplies the required finite configurations at the source itself. Potential Infinity supplies them at accessible points. Possible Infinity also suffices since an actually infinite carrier in the quantified sense cannot be exhausted by any standard finite list.

**Compactness.** Take the full pure theory of the designated point, together with $c\ne A$ for every closed pure $A$ of the type of $c$, and $\Box\exists y\,cy$. Add the signature B schema when $M$ satisfies pure B, and the signature No Contingency schema when it satisfies No Pure Contingency. In a finite part of this theory only finitely many $A$ are forbidden. Each equals at most one $H_j$ at the source. Interpret $c$ by some $H_j$ outside this finite list. Substituting this single closed pure term throughout each signature sentence turns every requested B or No Contingency instance into an instance of the corresponding pure schema. The entire finite part holds.

Finitary Henkin compactness gives a model of the joint theory. Its only nonlogical symbol is $c$, so every instance of Independence is one of the inequalities included above. The pure formula $\neg\exists y\,Xy$ has a current witness, the bottom predicate, and its instance at $c$ is impossible. This refutes Witnessed Possibility. Equality of the complete pure theories preserves both positive and negative pure schematic verdicts, as failure of a schema has a particular false instance. Where the source construction uses a relative consistency hypothesis, the finite-substitution argument uses the same hypothesis. Compactness concerns the pointed Henkin theory in the external metatheory.

For Modal Freedom there is a further observation applying to any one-constant signature. In an instance with disjoint tuples of constants, at least one of the two formulas is closed and pure. If No Pure Contingency holds, its possibility implies its necessity. Conjoining it to the other formula therefore preserves that formula's possibility.

**Two finite models.** A different construction applies to the default models on a point and three individuals, both the [[symmetry-constrained-full-point-and-three-forward]] and the [[symmetry-constrained-full-point-and-three]]. Their root has one individual $a$, with entrances $h_0,h_1$ into the same old object carrying $a$ to different individuals. Take the rooted category whose objects are arrows $f$ from the source and whose arrows out of $f$ are the old continuations $k$, leading to $kf$. Use the old domains at the target of $f$, their original transport and the original truth evaluation. Each old continuation is available at its corresponding copy, so induction on pure terms, including quantification and necessity, gives the old denotations. This also preserves literal identity at each type.

In this rooted model interpret the sole predicate $c^{et}$ at every $f$ by the haecceity of $f(a)$. These interpretations are coherent under continuation. If $c$ equalled a closed pure predicate at the root, their transports along $h_0,h_1$ would agree. The closed pure term has the same old denotation at their common target, whereas the haecceities of $h_0(a)$ and $h_1(a)$ differ. Thus Independence holds. Once again $c$ is necessarily nonempty, and the empty-predicate instance refutes Witnessed Possibility.

## Two independent disjoint proposition names

Let a pointed pure action model have infinitely many propositions at its evaluation point. There is a model with the same complete pure theory and exactly two nonlogical constants $c,d$ of type $t$, satisfying Independence but refuting Modal Freedom, No Contingency for the signature, and Witnessed Possibility.

An infinite Boolean algebra has arbitrarily large finite families of pairwise disjoint nonzero elements. Otherwise take one of maximum size. Its join is top, and each member is an atom, since a nonzero remainder or a further split would enlarge the family. Every element would be the join of some subfamily and the algebra would be finite.

For a finite list of $m$ Independence instances choose such a family $a_1,\ldots,a_k$ with $k>m+1$. Consider the $k(k-1)$ assignments $c=a_i$, $d=a_j$ for $i\ne j$. Each forbidden equation is either that one of these names equals a closed pure term, or that it equals a closed pure function of the other name. For each fixed value of the other name there is at most one forbidden value; at most $k$ pairs are lost per equation. Some pair therefore avoids every equation. Name this pair in the category of arrows from the evaluation point as in the finite construction above, transporting both values from the source. Compactness applied to the complete pure theory, all Independence instances, $c\ne\bot$, $d\ne\bot$ and $c\land d=\bot$ gives the claimed model.

Both names are possible and their conjunction is impossible, contradicting Modal Freedom with the disjoint one-variable formulas $P(x)=x$ and $Q(y)=y$. Each name is contingent since the other is nonzero and disjoint from it. The pure conjunction $x\land y$ has the actual witness pair $(\top,\top)$, whereas its instance at $(c,d)$ is impossible. This is the failing Witnessed Possibility instance.
