# Second-Order Analytic Necessitism in the infinite-class permutation model

GPT-6 Astra, prompted by Daniel Kodsi. 30 October 2026.

The existing model [[symmetric-permutations-infinite-classes]] satisfies full
Second-Order Analytic Necessitism (SOAN), both with its base individuals and
with singleton individuals. The proof is in ambient ZFC. The base variant's
existing failure of current Transversal is retained; no such negative verdict
is asserted for singleton individuals. This argument concerns this particular
model and does not reinstate the withdrawn general analytical arguments.

## 1. The construction and its whole entities

Let B be countable, partitioned into infinitely many infinite equivalence
classes. The arrows are M = Sym(B); the designated symmetries are
G = Aut(B,E). Every relational domain contains exactly the external
G-symmetric profiles with a finite pin in B. Transport is by right
precomposition. We use the construction and identity convention in the
existing record. Every arrow has an inverse and acts bijectively at every
type. Each entity has a finite pin; base individuals have singleton pins.

Write M_P and G_P for pointwise stabilizers of a finite P. If P pins x,
every m in M_P fixes x as a whole entity: for every later arrow k, km and k
agree on P. Equality here is equality of all profile rows, not merely current
coextension.

For singleton individuals, the auxiliary B, M, G and pin rule remain the same,
but the entire typed hierarchy is rebuilt over one fixed individual. Its
individual has an empty pin. The argument below applies within that hierarchy.

## 2. Any current real field is externally the ordinary real field

Fix a current native Real_sigma tuple R, with carrier X, order, addition,
multiplication, zero, one and least natural subset N. Take a finite common
pin P for these parameters. We first justify standardness using the exact
profile admission rule; Henkin completeness alone would not suffice.

At each arrow h and each external standard n, collect **all** endpoints of
length-n chains in hR starting at its zero and repeatedly adding its one.
Every chain variable is restricted to its carrier. Take the union over
standard finite n. This is a P-pinned profile: agreement on P gives the same
transported parameter data. It is symmetric: every designated symmetry and
its inverse transport precisely these solutions. Thus it is admitted.
Even if transported data fail field laws, all solutions are collected, so no
future existence or uniqueness is assumed.

Currently the profile contains exactly the standard numerals. Native
leastness of N, and external induction in the other direction, identify it
with N. If it had an upper bound, native completeness would give a supremum u.
A numeral greater than u-1 would have successor greater than u. Therefore
the field is Archimedean and embeds into the ordinary ordered real field.

For each rational q=a/b, b>0, finite relational equations in the same
transported tuple define all its solutions. For a>=0 use numeral witnesses
u=a, v=b and v*x=u; for a<0 use u=|a|, v=b, v*x=w and w+u=0.
These solution profiles have the same pin and symmetry as above, without
assuming unique future solutions. Their union over any external proper
nonempty rational lower cut C with no greatest member is therefore admitted.
Currently it is precisely the rational values belonging to C. It is bounded
by any rational outside C, so it has a supremum r_C in X. If q belongs to C,
choose a larger q' in C to obtain q<r_C; if q is outside C, q bounds C and
r_C<=q. Thus r_C has exactly cut C. Every real cut is realized, proving
that the current field is externally the ordinary complete ordered real
field, with its standard naturals.

## 3. A single pin works for every carrier member

Each g in G_P fixes R as a whole tuple and acts on its current field as an
ordered-field automorphism. Such an automorphism fixes rationals and all
elements determined by rational cuts. Hence it fixes each x in X literally.

For any finite P,T, the following group calculation holds:

    M_P is contained in the subgroup generated by G_P and M_T.

For distinct u,v outside P, choose distinct fresh u',v' outside
P union T union {u,v}, in the respective classes of u,v. The product
s=(u u')(v v') belongs to G_P, and (u' v') belongs to M_T.
Conjugating the latter by s gives (u v). Thus every finitary permutation
fixing P belongs to the generated group. An arbitrary pi in M_P agrees on
finite T with a finitary permutation rho fixing P; then rho^-1 pi fixes T.
This proves the claim for arbitrary permutations. It is the same generation
calculation already used in the model's countable-completeness argument.

For each x in X, choose its finite pin T_x. Its whole-entity stabilizer
contains G_P by field rigidity and M_(T_x) by pinning, hence contains M_P.
The resulting P is uniform for all x; no union of the varying T_x is taken.
If h,h' agree on P, h^-1 h' fixes P, and therefore h*x=h'*x for every x in X.

## 4. Rigid fields and represented powers at the same carrier type

For every external relation D on a finite power of X define

    H_D(h) = h[D].

Uniform pinning makes H_D P-pinned; H_D(gh)=g[H_D(h)] makes it symmetric.
Thus H_D is admitted. Apply this construction to the carrier, operation and
order graphs, and standard natural subset, keeping zero and one as entities.
It gives a new field tuple at the original type sigma.

At h its predicates have extension h[D], and at every further k extension
k[h[D]]. Hence they are persistent there. If a target test necessarily
holds of all current h[D]-members, every future member k(d), d in h[D],
satisfies that test. This proves inextensibility against every admitted
target test. Since h was arbitrary the predicates are rigid with the
required leading box. Every h is bijective on each type, so the structure
at h is an external copy of the real field, necessarily with distinct
carrier members. External completeness and induction verify the native
clauses against all admitted properties. The original R need not be rigid.

For each positive arity k define the higher profile

    P_k(h) = { h*H_D : D is an external subset of X^k }.

These are whole relation entities. If h,h' agree on P, then for every later l,
lh[D]=lh'[D]; hence h*H_D=h'*H_D literally. Consequently P_k has pin P.
Functoriality proves its symmetry. Its rows also transport by exact image,
so the preceding persistence and inextensibility argument proves its rigidity.
Its members are rigid and supported on the field's k-tuples.

At any h, let Z be any target k-ary relation. Pull its current extension on
h[X]^k back through the bijection h|X to D subset X^k. Then h*H_D belongs to
P_k(h) and agrees with Z on the carrier. This proves the boxed representation
clause for the full target domain, not just transported source relations.
Any prescribed finite list of arities uses the same field and common pin.

Finally, in this one-object group model, the closed pure assertion that a
Real_sigma tuple exists is invariant under the bijective typed transports.
A possible real field therefore gives a current one at that same type.
The preceding argument applies separately at each sigma. If none is possible
the implication is vacuous. In particular the base e-domain is countable,
and the singleton e-domain has one element; the standardness argument excludes
a real field on either. This establishes the full SOAN schema in both variants.
