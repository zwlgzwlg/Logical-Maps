# Maximalism with revised Mathematical Possibilism

For each set-sized free relational signature $\Sigma$ there is a pointed C action model satisfying both signature Maximalism and revised Mathematical Possibilism at every admitted carrier type. The same construction transfers pure Maximalism. The version with the signature-definable old component also satisfies signature Possibility+. The construction and proof are due to OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 26 November 2026. Work in ZFC.

## The old model and the terminals

Start with a pointed, set-sized signature-Maximalist model $K,w$. One choice is the [signature-definable coalesced root](coalesced-definable-root-signature.html). Take $I=D_e(w)$ as the new root's individuals. There is an entrance to $w$, identity on individuals, and its composites with every old arrow out of $w$. More generally one may take finitely many independent old entrances with compatible interpretations of the individual names.

Fix a strong limit cardinal $\mu$ larger than the cardinal bound on the ordinal codes calculated below. Take a set of representatives of all nontrivial complete Boolean algebras of size $<\mu$, their Boolean product $L$, and its Stone space $X$. For each ultrafilter $x\in X$ add an entrance to a full one-world classical model with one individual. It has the same finite discrete domain $F_\tau$ at every fixed type $\tau$ as every other such terminal. The external components have no arrows back to the root. The root initially has only its identity loop.

At a root relational type $\tau=\sigma_1\cdots\sigma_n t$, choose independently

1. any current subset of the product of the root argument domains,
2. any whole entity of $D_\tau(w)$ as the old value, and
3. any locally constant function $X\to F_\tau$ as the family of terminal values.

For a second old component use a second independent coordinate. These definitions recurse on the argument types. The prescribed value at each entrance gives all rows at its composites by old transport. Root individuals go to the sole terminal individual. Give each relational signature constant its old value, an arbitrary current row and a homogeneous terminal interpretation. The signatures at the old entrances must agree with this assignment.

These domains are closed under terms with parameters. At an old entrance the term is evaluated on the transported assignment in the old model. At a terminal the resulting whole denotation is a function of the finite tuple of parameter values in the same fixed finite structure. Their tuple map is locally constant. The calculation includes abstraction and quantification over the target domains, including arguments outside the image of an entrance. Every resulting current extension is admitted. Thus the ordinary action-model soundness theorem applies with the original theoremhood clause for Logical Equivalence. All old possibilities remain accessible by composition, proving the Maximalism transfer. For Possibility+ the entrance into the signature-definable point preserves the eligible individual tuple and its inequalities with named individuals, so its old prescribed witness still works.

## Bounding the inexact ordinals

Let $\rho=(e\to e\to t)\to(e\to t)\to t$, and $H$ be the set of actual $\rho$-entities satisfying the impredicative inexact ordinal predicate. Its constructors test well-orderhood, passage to a strict initial segment, and limits of rigid families. Let $Z$ consist of exactly those $\rho$-entities whose current application depends only on the extensions of their order and field on $I$, and whose terminal section is constant. The root admits $Z$ as the current row of a test predicate. [Ordinal definitions](https://github.com/andrew-bacon/Logical-Maps/blob/82133f4969047baa6ce02700483c9741c5857699/logical-maps/topics/classicism/verification/finite-power-ch/Classicism/Results/MathematicalContingency/InductiveOrdinals.lean)

Zero is in $Z$. Current well-orderhood depends only on the order and field, because all subcollections are available as current extensions. At each terminal the calculation is identical. If $z\in Z$, the current order and field below a candidate cut point are determined extensionally by the original order, field and point. Thus the successor of $z$ is in $Z$ as well.

Suppose $Y$ is rigid and its current members all lie in $Z$. Let $S\subseteq F_\rho$ collect the common terminal values of its current members. Define a tester with current and old rows everywhere top and terminal predicate constantly $S$. Every current member of $Y$ satisfies this tester necessarily. Inextensibility therefore forces every terminal $Y$-member into $S$, even members with no known root preimage. Conversely persistence puts each value in $S$ in $Y$ at every terminal. The terminal extension of $Y$ is consequently the same set $S$ everywhere. The limit tests well-orderhood and the applications of the current $Y$-members. Those applications are extensional in the order and field, and the identical terminal calculation now gives a constant value. Thus the limit lies in $Z$. Instantiating the ordinal definition with this test gives $H\subseteq Z$.

There are at most $B=2^{|\mathcal P(I^2)\times\mathcal P(I)|}$ extension-invariant current rows, one whole old coordinate from $D_\rho(w)$, and one value in the finite $F_\rho$. This bounds the number of whole root ordinal entities independently of $X$. The old coordinates for several components contribute their finite product. Choose $\mu$ greater than this bound and than $\aleph_0$ by taking a supremum of a strictly increasing sequence of beth iterates.

## Smallness and representing algebras

If a current carrier has size at least $\mu$, take a subset $Y$ of size $\mu>|H|$. For every strictly smaller $A\subseteq Y$, its powerset has size $2^{|A|}<\mu$. Choose a subset of $Y$ of that size, biject it with the subsets of $A$, and take the membership incidence as the power-coding graph. Current fullness admits the subset, the code set and the graph. Every subextension has exactly one code, including when several properties share that subextension. This makes $Y$ power-closed in the sense of the revised smallness test. Consequently every revised-small CBA has size below $\mu$. Current fullness also means that a CBA in the root satisfies the ordinary complete Boolean algebra laws for all its subsets. [Revised definition](https://github.com/zwlgzwlg/Logical-Maps/blob/4bb9287187ed7cb09567eb3a6302cb896c986ec4/logical-maps/topics/classicism/principles/forcing-possibilism.yaml)

The root proposition algebra is $2\times D_t(w)\times\operatorname{Clop}(X)$ with componentwise entailment, since the box checks the root, all old composites and every terminal. Stone representation identifies the clopen order with $L$. In the product, the element supported at the unit of one algebra factor has precisely that algebra as its principal lower interval. Use root and old coordinates bottom to retain that interval in the root algebra. The trivial algebra is the interval below bottom. In each case the mixed graph of the order isomorphism is an admitted current relation. This gives the required interval for every revised-small CBA in one model.

Lean proves the joint verdict for a concrete identity-loop catalogue. For each countable signature whose constants have closed types, `maximalist_mathematical_possibilism` constructs one model satisfying signature and pure Maximalism in their official distinctness forms and revised Mathematical Possibilism at every type. Its catalogue contains every Boolean algebra on every subset of a fixed bounding type. The proof bounds the actual native inexact ordinals and derives a strong-limit cut above them. Full current unary and binary relations let the native represented-power test exclude every carrier of at least that size. The mixed graph then realizes the complete principal entailment interval. The corollary `joint_complete_theory` gives a consistent complete theory entailing all three schemata in a signature with one proposition constant. Its supporting proposition has no atom below it and yet is a self-atom: at each terminal where it is true it equals the top of the two-valued algebra. For each of the listed discrete-loop examples an old point fails propositional Boolean Completeness (the signature-definable root or the finite-support permutation point). Its offending family can be placed in the corresponding old principal interval and is admitted as a current predicate. A root supremum would lie in that interval and supply an old supremum. Finally, unrestricted current graphs admit well-orders and the graphs of ordinary transversals and relational choices.

## Propositional infinity at the root and finite ordinary terminals

The root proposition carrier contains the factor $\mathcal P(\omega)$, and the ordinary standard Frege numerals are exactly its current numbers. Indeed their external set is the extension of an admitted current inductive property, and every inductive property contains each standard iterate. Each numeral counts just the extensions of its finite size, so none counts the universal root proposition property. This proves the unboxed Axiom of Infinity at $t$ for each choice of root loops and old components. In the Cantor-loop cases an arbitrary current row can be assigned constantly on the loop group.

An ordinary one-world terminal has one individual and two propositions. Its second numeral at $e$ and third at $t$ belong to the corresponding Frege number properties by successor closure. They have no instances, even possibly, since the terminal sees only itself. Thus both necessary Potential Infinity principles fail. The pure sentence $\exists p\,(p\land\Diamond\neg p)$ holds at the root, witnessed by the proposition true on the root loops and false at the exits. At an ordinary terminal it is impossible, so B for pure sentences fails as well. [Frege arithmetic](../index.html#background/arithmetic)

## Varying the loops and old components

For a root group or monoid $M$ let every external exit absorb precomposition by a loop. The designated root symmetry subgroup is trivial. A root relation now has a table of ordinary rows indexed by $M$, transported by right translation. On the discrete examples every such table is admitted. To bound ordinals require extension invariance at every loop and constant terminal value. This condition is stable under translation. For rigid $Y$ with current member set $U$, its row at $m$ is exactly $mU$. Persistence gives one inclusion. For the reverse, use the tester with row $mU$ at $m$ and top everywhere else. Inextensibility forces that row inclusion. For a group one can instead apply persistence along the inverse. The terminal tester above has top at all loops. The old induction now applies at each loop and the bound on its row factor becomes $B^{|M|}$, still fixed before $\mu$.

For a single loop, the proposition supported at the identity is a true strong atom and witnesses the ND-necessity of every current truth. For the two-element group the identity singleton remains a strong atom, and its nontrivial translate refutes Truths are ND-necessary. To see the latter, take a truth false at the other loop; any true guard can return to truth from that loop along its inverse.

The paired old components give a Barcan counterexample. At the old pure definable point a property $F$ has a possible instance but no current proposition possibly satisfies it. Give a new property old value $F$ on that branch and empty rows elsewhere. The old existential witness remains accessible. Every new root argument has an old value and hence cannot possibly witness the property on that branch. For the pure expansion with one name reinterpreted as top, the pure Possibility+ instances survive verbatim. The closed identity of that free name with top holds there although it is not C-provable in the free signature.
