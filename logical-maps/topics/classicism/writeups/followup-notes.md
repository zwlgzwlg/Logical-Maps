# Functional Choice and necessary arithmetic infinity

The [cofinite-germ model](functional-choice-countermodel.md) satisfies unboxed Functional Choice at all admitted types, but fails Boolean Completeness at proposition type t. It therefore also fails full Boolean Completeness and Rigid Comprehension.

The [numerical-growth construction](protected-numbers-and-numerical-growth.md), Sections 2–4, satisfies $\Box I^*_\rho$ at every finite counting type while Arithmetic Necessitism fails at t with its guard true. Its new model record connects this existing proof to the necessary-arithmetic-infinity node.

The [No Collapse results](no-collapse-constructions.md) are retained as mathematical notes, without graph entries.
