# Maximalism and revised Mathematical Possibilism without Actuality

OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 26 November 2026.

## The root

Take the [signature-definable coalesced point](coalesced-definable-root-signature.html) in a free signature with infinitely many constants of every admitted type. At the new root take its same individuals and an identity entrance map on individuals. As in the original construction, each relational domain has an independent whole old coordinate and a continuous section on the Stone space of the product of small complete Boolean algebras. The leaves are copies of the same finite singleton-individual terminal structure. We replace just the root loop rows.

Use the group G = (ℤ/2)ᴺ under coordinatewise addition. Every arrow out of the root to an external object absorbs right composition by any root loop. Use the trivial designated root symmetry subgroup. Recursively, for each flattened relational type with root argument tuple domain T, allow as loop table any function g ↦ Eᵍ in the powerset of T for which some finite prefix of g determines the entire subset Eᵍ. Root individuals have trivial loop action. A loop translates a table on the right and fixes the old and leaf coordinates. Interpret each relational signature constant with a constant loop table and its prescribed old interpretation. Every possible current extension is available by a constant table.

Each root parameter as a whole is fixed by translations with zero entries in some finite prefix. For a finite assignment take a common prefix. At a loop the row of a lambda term is obtained by evaluating its body at the same root object on the transported assignment, with bound arguments ranging over that object's domains. Equal transported assignments therefore give the same entire subset of T. In particular the prefix can be chosen before the bound arguments are supplied. For partial application the row is the slice of the function's table at the transported argument. At an old exit evaluate the term on the old parameters. On a terminal leaf it is a function of the transported finite tuple in the homogeneous finite model and so is continuous. These checks inductively give typed term closure, landing and the required reindexing of open terms.

## Revised smallness

At the ordinal type use an admitted current test Z consisting of the entities whose application depends only on the current field and order extensions at each loop row and whose leaf section is constant. Translation preserves Z. Zero is in Z and taking successors preserves Z by the current segment construction. If a rigid family has identity-row members U ⊆ Z, its members at g are precisely gU, by persistence in both directions around the invertible loop. For the limit on the leaves, let S be the set of the constant leaf values of the members of U. A tester with all loop and old rows top and leaf predicate constantly S necessarily holds on every member of U. Inextensibility bounds every leaf extension by S and persistence gives the reverse inclusion. Hence the ordinal limit has a constant leaf value. At every loop its tests use only members of Z, so its application is current-extension invariant there as well.

The impredicative ordinal carrier is consequently contained in Z. If B bounds the number of extension-invariant single rows, there are at most ∑ₙ B^(2ⁿ) possible loop tables for Z. The independent old ordinal-type domain and the finite constant leaf value supply the other two factors. All three bounds are fixed before choosing the strong-limit cut μ. The original power-closed smallness argument applies because current graphs are unrestricted. Each complete Boolean algebra of size below μ occurs as its own principal interval in the independent Stone factor. This proves revised Mathematical Possibilism at every admitted carrier type.


## Truth, vicinity and Boolean completeness

Every true proposition contains a finite-prefix cylinder about the identity. Split that cylinder by a fresh bit and take the proposition supported only on the half containing the identity. This gives a true proposition missed by the proposed strongest truth on the other half. Hence Actuality fails. The whole loop region, empty on all exits, witnesses Vicinity: every true proposition can return to truth from any loop along its inverse. A truth missing some loop also fails ND-necessity.

The principal interval below the loop region is the countable atomless algebra of clopen sets of the Cantor group. Consider the cylinders whose first 1 occurs at an even position. Every clopen upper bound contains the zero sequence and hence a neighbourhood of it. Delete an odd-position cylinder from that neighbourhood to get a smaller upper bound. Thus the family has no supremum. Current fullness admits the family and the graph pairing its nth cylinder with the nth Frege numeral at type t. These numerals are distinct: the root has admitted properties with exactly n members for every standard n. The family is therefore countable in the native sense with counting sort t.

## Current Barcan and signature witnesses

The old point with infinitely many constants at each type satisfies current Barcan. For an old descriptor containing only finitely many symbols, validity on all closed arguments includes a fresh constant of the argument type; generalizing that constant gives validity on all arguments at the components. Signature Possibility+ holds at that old point by its defining construction. Take the identity individual entrance, preserving all names and eligible tuples. Its possible witnesses survive by composition. If a property satisfies ∀x □Xx at the new root, surjectivity of the first old entrance transfers that premise to the old point. Old current Barcan now supplies its boxed universal on the full old cone. The terminal entrances are onto and the new loops are invertible, covering the remaining arrows.

The paired old-component variant instead fails propositional Barcan by the lifted counterexample, and the permutation variant has necessary Barcan and both necessary rigid-power principles.
